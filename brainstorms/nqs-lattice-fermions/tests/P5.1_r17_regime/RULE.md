# RULE: R51.32 `regmatch`, the 10 → 12 size ratio with the step regime matched (round 17, written before any arm)
Written 2026-11-18 by the round-17 worker. Pre-audit draft; nothing submitted. Not opened: `../P5.1_size_scaling_r04/results/r14_decay/` or any output of jobs 773500/773528.

**Question.** R51.8 rev. 2 measured $N_{\rm needed}$ = 31,284 (5×2) → 118,626 (6×2), $r'$ = 3.79 ≥ 3.52 (SUPPORT-LIKE), with one fixed metric length τ = 0.005 at both sizes. Round 17 measured that this τ is 3–14× longer relative to the remaining gain at 10 sites (first-order gain per step $g_1=2\tau\sqrt{x^{\mathsf T}Sx}/G_{\rm ref}$: 5×2 window mean 0.301 over steps 61–120; 6×2 two states 0.0220 and 0.1157), so $r'$ mixes size with step regime (L78). Does the ratio stay at or above 3.52 when the 10-site step is shortened until its $g_1$ matches the 12-site one?

**Matched quantity (T0).** Three candidates:
* (a) $g_1$ equal across sizes (chosen). $N_{\rm needed}$ compares the sampled arm with its own noise-free arm at the same τ; how much a given direction error costs, relative to $G_{\rm ref}$, depends on how large one step's progress is relative to the remaining gain, which is $g_1$. With $g_1$ equal, what is left in the ratio is the force signal-to-noise at each size, which is the size effect the Answer asks about.
* (b) Each size's τ tuned to its best exact-arm progress. Not well defined over a 120-step window: at 5×2 the τ = 0.005 exact arm bounces from step 8 and reaches its window level by about step 25, so any τ that does not diverge reaches about the same $G$ by step 61, and the tuned τ is set by where the bounce starts, a different regime at each size. It does show which way a practitioner would move at 5×2: the pre-flight τ = 0.0025 arm (disclosure below) was ahead of τ = 0.005 at step 5 (0.65 against 0.57 of τ = 0.005's $G_{\rm ref}$), so a tuned 10-site step is shorter, the direction this rule takes.
* (c) Each size's τ tuned to the fewest total samples to the target (the practitioner's choice). This is the statistic the Question's "fewer total samples … for the same accuracy" asks for, but in the optimiser comparison (guided vs Born SR vs MinSR at one size), where both arms share the τ grid; R51.16 and R51.31 do that. For the size trend, a per-size tuned τ puts each size in its own regime again (the confound under study), and it needs a τ × N grid with exact references at both sizes (6×2 exact 2.9 CPU-min per step in job: about 5.8 CPU-h per τ before any ladder).
So (a) is the quantity that removes the stated confound; (c) belongs to the comparator and is not bought.

**Zero-cost check.** No stored pair is regime-matched. Every 5×2 and 6×2 guided, Born and exact arm (R51.8, `../P5.1_size_scaling_r04/results/`) and every |ψ| arm (R51.11, `../P5.1_r05_gen/results/q1L_*`) uses τ = 0.005. The other-τ arms are R51.31's Born MinSR runs at 5×2, $N$ = 1024 (τ 0.0003125–0.0025; no exact arm at those τ, `../P5.1_r16_gen/`) and R51.1's arm D at 4×3 (τ 0.0025, another geometry, no exact arm at that τ). `bs.py grep tau` and the P4.1 r25/r42 test directories hold no 5×2 or 6×2 arm. Job 773528's DEC arms shorten τ from step 121 at both sizes, but as a decaying, averaged continuation with its own statistic (k 31–60), not a fixed-τ 120-step need; its outputs bear on the prediction only (`voi.md`).

**Direction.** Shorten τ at 5×2 to reach the 6×2 regime, not lengthen it at 6×2: 5×2 costs about 1/25 per step, and 6×2 already bounces at τ = 0.005, so a longer 6×2 step risks divergence. The 6×2 side is R51.8's stored arms and $N_{\rm needed}(6\times2)$ = 118,626 (one seed; `score_r08.json`).

**Arms (5×2 only; R51.8's start, `ns_run.py` unchanged, exact i.i.d. guided draws, 120 steps).**
* Stage 1: exact arms at τ ∈ {0.00375, 0.0025, 0.00125}, run through `xsx_run.py` (ns_run.py plus a per-step log of $\sqrt{x^{\mathsf T}Sx}$; round 17's wrapper reproduced the stored energies with max difference 0).
* Selection (`score_r17_regime.py select`): $g_1(\tau)$ = mean over steps 61–120 of $2\tau\sqrt{x^{\mathsf T}Sx}/G_{\rm ref}(\tau)$, with $G_{\rm ref}(\tau)$ from that τ's own exact arm. Target T = 0.0689 (mean of the two 6×2 states), band [0.0220, 0.1157] (their range). τ* = the grid value minimising $|\ln(g_1/T)|$. MATCHED iff $g_1(\tau^*)$ lies in the band; otherwise the job stops: verdict NOT-MATCHED, no class, nothing changes.
* Stage 2: guided rungs 4,096, 16,384, 65,536, 262,144 at τ*, seed0 9000.
* Stage 3 (seed clause, L72): seed0 19000 at the rungs that define $N_{\rm needed}(5\times2,\tau^*)$ under seed 9000 (the bracketing pair, or the one bounding rung if censored); always run, since a 5×2 rung costs 4–11 CPU-min.

**Statistic: R51.8 rev. 2 plus addenda, identical except τ.** $E_{\rm win}$ = mean exact energy over steps 61–120; $G_{\rm ref}(\tau^*)=E_{\rm start}-E_{\rm win}({\rm exact},\tau^*)$; $s=(E_{\rm win}({\rm arm})-E_{\rm win}({\rm exact}))/G_{\rm ref}$, seed mean where two seeds exist; $N_{\rm needed}$ at $s$ = 0.10 by $\ln s$–$\ln N$ interpolation between the first rung with $s\le0.10$ and the rung below. Censoring as `score_r16_qladder.py`: $(0,4096]$ if the lowest rung has $s\le0.10$; $(262144,\infty)$ if none does; $(N_{\rm lo},N_{\rm up}]$ if the upper bracketing rung has $s\le0$. Conditioning: gap $s({\rm lower})-s({\rm upper})>2\,sd_w$, $sd_w$ = max(detrended sd of the upper rung's three 20-step window means, seed-averaged; |seed difference|/√2), else UNRESOLVABLE. Reference energies (L75): $E_{\rm start}(5\times2)$ = −6.547115994; $G_{\rm ref}(\tau^*)$ is computed from the new exact arm and reported as a number; $N_{\rm needed}(6\times2)$ = 118,626.2, $G_{\rm ref}(6\times2)$ = 0.0070107.

**Verdict.** $r'_m=118{,}626/N_{\rm needed}(5\times2,\tau^*)$ as an interval. POLY-CONSISTENT if $r'_{m,\rm hi}\le1.728$ ($N_{\rm needed}(5\times2)\ge68{,}650$); SUPPORT-LIKE if $r'_{m,\rm lo}\ge3.52$ ($N_{\rm needed}(5\times2)\le33{,}700$); AMBIGUOUS if strictly between; a line inside the interval → UNRESOLVABLE (CENSORED-UNDECIDED). Precedence NOT-MATCHED > INCOMPLETE > UNRESOLVABLE > class. Near-line clause (L72): PROVISIONAL if $|\ln r'_m-\ln{\rm line}|\le0.1$ for either line; then the class is reported but the Answer is not changed, and the owed 6×2 second seed (R51.8, about 2.9 CPU-h, gated separately) is what can settle it. Reported only: $N_{\rm needed}(5\times2,\tau^*)/31{,}284$ (the τ effect at fixed size); $g_1$, $m$ (mean $|\Delta E|/G$ over 61–120; 6×2 0.0485, 5×2 at 0.005 0.153) and the exact-arm slope flag at each grid τ.

**What each verdict does.**
* SUPPORT-LIKE (not provisional): the confound is removed in the direction that could have produced the ratio. The Answer's growth sentence keeps "3.8 times" and adds "and X times with the 10-site step shortened to the 12-site regime"; the "size and step length are not separated" clause is dropped. R51.8's 10 → 12 result stands as SUPPORT-LIKE.
* AMBIGUOUS or POLY-CONSISTENT: the 3.8× growth was at least partly step regime. The Answer drops "as fast as the number of configurations the state occupies" and reports both ratios; R51.8's SUPPORT-LIKE is demoted to "at one fixed step length". POLY-CONSISTENT reopens the size question toward the yes side (the third size becomes the next spend if the budget allows).
* NOT-MATCHED, INCOMPLETE, UNRESOLVABLE, or PROVISIONAL: numbers only; the Answer's confound sentence stays.

**Scope and confounds named now.** Matching is one-sided (shorter 5×2 step against the stored 6×2 step) and the 6×2 target rests on two states, not a window (L78 asks for a window; the 6×2 window costs a 5.8 CPU-h exact rerun and is not bought; the band absorbs a ×5 phase alternation). At shorter τ the 5×2 exact arm may still descend over 61–120 (slope flag reported). One seed at 6×2. Over a size factor 1.2, no verdict separates a polynomial from an exponential (R51.8's Gate 4 note).
