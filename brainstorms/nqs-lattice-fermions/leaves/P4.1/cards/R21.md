# R21 — sampled SR with a truncated metric and many small steps (`trSR`) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: The 24-site rung needs a scalable optimiser. The sampled linear method fails because it solves an eigenproblem in one large step, which amplifies metric noise (at 65,536 samples the best candidate realised $R=0.080$ against 0.269 exact). The leaf's SR used an absolute diagonal shift of $10^{-3}$, which Round 11 left untested as the cause of the stall, while exact natural-gradient steps with a truncated metric (cutoff $10^{-4}$) realised 0.227 of the gap in one step. Sampled SR with the same relative truncation and a small fixed step averages noise over many steps instead of fitting it in one, and separates "SR is the problem" from "the Tikhonov shift is the problem", the confound Round 11 named.
* **Lineage**: Operation A on R15d, R18 and the r20 sampled-LM probes, Round 20 (the "SR or minSR variant" branch of r20's decision tree). Gates G0–G4 passed at T0; predicted NOT-VIABLE p 0.5, MARGINAL 0.3, VIABLE 0.2. T1 rule: VIABLE if $R_{\mathrm{trSR}}(15)\ge0.20$, at least 0.15 above the shift control and no exact rise above 0.02 of the gap; NOT-VIABLE if below 0.08; MARGINAL otherwise. Full gate text in log `round-25-critic.md`. Its protocol was reused by R38.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at G2 (dominated by R38, 2026-11-17).
* **Kill rule**: Gate 2: "does a cheap baseline or competing route reach the same metric?", applied by the per-leaf critic (round 23, objection 8) after T1. The T1 rule (`tests/P4.1_cheap_su2_r24/RULE.md`) gave MARGINAL; dominance replaces a continuation.
* **Evidence**: T1, job 748762 (19.0 CPU-min, 3 arms, 15 steps, 0.41 CPU-min per step, 16,384 exact samples, from `che`'s SR-trained start, $4\times3$ exact energies): best arm, shift-$10^{-3}$ SR with a held-out step-length ladder, R(15) = 0.195 of the gap (max exact rise 0.006); truncated SR 0.166 (rise 0.022). R38 (guided sampling) runs the same protocol at the same cost per step and reached R(15) = 0.364, then plateaued at R(60) 0.512 (median of 3) against 0.7475 for the exact polish (job 752885).
* **Why this kills it**: at equal cost per step R38 removes 0.364 of the gap in 15 steps against R21's 0.195, so any R21 continuation is beaten by R38 at equal compute; R38 itself plateaus below the 0.60 bar, so R21 cannot open a 24-site rung either.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: truncated-metric SR at much larger sample counts or at polished starts; R21's held-out step-length harness, which R38 reuses.
* **Strongest case against the kill**: one seed for R21, and R38's 15-step value is also one seed; answered: the gap (0.364 vs 0.195) is far larger than the step-to-step scatter (about 0.01–0.02), and R38's three 60-step replicas agree within 0.03.
* **Checked by**: per-leaf critic, 2026-11-17: the earlier "superseded" shelving was incomplete (objection 8); reclassified KILLED by dominance (orchestrator).
* **Lesson**: L59 (force noise limits sampled SR; truncating the metric does not help).
