# R17 — extensive local charge Jastrow on $2\times2$ plaquettes (`nj`) (SHELVED)
* **The idea**: Multiply the wave function by $e^{\sum_Pv_PN_P(c)}$, where $N_P(c)$ counts the $2\times2$ plaquettes showing each of 81 charge patterns: a size-independent, per-plaquette (hence extensive) charge correlator that commutes with spin rotations, so exact spin projection stays free and the cost is $\mathcal O(N)$.
* **Lineage**: the charge-only form of orchestrator suggestion (a), Round 10 (the R10 variant that was never run). T1 tangent test AMBIGUOUS in Round 10 and again at the ladder-polished parents in Round 23.
* **Outcome**: SHELVED (G2, orchestrator, 2026-11-17). Three AMBIGUOUS tangent reads: first-order gain as a fraction of the gap, route vs 81 random global tanh charge features: Round 10 `che` 0.005 vs 0.008, `pcg` 0.012 vs 0.007; Round 23 ladder-polished `che` 0.0073 vs 0.0041, `pcg` 0.0132 vs 0.0086 (1.5–1.8 times the foil, below the PASS bars of 2× the foil and 0.05 of the gap). Exact sums, open $4\times3$, $5+5$, $U=8$, $t'=-0.25$. Logs `round-23-R16R17.md`, `round-23.md` section 6.
* **Why shelved**: the next tier, training the 81 plaquette weights inside both ladder-polished arms, was GATE-REFUSED(G2) by the orchestrator. Cost (post-hoc estimate; none was stated at the refusal): about 10 ladder steps per arm, as job 742206 took 8–12, at 7–8 CPU-min each, so about 2.5–3 CPU-h. Predicted trained gain: below 0.05 of the gap, p about 0.9 (orchestrator), since reaching the bar would need training to multiply a first-order gain of at most 0.013 of the gap about fourfold; a gain that small could not change the leaf's answer.
* **What would unshelve it**: a tangent read at 24 sites (where per-plaquette terms multiply) showing route $\ge2\times$ foil, or a converged 12-site parent at which the gain exceeds 0.05 of the gap; either needs a 24-site exact-sum-free tangent estimator or a converging optimiser, neither available.
