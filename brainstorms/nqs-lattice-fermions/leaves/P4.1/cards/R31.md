# R31 — Krylov-subspace or larger-block linear method (`krylov-lm`) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Replace the ladder linear method's dense truncated eigen-solve by an iterative Krylov (Davidson or Lanczos) solve of the full, untruncated linear-method eigenproblem, or by larger blocks, so that each step uses more of the tangent space and the 12-site arms converge in fewer steps than the 9–12 ladder steps that left 22–100% of their error as fitted tail (job 742206, RULE_amend2).
* **Lineage**: sub-question A of Round 22 (coordinator addition: an exact-sum optimiser that converges both arms), from the coordinator's list; Krylov linear methods are known in QMC (Neuscamman, Umrigar & Chan, Phys. Rev. B 85, 045103, 2012).
* **Outcome**: KILLED at G2 (T0, 2026-11-17).
* **Kill rule**: Gate 2: does the change act on what limits the result? Here the limiter is gain per CPU-minute.
* **Evidence**: (i) the ladder step already uses the full 2637- or 2696-dimensional tangent space with a per-step cutoff, so there is no larger block; (ii) a step's cost is the exact build of $Y$ and $HY$ over 157,216 representatives (2.86 CPU-min for `che`, about 7.6 for `pcg`), while a dense eigen-solve of a $2637\times2637$ pencil takes seconds; (iii) the slow convergence is per-step gain ratio 0.76–0.98 at accepted steps, i.e. the curvature model of one tangent space, which a different solver of the same model does not change.
* **Why this kills it**: a Krylov solver changes only the cheap part of the step and solves the same truncated quadratic model, so neither cost per step nor gain per step can move.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: Krylov methods that avoid the build (matrix-free products with exact gradients), which is R29's form.
* **Strongest case against the kill**: an untruncated Krylov solve could use the small-eigenvalue directions the cutoff drops; answered by L29 and R18'/R18: those directions are where the map back to parameters blows up, so they need truncation or damping whatever the solver.
* **Checked by**: per-leaf critic, 2026-11-17: upheld.
* **Lesson**: route-specific (the build dominates the step cost in this code).
