# R28 — metric-whitened L-BFGS from one exact S build (`plbfgs`) (LIVE)
* **The idea**: Pay for one exact metric build (1.9 CPU-min for `che`), whiten the parameters with it, $\theta=\theta_0+Mz$, $M=U(w+10^{-7}w_{\max})^{-1/2}$, and then run L-BFGS in $z$ with exact gradients at 0.12 CPU-min each. The metric removes the near-redundant parameter combinations that made plain L-BFGS (R27) and Newton-CG (R29) crawl, and the cheap gradient steps replace further expensive builds.
* **Lineage**: Round 22 sub-question A, Operation A on R27 within the same batch (the plain form's failure points at conditioning); combines L29's truncated metric with quasi-Newton steps.
* **Outcome**: LIVE (T1 AMBIGUOUS, 2026-11-17). `che` from the 742206 endpoint: 7.217e-4 → 6.479e-4 in 9.0 CPU-min including the build, gamma/gamma_LM = 1.12 (second half 0.99), so it matches the ladder per CPU-minute and does not beat it by the pre-registered factor 3 (`tests/P4.1_cheap_su2_r25/RULE.md`). No `pcg` run (its build alone is about 7 CPU-min). Sharpest gap: whether refreshing the metric every $k$ gradient steps (a hybrid of the ladder and R28) beats both; that test needs a quick-note job of about 30 CPU-min and is not proposed until the coordinator decides whether 12-site convergence is still worth buying (L50: by itself it gives at most 1.1× the ladder's rate).
