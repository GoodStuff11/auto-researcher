# R28 — metric-whitened L-BFGS from one exact S build (`plbfgs`) (LIVE)
* **The idea**: Pay for one exact metric build (1.9 CPU-min for `che`), whiten the parameters with it, $\theta=\theta_0+Mz$, $M=U(w+10^{-7}w_{\max})^{-1/2}$, and then run L-BFGS in $z$ with exact gradients at 0.12 CPU-min each. The metric removes the near-redundant parameter combinations that made plain L-BFGS (R27) and Newton-CG (R29) crawl, and the cheap gradient steps replace further expensive builds.
