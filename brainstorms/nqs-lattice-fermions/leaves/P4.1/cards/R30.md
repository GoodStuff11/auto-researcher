# R30 — Anderson/DIIS acceleration of the ladder linear-method iterates (`anderson`) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: The ladder linear method (LM: one exact overlap S and Hamiltonian H build per step, solved at cutoffs 1e-5..1e-9, best rung kept) converges linearly at 12 sites, with per-step gain ratios 0.76–0.98 (job 742206). Treat one ladder step as a fixed-point map $x\mapsto G(x)$ and extrapolate from the last $m$ iterates and residuals $G(x_k)-x_k$ by Anderson mixing (DIIS) at no extra build cost.
* **Lineage**: Round 22 sub-question A (coordinator addition), cross-field translation from electronic-structure SCF convergence; parked at T0, tested round 23.
* **Source analogy**: Pulay's DIIS and Anderson mixing turn slowly, linearly converging SCF iterations into superlinear ones (Pulay 1980; Anderson 1965). Correspondence: Fock-matrix update → ladder LM step; DIIS error vector → parameter residual. Translation gap (decisive, see Evidence): the LM map is not smooth, because the accepted cutoff and line-search factor switch discretely from step to step.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at T1 (2026-11-17).
* **Kill rule**: `tests/P4.1_cheap_su2_r27/RULE_R30.md`, written before the run: `che` arm from the 742206 endpoint (relative energy error eps 7.217e-4), three ladder builds, safeguarded type-II Anderson (depths m = 1..min(k,4), each candidate scored by the exact energy, kept only if below the plain step); rate gamma_AA in relative energy per CPU-minute against the ladder's round-22 rate gamma_LM = 7.27e-6. KILL if gamma_AA < 1.5 gamma_LM; PASS-T1 if ≥ 3 gamma_LM and the fitted tail shrinks (2-point geometric ratio r < 0.975 and tail/eps < 0.5); AMBIGUOUS otherwise.
* **Evidence**: one thread, exact sums, 8.86 CPU-min total. eps 7.217e-4 → 6.707e-4 over 3 builds (8.80 CPU-min): gamma_AA = 5.80e-6 = 0.80 gamma_LM (builds 1–2: 0.77); for comparison R28 `plbfgs` gave 1.12. No Anderson candidate was accepted: after build 1, m=1 gave eps 6.889e-4 against the plain step's 6.877e-4; after build 2, m=1 6.800e-4 and m=2 6.781e-4 against plain 6.707e-4. Plain per-build gains 1.83, 1.57, 1.70e-5 (accepted line-search factors s = 1/4, 1, 1/4); r = 0.966, tail/eps = 0.72, so the tail did not shrink.
* **Why this kills it**: Anderson extrapolation was worse than the plain step at every opportunity, so the safeguarded method is the plain ladder plus overhead, below 1.5 gamma_LM. The mechanism is the translation gap: the step length jumps between 1/4 and 1 from one build to the next, so the residual history does not describe a smooth linear contraction, and the secant model extrapolates in the wrong direction.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: history depths 3–5 (need 4–6 builds, 12–17 CPU-min, untested); Anderson applied to the raw LM direction before the line search (a smoother map); the `pcg` arm; unpolished starts.
* **Strongest case against the kill**: the probe's own plain steps gained less (about 6.0e-6 per CPU-min) than the round-22 baseline, so the 0.80 partly reflects step-to-step noise; answered: the verdict does not rest on the baseline, since every Anderson candidate lost to the plain step taken from the same point (by 1.2e-6 to 9.3e-6 in eps).
* **Checked by**: per-leaf critic, 2026-11-17: upheld; its translation repair (Anderson before the line search) is ledger row 8 (objection 11).
* **Lesson**: supports the round-22 proposed entry (at ladder-polished endpoints slow convergence is ill-conditioning, not the linear method's algorithm): a third multisecant scheme (after R27, R29) fails to beat the ladder.
