# R30 — Anderson/DIIS acceleration of the ladder linear-method iterates (`anderson`) (LIVE)
* **The idea**: The ladder linear method converges linearly, with per-step gain ratios 0.76–0.98 (job 742206). Treat one ladder step as a fixed-point map $\theta\mapsto G(\theta)$ and extrapolate from the last $m$ iterates and residuals $G(\theta_k)-\theta_k$ by Anderson mixing (DIIS): a least-squares combination of past residuals that cancels the slowly decaying component, as it does for linearly converging self-consistent-field cycles. It costs no extra builds.
* **Lineage**: Round 22 sub-question A (coordinator addition), cross-field translation from electronic-structure SCF convergence.
* **Source analogy**: Pulay's DIIS and Anderson mixing turn slowly, linearly converging SCF iterations into superlinear ones (Pulay, Chem. Phys. Lett. 73, 393, 1980; Anderson, J. ACM 12, 547, 1965); Anderson mixing is a multisecant quasi-Newton method (Fang & Saad, Numer. Linear Algebra Appl. 16, 197, 2009). Correspondence: Fock-matrix update → ladder LM step; DIIS error vector → parameter residual. Translation gap: the LM map is not smooth (the per-step cutoff choice and line search switch discretely), and each map evaluation costs one full build (2.9 CPU-min for `che`, about 7.6 for `pcg`), so the $m\approx5$ history needed costs as much as the ladder itself.
* **Outcome**: LIVE (T0 PASS, parked). G0: Anderson on VMC linear-method iterates has no record found in a quick search; G1, G3 not applicable (an optimiser); G2: whether the discrete cutoff switching leaves a smooth enough map is unknown; G4: an exact-sum diagnostic at 12 sites only. Cheapest falsifier (about 20 CPU-min for `che`, so a quick-note job, not interactive): 6 ladder steps from the 742206 endpoint with type-II Anderson ($m=4$) extrapolation scored by the exact energy against the plain ladder's gain per CPU-minute; PASS if it at least doubles it.
