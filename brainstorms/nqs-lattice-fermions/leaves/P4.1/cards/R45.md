# R45 — supervised infidelity fit of charge-only and spin-reading exact-S networks to the exact ground state (sup-fit) (LIVE)
* **The idea**: Measure capacity, not optimiser progress. Fit R40's pair (one NNBF, $H=16$, $P=2637$; `che` reads charges, `nnbf` reads spins; both projected exactly onto $S=0$, (+,+)) to the exact 12-site ground state by exact-gradient L-BFGS on $-\log F$ ($F$ the fidelity), same init recipe, equal evaluations, and compare $\kappa=(1-F)_{\rm che}/(1-F)_{\rm nnbf}$. The energy landscape (near-singular metric, L60) is replaced by a regression onto a known target.
* **Lineage**: round-30 batch, item (b); cross-field translation from machine learning (teacher-student fitting as a capacity probe); absorbs R44's 16-site $t$–$J$ point as an optional extension; parent of R47 (released by this LOSS).
* **Source analogy**: in ML, a model family's capacity is probed by fitting a fixed teacher and reading the achievable loss (teacher-student, distillation); in NQS, supervised fits to exact states (Cai & Liu, PRB 97, 035116, 2018; Jónsson, Bauer & Carleo, arXiv:1808.05232; Kochkov & Clark, arXiv:1811.12423). Gap: the infidelity optimum is not the energy optimum (T1: $1-F$ fell while the energy error rose), and the fit is still non-convex.
