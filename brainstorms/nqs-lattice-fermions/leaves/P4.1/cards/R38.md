# R38 — guided (flattened) sampling distribution for the SR step (`guided`) (LIVE)
* **The idea**: The one-step screen showed that the force $f=\mathrm{cov}(O,E_{\mathrm{loc}})$, not the metric, limits sampled SR here. Near the nodes of a determinant state $O=\partial\ln\psi$ diverges, so under $\lvert\psi\rvert^2$ sampling the force has heavy tails. Draw the step's samples from a flatter mixture, $q=0.8\,p+0.2\,p_{\mathrm{flat}}$ ($p\propto\lvert\psi\rvert^2$, $p_{\mathrm{flat}}\propto\lvert\psi\rvert$), with self-normalised weights $p/q$: same cost per sample, bounded weights, more samples where the force estimator is noisy.
* **Lineage**: Round 22 Operation B (active probing: change where the estimator samples, not how it solves), after the class bounds killed every metric-side route (R32, R35, R36) and the force control variate (R37). Prior art: guiding functions for infinite-variance forces in real-space QMC (Attaccalite & Sorella, PRL 100, 114501, 2008; Pathak & Wagner, AIP Adv. 10, 085213, 2020) and overdispersed $\lvert\psi\rvert^\alpha$ sampling for NQS (arXiv:2507.05352, with NetKet code). G0: known and recently published, so it is adopted as the leaf's optimiser, not claimed as a new method (as R21); G1, G3 PASS; G2 tested below; G4: same per-sample cost at any size; whether the heavy-tail gain persists at 24 sites is the sharpest gap.
* **Outcome**: LIVE (T1 PASS, 2026-11-17). One-step screen (`tests/P4.1_cheap_su2_r25/RULE_B.md`, `che` SR-trained start, 16,384 samples, 3 seeds): Rbar 0.086 ± 0.011 against 0.016 ± 0.022 unguided, about what 4 times the samples give (0.095). Multi-step (RULE_C.md, R21's protocol and rule baseline, one seed): R(15) = 0.364 against R21's 0.195 (GUIDED-WINS, threshold 0.295; no step raised the exact energy; R21's final value reached at step 3). Next: T1b (RULE_D.md, three 60-step replicas, does it reach the exactly polished `che`, R = 0.7475?), `submit_guided.sbatch`, compute gate PASS (cap 2.0 CPU-h), not submitted. Untuned: the mixture weight 0.2 and $p_{\mathrm{flat}}$ were fixed before the screen.
