# R46 — S^2-penalised spin-reading network as a feasible point of the projected family at 24 sites (pen-bound-24) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Detect an expressivity loss at 24 sites without exponential projection. Train the unprojected spin-reading `nnbf` with an augmented-Lagrangian penalty on $\langle\hat S^2\rangle$ until its $S\neq0$ weight $w\le\langle S^2\rangle/2$ is below 0.025; then $P_S\psi$ has energy at most $E(\psi)$ whenever the $S\ne0$ part's mean energy $E_{\rm rest}\ge E(\psi)$ (the magnitude bound $w\lvert E_{\rm rest}-E\rvert/(1-w)$ needs $E_{\rm rest}$, not estimable at 24 sites, and grows with size times $w$), so $\psi$ is nearly a feasible point of the exactly projected spin-reading family. If its energy is below `che`'s by a margin, the charge-only restriction costs expressivity at 24 sites: a one-sided, scalable LOSS test.
* **Lineage**: round-30 batch; cross-field translation from constrained optimisation; R43's "strongest case against" (a spin penalty). Repaired as R47 (constraint from an $S=0$ teacher).
* **Source analogy**: augmented-Lagrangian methods (Hestenes 1969; Powell 1969) turn an equality constraint into penalty plus multiplier, so an unconstrained optimiser returns near-feasible points; constraint $\hat S^2=0$, feasible set the projected family. Gap: feasibility is approximate and a penalty is not an exact symmetry (Anti-Solution (a)); as a measurement it needs only a near-feasible point.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at G2 (T0, 2026-11-17), recorded prediction instead of a run; reopenable if R43 returns CONTAMINATION-EXPLAINS or MARGINAL (R43 returned CHE-STILL-WINS, 2026-11-18: not reopened).
* **Kill rule**: Gate 2: is the outcome predictable from data in hand, so the run changes no decision?
* **Evidence**: R42 (job 759907, equal 3 CPU-h): unpenalised `nnbf` ended 0.149–0.155 above `che` and was behind per step too (step 715: $-16.18$ vs $-16.43$). The penalty adds an $\hat S^2$ estimator ($\mathcal O(N_s^2)$ flip ratios per sample, about 1.5–2× per step) and constrains the path further. Prediction at R42's budget: NO-DETECTION ($E_{\rm pen}>E_{\rm che}$), p about 0.9; NO-DETECTION says nothing about expressivity (one-sided).
* **Why this kills it**: the only informative outcome has probability about 0.1 at an affordable budget; converging both arms at 24 sites is R51.8's open question and above the leaf's budget.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: the same test if R43 returns CONTAMINATION-EXPLAINS or MARGINAL (both mean contamination explains a large share of R42's gap); the fidelity version (R47).
* **Strongest case against the kill**: R42's gap may be mostly contamination, which the penalty removes, so detection could exceed 0.1. Answered by R43 (job 764949, CHE-STILL-WINS): removing the spin-1 and spin-3 contamination by flip symmetry roughly halved $\langle S^2\rangle$ (to 0.61–0.68) but left `che` at 0.49–0.55 of the gap and bought nothing at equal CPU ($E_{\rm even}-E_{\rm nnbf}=+0.017$, $0.000$; `che` also led at matched steps), so the reopen trigger is not met.
* **Checked by**: per-leaf critic, 2026-11-17: upheld; reopen trigger extended to MARGINAL, sign form of the bound (objection 5); per-leaf critic 2026-11-18: reopen trigger not met (verified).
* **Lesson**: route-specific (a one-sided test is worth running only where its informative side is not predicted unlikely).
