# R34 — importance-reweighted sample reuse across steps (`reuse`) (LIVE)
* **The idea**: Raise the samples per step without drawing new ones: keep the last $K$ steps' configurations, reweight them by $\lvert\psi_{\mathrm{new}}/\psi_{\mathrm{old}}\rvert^2$ (correlated sampling), and accumulate both $S$ and $f$ over them. One-step evidence says the force needs more samples, and reuse supplies them if drawing a configuration is a large part of its cost.
