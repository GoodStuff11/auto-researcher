# R16 — squeezed (spin–charge separated) frame term (`sq`) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Add to each occupied site's orbital row a learned row indexed by the site's rank among occupied sites along the snake path, an Ogata–Shiba-like squeezed coordinate. It depends on the charge configuration $c$ only, so exact spin projection $P_S$ stays closed-form, and each hole shifts every downstream row, a non-local extensive dressing a fixed-width affine backflow cannot represent.
* **Lineage**: Operation B.1 (duality / frame transformation), Round 10. Parked after a first AMBIGUOUS T1 until a polished parent existed; rerun at the ladder-polished parents of job 742206 in Round 23.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at T1 (2026-11-17).
* **Kill rule**: Round 10 tangent rule (`tests/P4.1_cheap_su2_r10/tangent.py`, unchanged in `tests/P4.1_cheap_su2_r26/RULE.md`): first-order energy gain $G$ (fraction of the parent's gap $g$ to $E_0$ removed by the best step in the span of the new directions, exact sums) for the route's directions against a matched generic foil (one random hidden unit times a free orbital vector, same count); KILL if route $\le$ foil on both `che` (projected UHF-type control) and `pcg` (R2); PASS if route $\ge2\times$ foil and $\ge0.05\,g$ on both; AMBIGUOUS otherwise.
* **Evidence**: open $4\times3$, $5+5$, $U=8$, $t'=-0.25$, exact sums over 627,264 configurations. Ladder-polished `che` (eps 7.217e-4): route 0.0064 vs foil 0.0067. Ladder-polished `pcg` (eps 4.230e-4): route 0.0097 vs foil 0.0127. Round 10 (SR-trained parents): `che` 0.018 vs 0.016, `pcg` 0.026 vs 0.027. Controls exact (positive 1.0000000000; negative, random $S=0$ vectors, 0.0033 and 0.0090). Logs: `round-23-R16R17.md`, `round-23.md` section 6.
* **Why this kills it**: the PASS clause needs a route gain of at least 0.05 of the gap at both parents, and the route gains at most 0.0097 of the gap at either polished parent (0.0064 on `che`, 0.0097 on `pcg`), five times below that bar; the KILL clause (route $\le$ foil on both) also fires as written. The kill rests on magnitude, not on the foil comparison: route–foil gaps of 0.0003 and 0.003 are no larger than the scatter between the two negative controls (0.0033, 0.0090), so they do not show the squeezed coordinate carries less information than generic directions.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: higher-order (non-tangent) gains after training the squeezed rows; other snake paths or 2D squeezing; larger clusters where the downstream shift spans more rows; a squeezed coordinate used as a backflow input instead of an additive row.
* **Strongest case against the kill**: the foil comparison is not like-for-like: the foil added 120 and 144 independent directions against the squeezed term's 90 and 96 (tangent ranks 284 vs 314 on `che`, 314 vs 362 on `pcg`, `tests/P4.1_cheap_su2_r26/results/tangent_*.json`), so per direction the squeezed term beat the foil at both parents; the foil is one random draw (seed 7) with no spread; and the parents are unconverged (fitted tails 22–100% of eps; first-order residual 0.0023 g on `che`, 0.0177 g on `pcg`). Answered only on magnitude: none of this lifts a 0.0097 g gain to the 0.05 g bar at first order. Higher-order gains after training stay untested.
* **Checked by**: per-leaf critic, 2026-11-17: kill upheld with the reason corrected to magnitude (objection 6).
* **Lesson**: route-specific (a single extra coordinate failing a tangent test does not generalise beyond this frame term).
