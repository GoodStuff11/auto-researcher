# R47 — teacher-student distillation of a 24-site DMRG state into charge-only and spin-reading students (distill-24) (LIVE)
* **The idea**: Take R45's capacity probe to 24 sites, where exact projection of a spin-reading network is unaffordable. Draw a fixed dataset of configurations from a DMRG MPS of the open $8\times3$ strip ($N_e=20$; perfect MPS sampling, with amplitudes) and fit students by maximising the estimated fidelity $\hat F=\lvert\mathbb E_g[\psi/g]\rvert^2/\mathbb E_g[\lvert\psi/g\rvert^2]$: `che` (exact $S$), unprojected `nnbf` and flip-even `nnbf`. For a teacher that is exactly $S=0$, $F(P_S\psi)=F(\psi)/\langle\psi|P_S|\psi\rangle\ge F(\psi)$: an unprojected spin-reading fit lower-bounds the exactly projected family's best fidelity, so a clear `nnbf` win is a valid LOSS verdict for the restriction at 24 sites (one-sided). Amplitudes only: about 50× cheaper per sample than a VMC step.
* **Lineage**: round-30 batch; cross-field translation from ML knowledge distillation (Hinton, Vinyals & Dean, arXiv:1503.02531) and NQS supervised fitting (Jónsson, Bauer & Carleo, arXiv:1808.05232); scalable mutation of R45 and translation repair of R46 (constraint supplied by the teacher's symmetry).
* **Source analogy**: a student's ability to match a strong teacher on the teacher's own data measures its capacity apart from how the teacher was found. Teacher → DMRG MPS; data → configurations from $|g|^2$. Gap: the teacher is imperfect ($\chi=1000$, energy error 0.016–0.032 against the students' 0.16–0.19) and 24-site fidelities are small, so the ratio estimator needs a held-out set.

* **T1 rule requirements (per-leaf critic r30, objection 2; binding before T1 is prepared)**: (a) the bound needs an exactly $S=0$ teacher, but the stored 8×3 DMRG is U(1) only (no $\langle S^2\rangle$ recorded). With teacher weight $w_T$ outside $S=0$, $\sqrt{F(P_S\psi)}\ge(\sqrt{F(\psi)}-\sqrt{w_T})/\sqrt{\langle P_S\rangle_\psi}$. So T1 either measures the teacher's $\langle\hat S^2\rangle$ (as an MPO), bounds $w_T$, and requires the LOSS margin to exceed the $\sqrt{w_T}$ correction, or reruns DMRG with SU(2) symmetry. (b) As in R45's amended rule: LOSS needs the `che` arm PLATEAUED (otherwise UNSETTLED, progress-limited, L33), and a half-budget class that agrees.
* **Sharpest Gap**: NO-LOSS is weaker than LOSS (it compares `che` with the best cheap spin-reading student, not the projected family); whether $\hat F$ at 24 sites is precise enough at 65k samples; whether unprojected students can show the 12-site loss (T1).
