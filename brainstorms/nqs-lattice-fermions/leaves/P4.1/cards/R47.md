# R47 — teacher-student distillation of a 24-site DMRG state into charge-only and spin-reading students (distill-24) (LIVE)
* **The idea**: Take R45's capacity probe to 24 sites, where exact projection of a spin-reading network is unaffordable. Draw a fixed dataset of configurations from a DMRG MPS of the open $8\times3$ strip ($N_e=20$; perfect MPS sampling, with amplitudes) and fit students by maximising the estimated fidelity $\hat F=\lvert\mathbb E_g[\psi/g]\rvert^2/\mathbb E_g[\lvert\psi/g\rvert^2]$: `che` (exact $S$), unprojected `nnbf` and flip-even `nnbf`. Since the teacher has $S=0$, $F(P_S\psi)=F(\psi)/\langle\psi|P_S|\psi\rangle\ge F(\psi)$: an unprojected spin-reading fit lower-bounds the exactly projected family's best fidelity, so a clear `nnbf` win is a valid LOSS verdict for the restriction at 24 sites (one-sided). Fitting also suppresses the student's $S\ne0$ weight for free (R46's feasible point without a penalty). Amplitudes only, no local energy: about 50× cheaper per sample than a VMC step.
* **Lineage**: round-30 batch; cross-field translation from ML knowledge distillation (Hinton, Vinyals & Dean, arXiv:1503.02531) and NQS supervised fitting (Jónsson, Bauer & Carleo, arXiv:1808.05232); scalable mutation of R45 and translation repair of R46 (constraint supplied by the teacher's symmetry).
* **Source analogy**: a student's ability to match a strong teacher on the teacher's own data measures its capacity apart from how the teacher was found. Teacher → DMRG MPS; data → configurations from $|g|^2$. Gap: the teacher is imperfect ($\chi=1000$, energy error 0.016–0.032 against the students' 0.16–0.19) and 24-site fidelities are small, so the ratio estimator needs a held-out set.
* **Outcome**: LIVE (T0 survivor; T1 not prepared this round).
* **Gates (T0)**: G0 PASS (supervised NQS fitting and MPS pretraining are known; the measurement and the one-sided projection bound are not). G1 PASS. G2 PASS (no 24-site fidelity data exist). G3 PASS with caveat: an MPS truncation could favour low-entanglement structure, but both students fit the same teacher. G4 PASS: teacher cost $\mathcal O(N d\chi^2)$ per sample, student cost per amplitude as in R42; reaches wherever DMRG is near exact (strips and cylinders, 24–48 sites), i.e. where references exist.
* **Cost (T0 estimate)**: no MPS is stored (only energies, `P4.1_cheap_su2_scaling/dmrg_irrep/`): DMRG rerun with MPS dump about 6 CPU-h ($\chi=1000$ took 1,400 s on 16 threads); 65k teacher samples about 1–2 CPU-h; 3 students × 2 seeds × about 3 CPU-h; total about 26 CPU-h.
* **Sharpest Gap**: NO-LOSS is weaker than LOSS (it compares `che` with the best cheap spin-reading student, not the projected family); whether $\hat F$ at 24 sites is precise enough at 65k samples; T1 = a 12-site check with exact $g$ that the estimator and the implicit contamination suppression work, conditional on R45's T2.
