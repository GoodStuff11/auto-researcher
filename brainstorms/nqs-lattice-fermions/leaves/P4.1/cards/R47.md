# R47 — teacher-student distillation of a 24-site DMRG state into charge-only and spin-reading students (distill-24) (SHELVED)
* **The idea**: Take R45's 12-site capacity probe (fit both networks to a known ground state, compare infidelities) to 24 sites, where exactly projecting a spin-reading network is unaffordable. Sample configurations from a DMRG matrix-product state of the open $8\times3$ strip ($N_e=20$) and fit students by maximising the estimated fidelity: `che` (exact $S$), unprojected `nnbf`, flip-even `nnbf`. For an exactly $S=0$ teacher, $F(P_S\psi)=F(\psi)/\langle P_S\rangle_\psi\ge F(\psi)$, so an unprojected spin-reading student's fit lower-bounds the projected family's: a clear `nnbf` win is a valid one-sided LOSS verdict at 24 sites, at amplitude-only cost.
* **Lineage**: round-30 batch; cross-field translation; scalable mutation of R45 and translation repair of R46 (constraint supplied by the teacher's symmetry). Released 2026-11-18 by R45's LOSS.
* **Source analogy**: ML knowledge distillation (Hinton, Vinyals & Dean, arXiv:1503.02531) and supervised NQS fitting (Jónsson, Bauer & Carleo, arXiv:1808.05232): a student's loss on a strong teacher's data measures its capacity apart from how the teacher was found. Gap: the teacher is imperfect and the students must also learn its symmetry from samples.
* **Outcome**: SHELVED (refused by the orchestrator on G2 grounds after the gate passed (KILL's action equals refusal's), 2026-11-18): T1 (`tests/P4.1_cheap_su2_r47/RULE_T1.md` with Amendments 1–2, online distillation; `voi.md` digest cd50cd7bb7ce; `compute_gate.py` PASS at 7.7 CPU-h) was not submitted. T1 at 12 sites (exact teacher) asked whether unprojected students can still show R45's 12-site loss; PASS needed $\kappa_u=(1-F)_{\rm che}/(1-F)_{\rm best\ unprojected}\ge1.65$.
* **Why shelved**: G2, outcome predictable and not decision-changing; capped cost 7.7 CPU-h (T1), then about 26 CPU-h (T2). Recorded prediction (log `round-34-R47`): KILL 0.40, UNSETTLED 0.30, INVALID 0.25, PASS 0.05. Pre-flight (12 sites): unprojected students lose to `che` mostly on spin contamination (40–70% of their error) early in fitting (100–400 of a planned 4,000 evaluations): $\kappa_u$ 0.73 at 100, 0.18 at 400 (`nnbf_u` $1-F$ $9.9\times10^{-3}$ against `che` $1.75\times10^{-3}$), while the exactly projected spin-reading student on the same samples leads ($\kappa_p\approx1.8$, read at 100 evaluations only). The refusal stands: PASS needs $\kappa_u$ to rise ninefold (0.18 → 1.65); KILL and refusal both close the leaf, so a one-sided 24-site test has no power to change the action.
* **What would unshelve it**: a cheap symmetry-restoring step for the spin-reading student: exact projection affordable at 24 sites, or an $S^2$ penalty shown (at 12 sites, against the exact state) to reach the projected arm's fidelity; or a budget that funds a converged 24-site comparison outright.
