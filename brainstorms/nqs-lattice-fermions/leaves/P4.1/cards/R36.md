# R36 — covariance-shrinkage metric: Ledoit–Wolf, NERCOME, low-rank-plus-diagonal (`shrink`) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: At about 6 samples per parameter the sample metric's eigenvalues are spread (small ones too small), and inverting it amplifies noise, as a sample covariance does in mean–variance portfolio optimisation. Replace $\hat S$ by a statistically better covariance estimate before the shifted solve: Ledoit–Wolf linear shrinkage toward the diagonal, NERCOME split-sample eigenvalue correction, or a factor model (top 256 eigenpairs plus a diagonal residual).
* **Lineage**: Round 22 sub-question B, cross-field translation from high-dimensional statistics and finance. Closest prior art in NQS: SR's diagonal shift read as shrinkage (Hur, arXiv:2609.23334, 2026).
* **Source analogy**: Markowitz weights $\Sigma^{-1}\mu$ from a sample covariance are dominated by estimation error when $P/N$ is not small; Ledoit–Wolf shrinkage (J. Multivariate Anal. 88, 365, 2004), nonlinear shrinkage and NERCOME (Abadir, Distaso & Žikeš, J. Econometrics 182, 2014) fix it. Correspondence: $\Sigma$ → $S$, $\mu$ → $f$, portfolio → SR step. Gap: in portfolios the mean $\mu$ is often treated as known or less noisy than $\Sigma$; here the force is the noisier ingredient.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at T1 (2026-11-17).
* **Kill rule**: `tests/P4.1_cheap_su2_r25/RULE_B.md` (as R32).
* **Evidence**: one step from `che`'s SR-trained start, 3 seeds: base 0.016 ± 0.022; Ledoit–Wolf 0.016 ± 0.020 (intensity 0.02–0.03); NERCOME 0.021 ± 0.019; factor model −0.011 ± 0.016; exact metric −0.024 ± 0.011; exact force +0.171 ± 0.015.
* **Why this kills it**: each estimator is below the per-variant KILL line $\bar R\le\bar R(\mathrm{base})+0.02=0.036$: Ledoit–Wolf 0.016, NERCOME 0.021, factor model $-0.011$, against base 0.016. The class bound (exact metric) is not used, since same-sample metric and force errors partly cancel and it is not an upper bound.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: shrinkage of the force (James–Stein toward a prior direction, e.g. the previous step), which is the translation with the gap repaired; tried as R39 and KILLED at T1.
* **Strongest case against the kill**: one start, one size; at $P\gg N$ (24 sites) the metric noise may dominate; unresolved.
