# R22 — balanced coupling-tree (spin-network) basis with a charge determinant (`spin-tree`) (SHELVED)
* **The idea**: Sample in a spin-adapted basis $\lvert c,T\rangle$: singly occupied sites carry spin $\tfrac12$, $T$ lists the intermediate spins of a fixed balanced binary coupling tree over the sites (a k-d tree in 2D), root fixed to $S$. Any $\psi(c,T)=D(c)F_\theta(c,T)$, with $D$ a charge-only determinant and $F_\theta$ any network, is an exact spin-$S$ state for every $\theta$; the commutant lemma does not apply, since this is not the $S^z$ basis. R0.4 died because sequential coupling (GUGA, Vieijra et al.) makes a hop across $k$ singly occupied sites reach about $2^k$ states. T0 lemma (Wigner–Eckart): a hop is a rank-0 tensor inside the subtree of the lowest common ancestor (LCA) of its sites, so it changes only the nodes on the two paths to the LCA; on a balanced 2D tree that is $\mathcal O(L^{2.5})$ connected states per configuration in the worst case, against $e^{\mathcal O(W)}$ for sequential coupling on a width-$W$ strip.
* **Lineage**: translation repair of R0.4, restoring sparsity by changing the coupling tree; cross-field translation from lattice gauge theory and SU(2) tree tensor networks, Round 20; the "tree spin factor" R2.5 left open. Gates at T0: G0 narrow PASS (no fermionic balanced-tree version with a charge determinant found); G1, G3 PASS ($F$ must learn the spin-sector sign, the Hard Core); G2 OPEN, the main risk (exact-singlet forms were 4–7 times worse in job 451886); G4 PASS on paper. Falsifier 1 rule: KILL if the connected-state exponent exceeds 3, PASS if $\le2.6$ (`tests/P4.1_cheap_su2_r21/`). Falsifier 2 rule (`tests/P4.1_cheap_su2_r23/RULE.md`, `RULE_amend1.md`): KILL (G2) if $\epsilon_{\mathrm{tree}}$ is at least the polished `che`'s, PASS-T1 if $\le0.6$ of it.
* **Source analogy**: Non-Abelian lattice gauge theory: the spin-network basis makes Gauss's law exact and keeps the Hamiltonian local because recoupling stays on the links a term touches; Jacobi trees in nuclear few-body physics; SU(2) tree tensor networks (T3NS, Gunst et al., J. Chem. Theory Comput. 14, 2026 (2018); arXiv:2104.14869). Correspondence: link irreps → intermediate spins; Gauss law → fixed root $S$; plaquette term → hop between leaves. Gap: fermions need a sign and the tree has no determinant for the spin sector, so $F$ carries that sign. Prior art: arXiv:1905.06034, 2104.14869, 2604.06841.
