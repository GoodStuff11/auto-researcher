# R35 — recursive-least-squares (Kalman) running estimate of the metric (`rls-S`) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Keep a running, exponentially forgotten estimate of $S$ across SR steps, as recursive least squares and the Kalman filter's information form do, so that the metric used at each step averages several steps' samples (stale rows reused at no cost) while the force comes from fresh samples. With $S$ averaged over $K$ steps the samples per parameter in the metric rise $K$-fold.
* **Lineage**: Round 22 sub-question B, cross-field translation from signal processing.
* **Source analogy**: online natural gradient is an extended Kalman filter, whose information matrix is a running Fisher estimate (Ollivier, Electron. J. Stat. 12, 2018, arXiv:1703.00209); RLS adaptive filters average the input covariance with a forgetting factor; K-FAC keeps moving averages of curvature factors. Correspondence: input covariance → $S$; forgetting factor → staleness across SR steps. Gap: in adaptive filtering the covariance estimate is the noisy part, here it turns out not to be.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at T1 (2026-11-17).
* **Kill rule**: `tests/P4.1_cheap_su2_r25/RULE_B.md`: V4 (metric from 4N samples, force from N, zero staleness) bounds the route from above; KILL if Rbar(V4) ≤ Rbar(base) + 0.02; and the metric-side class bound (as R32).
* **Evidence**: one step from `che`'s SR-trained start, 3 seeds: base 0.016 ± 0.022, V4 0.003 ± 0.011, exact metric −0.024 ± 0.011, plain 4N (metric and force) 0.095 ± 0.012.
* **Why this kills it**: even a perfect, staleness-free fourfold metric average did not help, and the exact metric hurt; the gain of more samples comes entirely through the force.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: a running average of the force (momentum), which SPRING (R15) and R34 address.
* **Strongest case against the kill**: averaging only $S$ breaks the regression consistency between $S$ and $f$ estimated on the same samples (their errors partly cancel), so a joint running estimate could behave differently; that is R34's bound (V5), not this route.
* **Checked by**: per-leaf critic, 2026-11-17: upheld.
* **Lesson**: as R32 (force noise limits the step); translation-specific gap: the property that made covariance averaging pay in filtering (noise in the covariance) is absent here.
