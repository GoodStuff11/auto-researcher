# R41 — Anderson mixing on the raw fixed-cutoff LM direction (R30 translation repair, `anderson-raw`) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: R30 mixed whole ladder steps, whose cutoff and line-search factor switch between builds, so its map was not smooth. Here the cutoff (1e-7) and factor (beta = 1/4) are fixed, $G(x)=x+\beta d(x)$ with $d$ the raw linear-method (LM) direction from one exact S, H build, and type-II Anderson extrapolates over the last $m\le3$ residuals, lengthening the effective step at no extra build cost.
* **Lineage**: translation repair of R30 (per-leaf critic round 23, objection 11; ledger row 8), round 26.
* **Source analogy**: DIIS/Anderson mixing in self-consistent-field electronic structure (Anderson, J. ACM 12, 547, 1965; Pulay, Chem. Phys. Lett. 73, 393, 1980). Source: a slowly, linearly contracting fixed-point map; Fock update → fixed-cutoff LM step, error vector → $\beta d$. It works there because the map is smooth and its slow modes nearly linear, so secant pairs span them. Gap: the LM step is already Newton-like, cut to 1/4 because the full step overshoots, so it is curvature-limited, not contraction-limited.
