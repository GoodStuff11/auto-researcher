* **Question**: Can $E_{\mathrm{loc}}$ be replaced by an unbiased stochastic estimator over the $\mathcal{O}(M^4)$ connected configurations, with variance bounded so that the total cost to reach chemical accuracy scales better than exact enumeration?
* **Answer**: No, not in general. If each amplitude costs one network evaluation, we proved that an exact local energy cannot skip terms, and an unbiased random one has a variance floor set by the terms it leaves out. On hydrogen chains and rings (accuracy checked up to 20 orbitals, cost up to 136), all 41 ideas that used extra structure either lost accuracy or cost more than existing methods. Dropping small terms in localized orbitals, which is already known, stayed best. Reusing earlier samples during optimisation was not refuted; its test was judged predictable and not run.
* **What we showed**: If the network is only queried one amplitude at a time, an exact local energy must look at every connected configuration, and any unbiased random estimate has a variance that no sampling scheme can push below a bound set by the configurations it skips; both are proofs within that model. On hydrogen chains and rings, the escapes that use more structure all failed: cheaper per-term networks lost accuracy as the basis grew and cost four times more at 136 orbitals; contractible families lost accuracy; error certificates either kept almost every term or, in localized orbitals, needed reference amplitudes over so many configurations that they cost 1.5 to 3.8 times full enumeration.
* **What it would take**: To overturn the general No: a network architecture whose amplitudes have structure that sidesteps the one-query-at-a-time model. To revive an escape: a cheap and accurate reference amplitude (relative error under about 17% at a tenth of the cost of enumeration) for the localized certificate, or a target where optimisation is limited by step count rather than noise, for sample reuse (its next test costs about 0.2 CPU-hours).
* **Conclusion**: When each connected amplitude costs one network call (the black-box model), no exact local energy can skip connected terms and no unbiased one can beat an inclusion-probability variance bound; this is proven within that model, and each of the 41 routes tried either falls under it or lost accuracy or cost when it used structure to escape. The last certified route, variational screening in localized orbitals (R7.4), is admissible on states trained to $\Delta E\le0.03$ mHa; at the leaf's working accuracy ($\approx0.74$ mHa) H$_8$ excess reached 9–16$\tau$, and it needs reference amplitudes on a global neighbourhood, so it costs more than exact enumeration with any accurate reference. Plain screening in localized orbitals (prior art) remains the best estimator found; anchored reuse in step-limited optimisation (R5.2) was not refuted, only refused by the compute gate as predictable, so the leaf is closed at this budget.
* **Scope**: hydrogen chains and rings (H$_4$–H$_{10}$; STO-3G, s-only, 6-31G**), HF weight 0.37–0.57; accuracy from ED up to $K=20$ spatial orbitals, cost ED-free up to $K=136$; NNBF with a Jastrow trained on the exact energy (no VMC noise); R7.4 on H$_6$, H$_8$ in Löwdin orbitals; sign half of the Hard Core.
* **Compute spent**: about 162 CPU-h (411919, 452238, 621074, plus about 0.95 CPU-h of capped interactive T1 runs); all Slurm spend predates the compute gate.

**The question.** VMC averages $E_L(\mathbf{n})=H_{\mathbf{n}\mathbf{n}}+\sum_{\mathbf{n}'\in\mathcal{C}(\mathbf{n})}H_{\mathbf{n}\mathbf{n}'}\psi(\mathbf{n}')/\psi(\mathbf{n})$; with dense integrals $\lvert\mathcal{C}\rvert=\mathcal{O}(N^2M^2)$ forward passes, exact enumeration (EXACT) dominates. The leaf asked for an estimator, unbiased or biased by at most $\tau=0.2$ mHa, whose cost to a fixed error bar falls relative to EXACT as $M$ grows and beats screening (SCREEN: drop $\lvert H_{\mathbf{n}\mathbf{n}'}\rvert\le\varepsilon$), semistochastic (SS) and heat-bath sampling.

**The result: the Saturation Diagnostic.** For mixed-sign rows one importance proposal has a variance floor; on H$_6$ even oracle sampling cost 146× EXACT (R1.1, R1.3). If amplitudes are revealed only by oracle calls, an adversary can change any unqueried one, so exact $E_L$ needs $\Omega(\lvert\mathcal{C}\rvert)$ calls (Statement 1); an unbiased estimator querying term $j$ with probability $\pi_j$ has worst-case variance ≥$\sum_js_j^2(1/\pi_j-1)$ (Statement 2). Both are proofs in the black-box model (a PROVEN-IMPOSSIBLE sub-result held in this leaf). Reuse gives no first-order gain while optimisation is noise-limited (Statement 3). Escapes: cheaper terms (F$_{A1}$), contraction (F$_{A2}$), amortisation (F$_B$), certified bias (F$_C$).
