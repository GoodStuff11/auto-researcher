## Leaf Status Summary
* **State**: ANSWERED

* **Question**: For a state-of-the-art fermionic NQS (HFDS or NNBF) on a small doped Hubbard cluster, what fraction of the energy error and of the pairing-correlation error comes from wrong signs $\mathrm{sgn}\,\psi_\theta\ne\mathrm{sgn}\,\psi_0$ versus wrong amplitudes? This decides whether P1 is the binding constraint at all.
* **Answer**: Mostly amplitudes, for the energy, on the clusters we could solve exactly. With the ground state's symmetries built into training, wrong signs cause at most 31% of the energy error on a 15-site and a 16-site doped cluster of the t–J model (the large-U limit of the Hubbard model). On three 12-site Hubbard clusters the share is under 3%, but there the network is big enough to fit almost any symmetric state, so this only shows that the earlier "signs dominate" result came from broken symmetry. The share of the pairing error is not settled (about a third to two thirds). Nothing beyond 16 sites was measured.
* **What we showed**: On two doped t–J clusters of 15 and 16 sites with open edges, where the network has far fewer parameters than the symmetric states it could fit, the best energy reachable with the network's own signs and ideal magnitudes accounts for at most 31% of the energy error in every run, and at most 23% once total spin is projected exactly. The 16-site reading was confirmed on a 15-site cluster this leaf had not looked at, under a rule fixed beforehand (16%). An earlier "signs dominate" result on 12-site Hubbard clusters disappeared once training kept the lattice symmetry. The pairing-error share came out about one half on 16 sites but one third on 15 sites, so it is unsettled.
* **What it would take**: To overturn the energy answer: a symmetry-projected backflow network on an open t–J cluster of this kind whose signs carry 60% or more of the energy error, or an independent code giving a larger sign share on the same states. To extend it: a Hubbard cluster too large for the network to fit (about 30 CPU-hours, predicted to agree); beyond about 20 sites the measure itself needs an exact ground state and a hard inner optimisation, so no such budget is known.
* **Conclusion**: At exact-diagonalization size, the energy error of a symmetry-projected neural-network backflow is amplitude-dominated: on the two open $t$–$t'$–$J$ clusters below the sector-fitting regime, the sign-fraction upper bound is $f_E^{\mathrm{up}}\le0.31$ in every run (largest 0.3047, W arm width 4 seed 22, after P1.3 job 625182; 0.157 on the held-out $5\times3$) and $\le0.23$ after exact spin projection; on the three $4\times3$ Hubbard tori it is at most 2.4%. The decisive test's PASS ("sign is binding") was symmetry contamination of unprojected states. The share of the pairing error carried by signs is not settled: about half on $4\times4$, but 0.31 on the held-out instance, between the pre-registered thresholds.
* **Scope**: exact full-basis sums, no sampling. Energy claim: open-boundary $t$–$t'$–$J$ ($t'=-0.25t$, $J=0.4t$, $6+6$) on $4\times4$ (projected NNBF, 1284/1734/2634 parameters, 3 seeds × 3 starts) and $5\times3$ (1626 parameters, one seed, 1200 SR steps), both at parameter counts ≤11% of the projected sector dimension; plus the three $4\times3$ periodic Hubbard tori of P1.1-P (sector-fitting regime, read only as "the PASS was contamination"). Not covered: Hubbard below the sector-fitting regime, sizes beyond 16 sites, sampled VMC, the competitor half of the Hard Core, the pairing share.

**The question.** A neural quantum state (NQS) $\psi_\theta(\mathbf n)$ can have wrong signs or wrong magnitudes. Branch P1 (sign layers P1.2, wrong-sign basins P1.3) is worth pursuing only if the signs limit accuracy. This leaf measures that for neural-network backflow (NNBF: a Slater determinant whose orbitals are shifted by a one-hidden-layer network reading the configuration) on doped 2D clusters small enough for exact diagonalization (ED).

**The tool (R1).** With $E_0$ exact, $E_\theta$ the network's, and $E_{\mathrm{sign}}(s_\theta)$ the lowest energy any non-negative amplitude vector reaches when forced to carry the network's signs, $\Delta E_{\mathrm{sign}}=E_{\mathrm{sign}}-E_0$ and $\Delta E_{\mathrm{amp}}=E_\theta-E_{\mathrm{sign}}$ are non-negative, add to the total error and are gauge-free. $f_E=\Delta E_{\mathrm{sign}}/(E_\theta-E_0)$; the same state gives $f_C$ for the long-range $d$-wave pair correlation. $E_{\mathrm{sign}}$ is co-NP-hard, so the test reports a rigorous upper bound $f_E^{\mathrm{up}}$ (best of four L-BFGS-B starts and a fixed-node state, calibrated to recover $E_0$ from exact signs): a small $f_E^{\mathrm{up}}$ certifies a small sign share without trusting the optimiser.

**Chain of evidence.**
1. *Decisive test, unprojected (329057).* PASS if $f_E^{\mathrm{up}}\ge0.6$ on all three $4\times3$ Hubbard tori at width 128, FAIL if ≤0.3: 0.607, 0.772, 0.652, PASS (Jastrow–Slater foil 0.24–0.33).
2. *Confound (P1.1-P, 407525).* The networks inherited wrong signs from a symmetry-broken mean-field start; trained in the ground state's space-group sector with the same code, widths, steps and seeds, median $f_E$ falls to 0.005–0.008 (SYMMETRY-DOMINATED). Parameter count there is comparable to the sector dimension, so the tori show only contamination.
3. *Below sector fitting.* $4\times4$ OBC $t$–$t'$–$J$ ($d_{S\chi}=30220$; P1.3 jobs 507179, 625182): $f_E^{\mathrm{up}}\le0.31$ in every run, $\le0.23$ spin-projected; from 1284 to 2634 parameters the sign error falls 2.9× and the amplitude error 2.1×. Read after the fact, so not closing on its own.
4. *Held-out replication.* Under the unchanged 329057 thresholds, $5\times3$ OBC (1626 parameters, $d_{S\chi}=15080$, P2.1 job 457482): $f_E^{\mathrm{up}}=0.157$ (0.134 spin-projected), amplitude-dominated.
5. *Pairing.* $f_C$ 0.48–0.71 on $4\times4$, 0.305 held out (between thresholds): no pairing claim.

**Why the alternatives fail.** Swapping exact and trained parts (R2, Gate 0) gives a negative amplitude share on all 12 states, because trained magnitudes are co-adapted to trained signs. The sign-mismatch weight (R3, Gate 4) does not track energy; lattice fixed-node energies (R4, Gate 0) bound more loosely. Kills upheld by the per-leaf critic, 2026-11-17.

**Beyond this leaf.** L31 (swapping co-optimised factors is not an error budget); L32 (only the best energy at fixed signs is a sign-only measure); L1 (an unprojected budget from a broken-symmetry start measures contamination).

**Strongest objection and answer.** "Small clusters let any network fit the signs" (cross-node critic, Major 2): true on the tori, read only as contamination; the energy claim rests on the open clusters at 4–11% of the sector dimension. Not answered: one held-out seed, $t$–$J$ only.

**Limits.** See Scope; also $f_E^{\mathrm{lo}}=0$ everywhere (the claim is a bound), the $5\times3$ state is not at a plateau (last 700 steps moved $E$ by ~10% of $\Delta E_{\mathrm{tot}}$), and "held out" means not read by this leaf (its value was printed in the 457482 log after the thresholds were fixed).

### Closure certificate
* **Claim**: For symmetry-projected NNBF trained by exact-sum SR on open $t$–$t'$–$J$ clusters at ED size ($4\times4$, $5\times3$; $6+6$, $t'=-0.25t$, $J=0.4t$) at ≤11% of the projected sector dimension, $f_E^{\mathrm{up}}\le0.31$ in every run (max 0.3047) and ≤0.23 spin-projected, so the energy error is amplitude-dominated under P1.1's pre-registered rule; P1.1's sign-binding PASS on the $4\times3$ tori is symmetry contamination (P1.1-P). No claim on the pairing share, sub-fitting Hubbard instances or sizes beyond 16 sites.
* **Decisive evidence**: P1.1-P job 407525 (critic-signed 2026-11-16); $4\times4$ budgets from P1.3 jobs 507179, 625182 (27 runs, re-checked 2026-11-16); held-out $5\times3$ budget from P2.1 job 457482 under the 329057 thresholds (2026-11-17).
* **Why no doubt remains**: contamination → P1.1-P; sector fitting → only $P/d_{S\chi}=0.04$–$0.11$ used; spin contamination → exact projection, ≤0.23; optimiser bias → $f_E^{\mathrm{up}}$ is a rigorous upper bound; after-the-fact reading → held-out read; unconverged $4\times4$ → extension moved $f_E$ ≤0.018; one held-out seed → accepted as scope.
