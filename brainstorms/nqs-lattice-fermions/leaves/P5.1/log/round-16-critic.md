2. **The push-through scope is wrong: the pseudo-inverse variant is also SR.** By SVD, A^T(AA^T)^+_c b = VΣ_c^{-1}U^T b = (A^TA)^+_c A^T b. The nonzero spectra are identical, so a relative eigenvalue cutoff on the sample-space Gram matrix is the same cutoff on S. Chen & Heyl's cutoff MinSR is therefore truncated-SVD SR at N<P, not a different optimiser. MinSR equals SR with the same regulariser at every N, and only the cost differs. Fix the card, L79 ("only a pseudo-inverse-cutoff variant differs") and the summary's "What would close it" ("pseudo-inverse-cutoff variant untested").
