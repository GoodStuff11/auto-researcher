# R51.14 — importance-reweighted reuse of high-leverage configurations (levreuse) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Keep a pool of past configurations with high leverage (those carrying the small-eigenvalue directions, R51.3) and reuse them in later steps with weights $|\psi_\theta/\psi_{\theta_{\rm old}}|^2$, so each step sees more of the rare, informative configurations without drawing them again; a targeted version of P4.1 R34's sample reuse.
* **Lineage**: round 5 batch (orchestrator's candidate list), Operation A from P4.1 R34 (killed at G4 for cost), adding leverage targeting.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at T0 (2026-11-17).
* **Kill rule**: P4.1 R34's pre-registered bound: reuse saves only the drawing cost, so its speed-up per step is at most (draw + row cost)/(row cost); below 2 it dies at G4.
* **Evidence**: (i) a reused configuration needs its local energy and row at the new parameters; P4.1 R34 measured (24 sites) draw : local energy : rows = 0.27 : 3.12 : 0.31 s, ratio 1.05–1.08. Choosing which configurations to reuse by leverage does not change that ratio. (ii) The weights of old draws blow up with age: in R51.13's T1 at 10 sites, the $\chi^2$ of the weight $p_{\theta}/p_{\tilde\theta}$ is $1.7\times10^{-4}$ one step apart, 0.074 five steps apart and $1.7\times10^4$ twenty steps apart (maximum weight $1.9\times10^{10}$), because high-leverage configurations sit near the moving nodes. Leverage targeting selects exactly the configurations whose weights diverge.
* **Why this kills it**: the kill rests on weight growth alone: the targeted configurations are the ones whose importance weights explode beyond a few steps (χ² $1.7\times10^4$ at 20), so reuse over the horizon a leverage pool needs adds variance rather than information. The 5–8% cost bound holds only for exact i.i.d. draws.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: reuse over 1–5 steps, where χ² ≤ 0.074, under samplers with long autocorrelation (Markov chains), where drawing dominates the cost; reuse inside a control variate (R51.13).
* **Strongest case against the kill**: exact i.i.d. draws hide Markov-chain cost; the round-5 rebuttal (more sweeps leave the per-reused-sample cost unchanged) works against itself, since dearer fresh draws raise the drawing share and so what reuse saves. Accepted for short horizons (scope above); the kill stands only for long-horizon leverage pools.
* **Checked by**: per-leaf critic (2026-11-17): kill upheld on weight growth; objection 6 applied.
* **Lesson**: route-specific (it rests on R51.13's measured weight growth near moving nodes).
