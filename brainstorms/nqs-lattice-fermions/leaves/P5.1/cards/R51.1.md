# R51.1 — noise-floor scaling of fixed-length guided SR (nfloor) (LIVE)
* **The idea**: Guided sampled SR (P4.1 R38: stochastic reconfiguration with samples drawn from $0.8|\psi|^2+0.2|\psi|$) plateaued at $R\approx0.51$ of the gap against 0.7475 for exact polishing of the 12-site `che`. Measure whether the plateau is a sampling floor: run the step at a fixed metric length, always taken, at 1, 4 and 16 times 16,384 samples per step (and at half the length), and read off how the remaining deficit scales with samples. The scaling exponent says what any scalable optimiser must buy: more samples, variance reduction, or better conditioning.
* **Lineage**: initial batch, seed (a) from P4.1 R38's kill; a premise test shared by R51.3 and R51.7. Feeds R51.8 (size scaling of the sample need).
* **Outcome**: LIVE (T2 decided: NOISE-FLOOR, not STEP-NOISE, 2026-11-17; unit 3 refused by the compute gate, G2).
* **T1a (stored data of job 752885, `RULE_A.md`)**: over steps 41–60 of R38's 3 replicas the best step-length candidate gained 0.0079 of the gap per step, the held-out choice 0.0020; length 0.005 never raised the exact energy and gained $0.0078\pm0.0006$. Most of R38's plateau was the step choice.
* **T2 (`tests/P5.1_noise_floor_r01/RULE_B.md`; jobs 759974, 761860; `results/score_unit2.json`)**: $R_{\rm plat}$ = mean of the last 20 steps, $\Delta=0.7475-R_{\rm plat}$. A ($N$=16,384, $\tau$=0.005, 120 steps) 0.576, $\Delta$ 0.171; B ($4N$) 0.680, 0.068; C ($16N$, exact duplicate aggregation) 0.732, 0.0155; D ($N$, $\tau$/2, 240 steps) 0.601, 0.147. $\Delta_B/\Delta_A=0.395\le0.62$ and $\Delta_C/\Delta_B=0.229\le0.62$ → **NOISE-FLOOR**, fitted exponent $a=0.87$ (0.67 for A→B, 1.06 for B→C). $\Delta_D/\Delta_A=0.858>0.75$ → **not STEP-NOISE**. Tail slopes 0.0012, 0.0009, 0.0007, 0.0005 per step: no arm has levelled off, so $\Delta$ is the shortfall after a fixed step count, not a floor.
* **Unit 3**: the margin clause (2 replica spreads, 0.030) names A (0.025), C (0.026), D (0.018); prepared (`submit_unit3.sbatch`, `voi_unit3.md`), gate REFUSED (G2: verdict unchanged predicted at p 0.93; C's tail spread 0.0011 makes a flip of 0.053 implausible, STEP-NOISE flip about 0.03). Recorded prediction stands.
* **Reading**: a stationary noise ball over a fixed deterministic shortfall ($\Delta=\Delta_0+b/N$, fitted on A, B) predicts $\Delta_C=0.042$ against 0.0155: noise slows the path itself. At equal total samples fewer samples per step win (1.97M: A 0.587, B 0.516, C 0.403; 3.9M: B 0.608, D 0.606, C 0.493); 90% of $R_{\rm pol}$ first reached by B at 6.75M samples, 95% only by C at 22M.
* **Sharpest Gap**: the exponent is a 12-site property at fixed step count; how the needed samples grow with size is R51.8's question.
* **Gate 4 claim**: a measurement; per-step cost is SR's ($\mathcal O(NP^2+P^3)$ plus $N$ local energies). The 0.8 CPU-min/step of C relies on duplicate aggregation (31,700 unique of 262,144 draws), a small-cluster saving (R51.8, T0).
* **Gate 0**: constant-step noise balls and SR's $\mathcal O(1/N_s)$ floor are known (Wang & Liu, arXiv:2604.18357); their size against a determinant NQS's representable minimum had not been measured. G1–G3 PASS.
