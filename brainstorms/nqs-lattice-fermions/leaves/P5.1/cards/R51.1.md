# R51.1 — noise-floor scaling of fixed-length guided SR (nfloor) (LIVE)
* **The idea**: Guided sampled SR (P4.1 R38: stochastic reconfiguration with samples drawn from $0.8|\psi|^2+0.2|\psi|$) plateaued at $R\approx0.51$ of the gap against 0.7475 for exact polishing of the 12-site `che`. Measure whether the plateau is a sampling floor: run the step at a fixed metric length, always taken, at 1, 4 and 16 times 16,384 samples per step (and at half the length), and read off how the remaining deficit scales with samples. The scaling exponent says what any scalable optimiser must buy: more samples, variance reduction, or better conditioning.
* **Lineage**: initial batch, seed (a) from P4.1 R38's kill ("a plateau at a noise floor is expected for any fixed-sample SR"); a premise test shared by R51.3 and R51.7.
* **Outcome**: LIVE (T1a done; T2 unit 1 prepared, gate PASS, not submitted).
* **T1a (free, stored data of job 752885, `tests/P5.1_noise_floor_r01/RULE_A.md`)**: over steps 41–60 of the 3 replicas the best candidate per step would have gained 0.0079 of the gap, the held-out choice realised 0.0020 (A1 MIXED, just above the 0.002 SELECTION bound); the shortest length 0.005 never raised the exact energy (0 of 60) and gained $0.0078\pm0.0006$, while the chosen 0.01 rose in 68% of steps. Replica average: $R(\bar\theta)=0.548$ against 0.536 best replica (A2 WEAK). So most of R38's plateau was the step-length choice, not the sampled direction.
* **Pending kill rule**: `tests/P5.1_noise_floor_r01/RULE_B.md` (deficit $\Delta=0.7475-R_{\rm plat}$; NOISE-FLOOR if $\Delta(4N)/\Delta(N)\le0.62$ and $\Delta(16N)/\Delta(4N)\le0.62$; NOT-NOISE if $\Delta(4N)/\Delta(N)\ge0.85$ and $\Delta(16N)/\Delta(N)\ge0.75$; REACHES if $R_{\rm plat}(N)\ge0.7475$; STEP-NOISE from the half-length arm).
* **Sharpest Gap**: whether 120 fixed-length steps plateau at all (gain was still 0.008 per step at the R38 plateau); an unplateaued arm cannot support NOT-NOISE.
* **Gate 4 claim**: a measurement, not a method: per-step cost is that of SR ($\mathcal O(N P^2+P^3)$ plus $N$ local energies, polynomial in $V$); the exponent it measures is a property of the 12-site landscape and must be re-measured once at 24 sites before it is used there.
* **Gate 0**: the floor itself is known (constant-step SGD noise ball; SR with SPRING momentum has an $\mathcal O(1/N_s)$ floor, Wang & Liu arXiv:2604.18357); its size against the representable minimum for a determinant NQS has not been measured. G1–G3 PASS (exact scoring, non-stoquastic doped instance).
