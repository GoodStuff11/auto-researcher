# R51.1 — noise-floor scaling of fixed-length guided SR (nfloor) (LIVE)
* **The idea**: Guided sampled SR (P4.1 R38: stochastic reconfiguration with draws from $0.8|\psi|^2+0.2|\psi|$, step length chosen on held-out samples) was stopped after 60 steps at $R\approx0.51$ of the gap, against 0.7475 for exact-sum ladder optimisation of the 12-site `che`. Measure whether the shortfall is set by sampling noise: a fixed metric length, always taken, at 1, 4 and 16 times 16,384 samples per step (and at half the length); the exponent says what a scalable optimiser must buy (samples, variance reduction or conditioning).
* **Lineage**: initial batch, seed (a) from P4.1 R38's kill; premise test shared by R51.3 and R51.7; feeds R51.8.
* **Outcome**: LIVE (T2 decided: NOISE-FLOOR, not STEP-NOISE, 2026-11-17; unit 3 refused by the compute gate, G2).
* **T1a (job 752885 data, `RULE_A.md`)**: pre-registered verdict A1 MIXED (0.00202 against a 0.002 bound). At equal samples and step counts R38 led the fixed-step arm A at every point (R(60) 0.508–0.536 vs 0.494) and gained 0.0020 of the gap per step over steps 41–60, as A did at similar $R$: fixed-step and held-out-step SR progress at the same rate at equal samples through 60 steps; the fixed step simply keeps going. The 0.0078 per step of length 0.005 is a one-step counterfactual (3–4× high, R51.9). Full numbers: log round-05-critic.
* **T2 (`tests/P5.1_noise_floor_r01/RULE_B.md`; jobs 759974, 761860)**: $\Delta=0.7475-$(mean $R$ of last 20 steps). A ($N$=16,384, $\tau$=0.005, 120 steps) 0.171; B ($4N$) 0.068; C ($16N$) 0.0155; D ($N$, $\tau/2$, 240 steps) 0.147. $\Delta_B/\Delta_A=0.395$, $\Delta_C/\Delta_B=0.229$ (both $\le0.62$) → **NOISE-FLOOR**, $a=0.87$; $\Delta_D/\Delta_A=0.858>0.75$ → **not STEP-NOISE**. Tail slopes 0.0005–0.0012 per step: no arm levelled off, so $\Delta$ is a fixed-step-count shortfall, not a floor.
* **Unit 3**: margin clause names A, C, D; gate REFUSED (G2). Recorded p 0.93 overstated (observed margin taken as true, within-run spread for between-seed); corrected $p\approx0.88$–0.90, still $\ge0.8$, refusal stands. The pre-registered release order (second seeds) was overridden by the gate.
* **Reading**: a stationary noise ball over a fixed shortfall ($\Delta_0+b/N$, fit on A, B) predicts $\Delta_C=0.042$ against 0.0155: noise slows the path. At equal total samples fewer samples per step win within 120 steps (1.97M: A 0.587, B 0.516, C 0.403), but only the larger batches reached 90% of $R_{\rm pol}$ (B at 6.75M; A never).
* **Sharpest Gap**: (i) $\Delta$ is measured against $R_{\rm pol}$ (a different optimiser), not this schedule's noise-free run, so $a$ mixes a deterministic 120-step shortfall with noise (a 2-point ball fit puts the deterministic part at 0.033, above C's 0.0155); $R_{\rm pol}$ bounds the representable minimum's energy from above, so in $R$ it is a lower bound on $R^\star$. R51.8's exact arms fix both. (ii) 12 sites only; size growth is R51.8's question.
* **Gate 4 claim**: a measurement; per step SR's $\mathcal O(NP^2+P^3)$ plus $N$ local energies; C's 0.8 CPU-min/step relies on duplicate aggregation, a small-cluster saving (R51.8, T0).
* **Gate 0**: SR's $\mathcal O(1/N_s)$ floor is known (Wang & Liu, arXiv:2604.18357); its size against a determinant NQS's representable minimum was not. G1–G3 PASS.
