# R51.22 — symmetry-paired antithetic samples (antithetic) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Pair every drawn configuration with its image under a lattice symmetry or a global spin flip and average their force terms, as antithetic variates; if the per-sample force terms of the two partners are negatively correlated, the pair's variance falls below that of two independent draws.
* **Lineage**: round 12 batch (orchestrator's list), from Monte Carlo integration (antithetic variates).
* **Source analogy**: antithetic variates (Hammersley & Morton 1956): pairing $u$ with $1-u$ helps when the integrand is monotone, so the pair is negatively correlated. Correspondence: $u\mapsto1-u$ → a symmetry $g$ of the state. Gap: a symmetry of the state makes the integrand invariant, i.e. correlation +1.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at T0 (2026-11-18).
* **Kill rule**: T0: the pair's force terms must be less than perfectly correlated.
* **Evidence**: the `che` state is projected onto the (+,+) point-group irrep and draws are already over orbit representatives (`ns_run.py`), and it has exact total spin $S=0$. For $\psi(gx)=\chi_g\psi(x)$ for all $\theta$, $O_k(gx)=O_k(x)$ and $E_{\rm loc}(gx)=E_{\rm loc}(x)$. Checked on 256 random 5×2 configurations (`vr_t1.py --flipcheck`): spin flip changes $\ln|\phi|$ by $\le1.8\times10^{-12}$ with sign ratio +1 and $O$ by $\le1.8\times10^{-12}$ relative. The open strips have no translations.
* **Why this kills it**: partners have identical force terms (correlation 1), so the pair is one sample: no variance reduction.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: unprojected states or broken-symmetry ansätze (where symmetrisation is a projection, P4.1's question); non-symmetry antithetic maps (none with known negative correlation was found).
* **Strongest case against the kill**: none; the invariance is exact by construction.
* **Gate 4 (explicit)**: zero gain at every size.
* **Gate 0**: symmetric sampling over orbits is standard in projected NQS; it is already in the foil.
* **Checked by**: not yet reviewed.
* **Lesson**: route-specific (follows from exact projection of the ansatz).
