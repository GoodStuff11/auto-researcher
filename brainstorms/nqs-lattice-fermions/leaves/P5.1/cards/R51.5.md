# R51.5 — median-of-means SR step over sample blocks (mom) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Split the $N$ samples of an SR step into $K$ blocks, solve the SR system $S_b\,\delta_b=f_b$ in each block (metric and force from the same block, so their error cancellation is kept), and take the geometric median of the $K$ block steps. A median is robust to the rare near-node configurations that make the force heavy-tailed (L59), so the step should be less noisy than the mean at the same $N$.
* **Lineage**: initial batch, cross-field translation from robust statistics (barrier B2); killed at T0 in round 1, reopened by the per-leaf critic (2026-11-17) because R51.1 superseded the kill's premise.
* **Source analogy**: median-of-means estimators for heavy-tailed means (Lugosi & Mendelson, Found. Comput. Math. 19, 1145, 2019). Source: many samples per block, so the median only discards outlier blocks; block mean → block SR step. Gap: each block must itself give a usable SR step.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at T1 (2026-11-18), on the premise arm's rise clause (job 769669); T2 never released.
* **Former kill**: T0 in round 1 (one-step Born probe, L59); reopened when R51.1 showed fixed-step guided SR descends at 6.2 samples per parameter (`log/round-08-R51.5.md`).
* **Kill rule**: pre-registered 2026-11-17, before any data; 12-site `che`, guided draws, fixed length $\tau=0.005$, exact scoring, seed 9000: "*Premise (T1)*: the fixed-step guided arm at $N/4=4096$ samples per step, 240 steps. KILLED if its $R$ at 0.98M total draws (step 240) is below 0.247 (half of the 16,384 arm's 0.494 at the same draws) or more than a third of its steps raise the exact energy." T2 ($K=4$) was gated on it.
* **Evidence**: $R$ = fraction of the gap from the start energy to the exact ground energy closed (gap 0.022373). Job 769669 (`tests/P5.1_r51_5/results/score_premise.json`), $P$=2637 (4096 draws is 1.55 per parameter), 983,040 draws: $R(240)=0.495$ against the 0.247 line (the 16,384 arm: 0.494 at equal draws); energy rose on 83 of 240 steps (0.346) against the line of 80, binomial sd at $p=1/3$ about 7.3. R51.1 arm A (16,384 per step) rose on 18 of 120.
* **Why this kills it**: the rise clause is met (83 > 80), so the premise fails under the rule. The premise was that a 4096-sample block step is reliable enough to be one of four votes. Measured at matched progress, one block step went uphill on 35% of steps against 3% for the 16,384-sample average of four such blocks: averaging, not a median, removed the noise.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: larger blocks ($K=2$ of 8192, or $K=4$ at $N$=65,536); other robust estimators (trimmed means, coordinate medians, clipping of near-node forces); other sizes or a decaying step.
* **Strongest case against the kill**: 83 against 80 is about 0.4 binomial sd, $R$ kept pace with the 16,384 arm (0.495 vs 0.494), and 5×2 fixed-step SR rose on about 0.39 of steps at every $N$, so rises might be generic to fixed-step SR near the floor. Answered: at matched progress the 4096 arm rose far more often than arm A ($R<0.3$: 9/51 vs 0/13; 0.3–0.4: 24/70 vs 0/15; 0.4–0.5: 50/119 vs 4/33; overall below $R$ 0.495: 83/240 = 0.35 vs 2/59 = 0.03, Fisher $p=10^{-7}$), so the extra rises come from small blocks, not from fixed-step SR near the floor.
* **Checked by**: per-leaf critic 2026-11-18: upheld under the pre-registered rule; matched-R rise rates 0.35 vs 0.03 support it; objections 6, 7 applied.
* **Lesson**: L72 (a count line within about 1 sd needs a stage-2 seed clause); otherwise route-specific (one size, one block size).
