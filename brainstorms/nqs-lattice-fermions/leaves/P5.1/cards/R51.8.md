# R51.8 — samples per step needed versus system size (nscale) (LIVE)
* **The idea**: Run R51.1's optimiser (guided SR, fixed metric length 0.005, always taken, 120 steps) at two, then three, exact-sum sizes, each against its own noise-free version (exact sums, $N=\infty$), and measure $N_{\rm needed}(V)$, the samples per step at which the shortfall to that reference is 5% of its gain. Compare its growth with $V^3$ and with the growth of the effective support $e^{H[|\psi|^2]}$, and score plain SR (Born draws; MinSR's regime at $N<P$) on total samples from the same runs. This turns R51.1's 12-site exponent into the leaf's actual question: does the sampled cost per step stay polynomial?
* **Lineage**: Operation B (round 4), from R51.1's NOISE-FLOOR verdict and the orchestrator's size-scaling question; premise for R51.10.
* **Outcome**: LIVE (T2 prepared: unit 1, gate PASS 2.5 + 8.0 CPU-h, not submitted, 2026-11-17).
* **Rule**: `tests/P5.1_size_scaling_r04/RULE.md`. $r$ = the need at 12 sites divided by that at 10: POLY-CONSISTENT if $r\le1.73$ ($=1.2^3$), SUPPORT-LIKE if $r\ge3.89$ (measured support growth, 781 → 3035), AMBIGUOUS between; comparator GUIDED-FEWER if plain SR needs $\ge2\times$ the total samples to 90% of the reference gain.
* **T0**: (i) R51.1's shortfall fell as $N^{-0.87}$; a stationary noise ball over a fixed shortfall predicts 0.042 at $16N$ against 0.0155, so the need is set by the step's direction fidelity, which ridge-regression theory makes polynomial unless the residual's tails grow (R51.10). (ii) Duplicate aggregation: unique draws saturate at the effective support ($e^{H}=781$ at 10 sites, 3035 at 12; 31,700 unique of 262,144 guided draws at 12), so the saving $N/n_{\rm unique}$ tends to 1 once $e^{H}\gg N$; at the observed rate (×3.9 per 2 sites) $e^{H}\sim10^{7}$ at 24 sites, so the 0.8 CPU-min/step of R51.1's best arm is a small-cluster artefact and the honest scale is the un-aggregated 2.2 CPU-min/step. (iii) The existing arms give no SR comparison: all are guided SR; plain SR exists only as R21's held-out run (R 0.195 at 15 steps) and the SR-trained start itself (1024 samples × 1000 steps, $R=0$ by definition). At $N\ge P$ MinSR equals SR with the same shift.
* **Unit 1** (`submit_unit1_s10.sbatch`, `submit_unit1_ref12.sbatch`, `voi_unit1a.md`, `voi_unit1b.md`): S10 (5×2, 4+4, $P$=1832, start trained interactively, 9.9 CPU-min) with exact, guided 1k–1M and Born 1k–256k arms; S12 exact reference (R51.1's A–C are its guided rungs). Predicted POLY-CONSISTENT (p 0.55) from $\kappa$ (1.24e5 vs 1.08e5).
* **Sharpest Gap**: representativeness (L40): S10's start is 5× closer to its exact energy and $P$/representatives is 10× larger than S12's, so S10 is easier; geometry (2-leg vs 3-leg) and doping (1/5 vs 1/6) also change. S12' (6×2) in unit 2 separates them.
* **Gate 4 claim**: per sample $\mathcal O(\text{connections}\times N_e^3+P)$, per step $\mathcal O(NP^2+P^3)$; $N_{\rm needed}(V)$ is the measured unknown. Over a factor 1.2–1.4 in $V$ it can exclude growth like the support, not prove a polynomial.
* **Gate 0**: no measurement of samples-to-representable-minimum versus size for fermionic NQS found (round-01 searches); SR sample-complexity bounds (Wang & Liu, arXiv:2604.18357) state a floor, not its size scaling. G1–G3 PASS (doped, non-stoquastic, exact references).
