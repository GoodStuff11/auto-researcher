# R51.8 — samples per step needed versus system size (nscale) (LIVE)
* **The idea**: Run R51.1's optimiser (guided SR, fixed metric length 0.005, always taken, 120 steps) at two, then three, exact-sum sizes, each against its own noise-free version (exact sums, $N=\infty$), and measure $N_{\rm needed}(V)$, the samples per step at which the shortfall to that reference is 5% of its gain. Compare its growth with $V^3$ and with the growth of the effective support $e^{H[|\psi|^2]}$, and score plain SR (Born draws; MinSR's regime at $N<P$) on total samples from the same runs.
* **Lineage**: Operation B (round 4), from R51.1's NOISE-FLOOR verdict and the orchestrator's size-scaling question; premise for R51.10.
* **Outcome**: LIVE (T2: 5×2 ladder IN-BETWEEN under rev. 1; rev. 2 adopted before any 6×2 data; 769098, 5×2 second seeds, running).
* **Evidence**: unit 1a (763968), 5×2, one seed: guided shortfall $s$ 0.926→0.040 ($N$ 1k–262k), Born 1.057→0.072; 5% need 94,150 guided, Born censored above 262,144. To 90% of the exact gain: guided 1.72M total draws, Born 16.25M (9.4×, provisional). T0: the fixed step bounces with period 2 near the minimum, so the 5% need is window-dependent; rev. 2: $s$ = 0.10 on the steps 61–120 mean.
* **Rule**: `tests/P5.1_size_scaling_r04/RULE.md` (revision 1, auditor): primary ratio same-geometry, $r'$ = need at 6×2 over need at 5×2, then 7×2; POLY-CONSISTENT if $r'\le1.73$ ($=1.2^3$; $1.4^3$ for 7×2), SUPPORT-LIKE if $r'\ge$ the measured support growth, AMBIGUOUS between. The 4×3 state is a geometry check: R51.1's arms bound its need in (65k, 262k] if its exact-arm plateau lies in [0.732, 0.770], so S10 alone decides that ratio when its need is $\ge$151,700 or $\le$16,850. Comparator GUIDED-FEWER if plain SR needs $\ge2\times$ the total samples to 90% of the reference gain.
* **T0**: (i) R51.1's shortfall fell as $N^{-0.87}$; a stationary noise ball over a fixed shortfall predicts 0.042 at $16N$ against 0.0155, so the need is set by the step's direction fidelity, which ridge-regression theory makes polynomial unless the residual's tails grow (R51.10). (ii) Duplicate aggregation: unique draws saturate at the effective support ($e^{H}=781$ at 10 sites, 3035 at 12; 31,700 unique of 262,144 guided draws at 12), so the saving $N/n_{\rm unique}$ tends to 1 once $e^{H}\gg N$; at the observed rate (×3.9 per 2 sites) $e^{H}\sim10^{7}$ at 24 sites, so the 0.8 CPU-min/step of R51.1's best arm is a small-cluster artefact and the honest scale is the un-aggregated 2.2 CPU-min/step. (iii) The existing arms give no SR comparison (all are guided SR; plain SR only as R21's held-out run and the SR-trained start); at $N\ge P$ MinSR equals SR.
* **Staged plan**: 5×2 ladder (done), 6×2 ladder (re-audit after 769098), 7×2 ladder; the 4×3 exact arm is not bought for now.
* **Sharpest Gap**: representativeness (L40): S10's start is 5× closer to its exact energy and $P$/representatives is 10× larger than S12's, so S10 is easier; geometry (2-leg vs 3-leg) and doping (1/5 vs 1/6) also change. S12' (6×2) in unit 2 separates them.
* **Gate 4 claim**: per sample $\mathcal O(\text{connections}\times N_e^3+P)$, per step $\mathcal O(NP^2+P^3)$; $N_{\rm needed}(V)$ is the measured unknown. Over a factor 1.2–1.4 in $V$ it can exclude growth like the support, not prove a polynomial.
