# R51.20 — gradient-optimal importance sampling of the force (gradq) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Draw the step's configurations from $q\propto p\,\lVert O_c(x)(E_{\rm loc}(x)-E)\rVert$, the variance-optimal importance distribution for the energy gradient (force) rather than for the step, reweighting by $p/q$; this targets the heavy-tailed configurations that set the force noise, which grow with the occupied support.
* **Lineage**: round 12 batch (orchestrator's list), field's own playbook (importance sampling for VMC gradients, arXiv:2507.05352).
* **Outcome**: KILLED at T0 (2026-11-18).
* **Kill rule**: T0: the route must beat, at equal cost, the cheapest distribution already measured for the same error (R51.11's oracle $q^\*\propto p\sqrt h$ and its free binned amplitude-only optimum).
* **Evidence**: derivation plus stored R51.11 T1 numbers (`results_kq_*.json`). The step error is what the optimiser feels; $q^\*\propto p\sqrt h$ minimises it exactly, so any other $q$ (including the gradient-optimal one) has $\kappa\ge\kappa_{q^\*}$. Both need a derivative row and a local energy for every proposal, i.e. a full sample per Markov-chain proposal (cost × the thinning interval). Yet the free binned amplitude-only $q$ already comes within 2.25× (5×2 start: 2200 vs 977, against guided 108,000) and 4.0× (4×3: 5402 vs 1353) of $q^\*$. R51.11's weight variance $E_q[w^2]$ grows with size (2.24 / 3.21 / 4.45 at 10 / 12 / 14 sites for $|\psi|$), and a residual-weighted $q$ concentrates harder.
* **Why this kills it**: its error is bounded below by the oracle's, the oracle beats the free binned distribution by at most 2–4×, and paying a full sample per proposal costs more than that.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: an amplitude-only approximation of $q^\*$ (that is R51.11); exact-enumeration settings where proposals are free.
* **Strongest case against the kill**: the gradient norm might be cheaper to estimate per proposal than $h$; but both need the row and $E_{\rm loc}$, which is the whole per-sample cost.
* **Gate 4 (explicit)**: gain over the free binned $q$ is a constant (2.25–4×) at best; weight variance grows with size. FAIL-leaning.
* **Gate 0**: published family (arXiv:2507.05352); its adoption barrier (per-proposal cost) is not removed.
* **Checked by**: not yet reviewed.
* **Lesson**: route-specific (dominated by R51.11's oracle by construction).
