# R51.21 — Neyman-stratified and quasi-Monte Carlo draws over amplitude strata (strat) (MERGED)
* **The idea**: Partition configurations into strata by amplitude ($\log|\psi|^2$ quantiles) and draw a fixed number from each, allocated by stratum weight × within-stratum spread (Neyman allocation). Stratification removes the between-strata variance and fixes how many tail configurations each step sees. A quasi-Monte Carlo (low-discrepancy) variant would spread draws evenly over the sampler's unit cube.
* **Lineage**: round 12 batch, cross-field translation from survey sampling and QMC integration; merged into R51.11.
* **Source analogy**: Neyman allocation (Neyman, JRSS 97, 558, 1934) and randomised QMC (Owen; Dick & Pillichshammer 2010): variance falls by the between-strata share; RQMC improves the rate for smooth, low-effective-dimension integrands. Gap: force terms are heavy-tailed within strata, and a determinant NQS sampled by Markov chains has no smooth unit-cube map.
* **Outcome**: MERGED into R51.11 (T1, 2026-11-18); QMC variant KILLED at G4.
* **Kill rule**: `tests/P5.1_vr_r12/RULE.md` (written first): MERGED into R51.11 if $\kappa_{\rm strat}\ge0.8\,\kappa_{\rm amp40}$ at both endpoints ($\kappa$: first-order SR step-error constant; amp40: R51.11's binned amplitude-only importance optimum, same 40 bins). QMC: Gate 4.
* **Evidence**: exact sums (`vr_t1.py`, R51.8's 5×2 and 6×2 states): $\kappa_{\rm strat}/\kappa_{\rm guided}$ = 0.020 / 0.0157 (5×2 start / end), 0.0576 / 0.0444 (6×2 start / end); $\kappa_{\rm strat}/\kappa_{\rm amp40}$ = 0.992 / 0.993 / 0.9994 / 0.9995 (same order; $\kappa_{\rm amp40}$ reproduces R51.11's 2200 at the 5×2 start). QMC: low-discrepancy points need a smooth cube-to-configuration map (autoregressive models only); for discontinuous integrands RQMC's rate gain fades as the effective dimension grows (He & Wang, Math. Comp. 84, 2015), here ∝ $N_e$. No QMC or stratified NQS use found (search 2026-11-18).
* **Why this kills it**: Neyman strata on $\log p$ equal R51.11's binned optimum minus a between-strata term below 1%, so the route is that distribution; QMC has no gain that survives size.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: strata on other cheap variables (local energy is not free per proposal); stratification of Markov chains (replica / umbrella chains), which would be how R51.11's binned $q$ is sampled in practice.
* **Strongest case against the kill**: stratification has no weights, so it avoids R51.11's growing weight variance; but at fixed bins Neyman allocation and binned-$q$ importance sampling have the same weights per bin, hence the same $\kappa$ to <1%.
* **Side result for R51.11**: the binned optimum's gain over guided at the endpoints shrinks from 63× (5×2) to 22.5× (6×2), at the starts from 49× to 17.3×; one-step constants (L67).
* **Checked by**: per-leaf critic round 15 (2026-11-18): merge and QMC kill upheld.
* **Lesson**: route-specific (an equivalence, not a mechanism).
