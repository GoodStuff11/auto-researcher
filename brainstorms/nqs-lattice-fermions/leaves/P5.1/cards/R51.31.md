# R51.31 — Born MinSR at N below P against guided SR at equal draws (minsr) (LIVE)
* **The idea**: Fill the untested half of the Question's comparator: MinSR (the SR step solved in sample space) run with fewer draws per step than parameters, Born draws, many short steps, scored at equal total draws against guided SR at $N\ge P$. T0: with a Tikhonov shift MinSR equals SR at every $N$ (push-through, $A^{\mathsf T}(AA^{\mathsf T}+\lambda)^{-1}b=(A^{\mathsf T}A+\lambda)^{-1}A^{\mathsf T}b$; reproduced to $3\times10^{-13}$, `snr_run.py --mode check`), so "MinSR at $N<P$" in NetKet's shift form is SR at $N<P$ solved cheaply. Chen & Heyl's pseudo-inverse-cutoff variant is also SR: by SVD $A^{\mathsf T}(AA^{\mathsf T})^+_cb=V\Sigma_c^{-1}U^{\mathsf T}b=(A^{\mathsf T}A)^+_cA^{\mathsf T}b$, a relative cutoff on the sample Gram matrix is the same cutoff on $S$ (truncated-SVD SR). MinSR equals SR with the same regulariser at every $N$; only the cost differs. A practitioner at $N<P$ shortens the step: R51.8's Born 1024 arm at $\tau$ 0.005 rose above its start ($s$ 1.09).
* **Lineage**: round 16 batch, from the summary's "MinSR half has no route"; complements R51.16.
* **Outcome**: LIVE (T1 done 2026-11-18: **GUIDED-AHEAD** at 10 sites; `tests/P5.1_r16_gen/RULE_minsr.md`, `results/score_r16.json`). 5×2, Born, $N$=1024 ($P$=1832; about 510 unique draws per step), 800 steps = 819,200 draws; trailing-20 $R$ (fraction of the noise-free gain) by $\tau$: 0.0003125 → 0.046, 0.000625 → 0.081 (critic arms, `results/critic_tau/`), 0.00125 → 0.124, 0.0025 → 0.175 (round 17, addendum; `score_r17_minsr_tau.json`); 0.005 diverged (R51.8). References at equal draws (R51.8, $N$=16,384, step 50): guided SR 0.691, Born SR 0.383. No arm reaches sustained 90%; 0.0025 still rising (0.127 at step 600). 16 CPU-min. Reading: best of four step lengths, Born MinSR at $N<P$ is behind Born SR at $N\ge P$ (by 0.21) and guided SR (by 0.52) at 0.82M draws: learning speed at one budget (L33), before either reaches the target. τ/N scaling fails (0.0003125 has Born SR's τ/N and path length, 0.046 vs 0.383): the $N<P$ step direction itself is poor. Caveats: one seed, 10 sites, $\tau$ between 0.0025 and 0.005 untried, truncated-SVD regulariser at $N<P$ untested. A 12-site repeat (4x3, to guided's 4.51M draws, about 3.7 CPU-h) is not proposed: its likeliest outcome (GUIDED-AHEAD, p about 0.9 given these margins) changes no decision.
