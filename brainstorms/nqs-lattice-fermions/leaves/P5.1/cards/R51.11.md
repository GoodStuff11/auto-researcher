# R51.11 — kappa-optimal sampling distribution for the SR step (qopt) (LIVE)
* **The idea**: Choose the distribution the step's samples are drawn from to minimise the error of the SR direction itself, not the energy gradient. The first-order step error from $N$ draws of $q$ is $\sum_x p\,(p/q)\,h(x)/N$, with $h$ each configuration's contribution in the metric norm, so the optimum is $q^\*\propto p\sqrt h$ (optimal subsampling for regression); within what costs only amplitudes, the best is a reweighting of $p$ by a function of $|\psi|$. Guided draws ($0.8p+0.2|\psi|$) were a fixed guess at this.
* **Lineage**: round 5 batch, cross-field translation from statistics (optimal subsampling / A-optimal design), replacing the orchestrator's "leverage-targeted sampling" (leverage alone ignores the residual); extends P4.1 R38.
* **Source analogy**: optimal subsampling probabilities for least squares and logistic regression, $\pi_i\propto|r_i|\,\lVert A x_i\rVert$ (Wang, Zhu & Ma, JASA 113, 829, 2018; Ma, Mahoney & Yu, JMLR 16, 861, 2015). Correspondence: rows → configurations, residual → $e-O_c^{\mathsf T}x$, $A$ → $(S+\lambda)^{-1}$. Gap: $q^\*$ needs rows and local energies per proposal (a Metropolis proposal then costs a full sample); only amplitude-based $q$ is free. Prior art in VMC: overdispersed $|\psi|^\alpha$ with adaptive $\alpha$ tuned to gradient variance (Misery, arXiv:2507.05352, MLST 2026).
