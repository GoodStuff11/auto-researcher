# R51.29 — start-state origin of the support-like growth (startstage) (MERGED)
* **The idea**: Test whether R51.8's ×3.79 growth of the samples per step (10 → 12 sites) belongs to the optimiser or to where each size's run sits. Round-16 T0 called this a stage difference (5×2 limit cycle, z −1.1; 6×2 descending, z −3.7). Round 17 corrected it (critic 16, objection 4, re-measured): over steps 61–120 both exact arms drift by the same share of their own gain $G$ (4.6% at 5×2, 4.3% at 6×2), and both bounce with period 2 (energy rises on 30 of 60 steps at each size); the z-scores differ through the bounce amplitude, not the stage. What differs is the step regime: the first-order gain of one fixed step, $2\tau\sqrt{x^{\mathsf T}Sx}/G$, averages 0.30 at 5×2 over steps 61–120 (range 0.01–0.62, exact rerun reproducing R51.8 to 0), against 0.022 and 0.116 at the 6×2 step-120 state and one step later (`results/r17_gain/`), so τ is about 3–14× longer relative to the remaining gain at 10 sites. The ×3.79 mixes size with step regime. Two variants: (i) measure the need with the step length matched in regime; (ii) from random initialisation.
* **Lineage**: round 16 batch, orchestrator's direction (d). Variant (i) MERGED into R51.7's running test (job 773528): its DECAY arms shorten τ at both sizes, and its CONT mode continues every R51.8 arm at fixed τ (steps 121–180); `score_r15_decay.py` reports both. Variant (ii) dismissed at T0: from random initialisation the early steps have large forces and small needs; the Question asks for the representable minimum, so the need that matters is the late one, which (ii) only postpones (R51.27's evidence (i)).
* **Outcome**: MERGED into R51.7's running test (variant i, 2026-11-18); variant (ii) dismissed at T0.
