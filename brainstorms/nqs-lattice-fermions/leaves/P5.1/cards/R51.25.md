# R51.25 — discrete Stein control functionals from free hop ratios (stein) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: For any function $v$ of the occupations, $Z[v](x)=\sum_{x'}H_{xx'}\frac{\psi(x')}{\psi(x)}(v(x')-v(x))$ has mean exactly zero under $|\psi|^2$ and costs only the amplitude ratios the local energy already computes. Regress the force terms on $Z[v_j]$ for a dictionary of cheap symmetrised features (site density, doublon, nearest-neighbour density–density and $S^z$–$S^z$) and subtract: a free control variate whose span grows with the lattice.
* **Lineage**: round 12 Operation B (forced direction 3, cross-field translation), replacing R51.24 (MLMC killed T0: no known-mean coarse model); also the translation repair of R51.19 (replace $v=O_k$, whose control is too noisy, by cheap bounded features).
* **Source analogy**: control functionals and discrete Stein operators (Oates, Girolami & Chopin, JRSSB 79, 695, 2017; Shi et al., NeurIPS 2022, arXiv:2202.09497): zero-mean functions generated by a Markov generator; Assaraf–Caffarel's ZV is the case generator $=H$. Correspondence: generator → $H$'s hop graph, features → occupation polynomials. Gap: the target's variance must lie in the span of $Z[v]$ for cheap $v$.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at T1 (2026-11-18).
* **Kill rule**: `tests/P5.1_vr_r12/RULE.md` (written first): cost factor 1; KILL if eff$_{6\times2}\ge0.8$ and (eff$_{5\times2}\ge0.8$ or $\rho_6/\rho_5>1$) at the exact-arm endpoints, with the oracle (infinite-sample) regression.
* **Evidence**: exact sums (`vr_t1.py`, $J$ = 46 features at 5×2, 56 at 6×2; $|\langle Z\rangle_p|\le4\times10^{-16}$ rms): $\rho$ = 0.99991 / 0.99995 (5×2 start / end), 0.99993 / 0.99996 (6×2 start / end).
* **Why this kills it**: even the oracle fit removes $<0.01\%$ of the step-error variance at both sizes; a sampled fit only does worse.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: features that include the network's own log-derivatives or amplitudes (then the cost is R51.19's); generators other than $H$ (graph Laplacians) — they produce the same $1/\psi$-scaled terms, which cannot cancel the $O\cdot E_{\rm loc}$ product.
* **Strongest case against the kill**: a richer dictionary might help; but the force terms are products of $O$ (network-specific, unbounded) with residuals, nearly orthogonal to any bounded occupation feature, and 56 features gave $4\times10^{-5}$.
* **Gate 4 (explicit)**: dictionary grows with $V$ at free cost; the measured gain is nil at both sizes.
* **Gate 0**: discrete Stein CVs exist for score-function gradients in ML; not found in VMC/NQS (searches 2026-11-18). G1–G3 PASS.
* **Checked by**: per-leaf critic round 15 (2026-11-18): upheld.
* **Lesson**: proposed library entry (with R51.19): Hamiltonian-generated zero-mean controls do not reduce SR force noise.
