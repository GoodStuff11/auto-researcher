# R51.23 — Rao-Blackwellised force over local Metropolis moves (rbmove) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Replace each sample's force term $g(x)$ by its conditional expectation over one reversible Metropolis move, $Kg(x)=\sum_{x'}K(x,x')g(x')$, which has the same mean under $|\psi|^2$ and lower variance. From a near-node configuration nearly every move is accepted, so the singular $g(x)$ gets almost no weight: the tail that may track the support is averaged away using neighbours whose amplitudes the local energy already evaluates.
* **Lineage**: round 12 batch (orchestrator's list), cross-field translation from MCMC statistics.
* **Source analogy**: Rao–Blackwellisation and waste recycling of rejected moves (Frenkel, PNAS 101, 17571, 2004; Delmas & Jourdain, J. Appl. Prob. 46, 938, 2009): conditional expectations over the proposal never raise the variance. Correspondence: proposal set → hop graph of $H$. Gap: there the neighbours' integrand values are cheap; here $g(x')$ needs each neighbour's derivative row and local energy.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at T1 (2026-11-18).
* **Kill rule**: `tests/P5.1_vr_r12/RULE.md` (written first): eff $=\rho\times(1+E_q[\text{degree}])$; KILL if eff$_{6\times2}\ge0.8$ and (eff$_{5\times2}\ge0.8$ or $\rho_6/\rho_5>1$), at the exact-arm endpoints.
* **Evidence**: exact sums (`vr_t1.py`; Metropolis kernel on the symmetrised hop graph of the orbit representatives, reversibility error $\le10^{-19}$, mean shift $\le10^{-19}$): $\rho$ = 0.132 / 0.108 (5×2 start / end), 0.157 / 0.139 (6×2 start / end); mean degree under guided draws 23.3 (5×2), 29.8 (6×2) → eff 3.2 / 2.6 / 4.9 / 4.3; $\rho_6/\rho_5$ = 1.28 at the endpoints.
* **Why this kills it**: a 7–9× variance cut costs 24–31 samples' worth of rows and local energies, a net loss of 2.6–4.3×, and the variance cut does not grow from 10 to 12 sites while the degree does (∝ $N_e$).
* **What the kill does not rule out**: Rao–Blackwellising only quantities whose neighbour values are free (the energy alone, not the force); sampling one random neighbour (which is just a longer chain).
* **Strongest case against the kill**: neighbour rows could be batched (low-rank updates) at below one sample's cost each; even at 1/5 of a sample per neighbour eff $\approx$ 0.6–0.95 at 6×2, and the cost still grows with $V$. Unresolved but G4-negative.
* **Gate 4 (explicit)**: cost factor ∝ degree ∝ $V$; variance cut constant (0.11 → 0.14). FAIL.
* **Gate 0**: no NQS/VMC use found (searches 2026-11-18). G1–G3 PASS.
* **Checked by**: not yet reviewed.
* **Lesson**: route-specific (the neighbour cost is a property of force terms).
