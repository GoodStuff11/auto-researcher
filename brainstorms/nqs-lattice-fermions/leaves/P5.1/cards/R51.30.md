# R51.30 — trust-region step length from a held-out metric, TRPO/PPO-style (kltrust) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: SR's fixed length $\tau$ is measured in the sampled metric $S_N$, which can mis-estimate curvature along the step (the evidence below shows it over-estimates: the true length is shorter than intended). Reinforcement learning's trust-region methods check the realised KL divergence of a policy step on samples and shrink or grow the step (TRPO's backtracking, PPO's adaptive KL penalty). Here: build the direction from one half of the draws and set its length from the other half's metric, an unbiased estimate of $d^{\mathsf T}Sd$.
* **Lineage**: round 16 batch, cross-field translation from reinforcement learning (Analogy Map B3).
* **Source analogy**: TRPO (Schulman et al., ICML 2015) and PPO (Schulman et al., arXiv:1707.06347): natural policy gradient with a KL trust region, checked on samples because large policy steps leave the quadratic regime. Correspondence: policy → $|\psi_\theta|^2$, Fisher matrix → $S$, KL of a step → $d^{\mathsf T}Sd/2$. Gap: the check pays only if the sampled metric length is badly wrong.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at T0 (2026-11-18).
* **Kill rule**: T0: is the plain step's true metric length far enough from $\tau$ that correcting it could change the shortfall by more than step-length changes already did?
* **Evidence**: R51.18's critic replay (`tests/P5.1_r05_gen/results/storm_N2048_a0.1_sf_v2/lenratio.json`, 5×2, 2048 guided draws, about 920 unique, 40 steps; states from R51.18's STORM trajectory, energy rose on 82 of 120 steps, not plain-SR states): for the plain SR direction $\hat x_N$, $\hat x^{\mathsf T}S_{\rm exact}\hat x/\hat x^{\mathsf T}S_N\hat x$ median 0.67 (10–90%: 0.50–0.77), so the true length is 0.71–0.88 of $\tau$, a nearly uniform rescaling. R51.1: halving $\tau$ (arm D) moved the 120-step shortfall by 14%; R51.7 (running) tests step length directly.
* **Why this kills it**: the held-out correction is a ×1.1–1.4 lengthening that varies little from step to step, i.e. a change of $\tau$, whose effect is measured as small (R51.1) and is the subject of a running test (R51.7); it costs half the draws for the length estimate.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: large-step regimes (early training, $N\ll P$) where the quadratic KL fails; held-out direction checks (R51.6 killed those for energies).
* **Strongest case against the kill**: one size, one $N$; at larger $N/P$ ratios the ratio is nearer 1, so the case only weakens. **Translation repair** (property missing: steps large enough to leave the quadratic regime; here steps are tiny): replace the length check by PPO's clipped multi-epoch reuse of one batch: dismissed at T0, it is importance-reweighted reuse (R51.14, R51.17 killed; L70).
* **Checked by**: per-leaf critic round 16 (2026-11-18): upheld; objection 8 applied round 17.
* **Lesson**: proposed entry: in fixed-length SR at small steps the sampled metric length is a nearly uniform 0.7–0.9 rescaling, so trust-region length checks reduce to a step-length change.
