# R51.19 — zero-variance (Assaraf-Caffarel) control variate for the SR force (zvforce) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Add to each sample's force term $g=O_c r$ (log-derivative $O$ centred, times the regression residual $r$ of the local energy $E_{\rm loc}$) a multiple $c$ of $D_k=\partial_k E_{\rm loc}=(H\partial_k\psi)/\psi-E_{\rm loc}O_k$, whose mean under $|\psi|^2$ is exactly zero for a real Hamiltonian. At $c=1$ the $1/\psi^2$ near-node product $O\,E_{\rm loc}$ is replaced by $(H\partial\psi)/\psi\sim1/\psi$, so the heavy tail that R51.8's support-like growth might come from would be cut.
* **Lineage**: round 12 batch (released by R51.8 SUPPORT-LIKE), cross-field translation from continuum QMC forces; repaired as R51.25.
* **Source analogy**: zero-variance zero-bias force estimators (Assaraf & Caffarel, PRL 83, 4682, 1999; JCP 119, 10536, 2003; NQS forces: Linteau, Moroni, Carleo & Holzmann, arXiv:2603.14521): the bare Hellmann–Feynman force $\langle\partial_R H\rangle$ has infinite variance near nodes, and a zero-mean term built from $H$ cancels it. Correspondence: nuclear coordinate → network parameter. Gap (predicted): cost of $H\partial\psi$; (found): the SR force is already a covariance with $E_{\rm loc}-E$, i.e. already zero-variance at an eigenstate.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at T1 (2026-11-18).
* **Kill rule**: `tests/P5.1_vr_r12/RULE.md` (written first): $\rho=\kappa_X/\kappa_{\rm guided}$ (first-order SR step-error constant under guided draws, same state); eff $=\rho\times$ cost (3 for ZV); KILL if eff$_{6\times2}\ge0.8$ and (eff$_{5\times2}\ge0.8$ or $\rho_6/\rho_5>1$), at the exact-arm endpoints.
* **Evidence**: exact sums (`vr_t1.py`, 5×2 and 6×2 `che` ladders, start and step-120 exact endpoint; sanity $|\langle D\rangle_p|\le4\times10^{-17}$ rms): $\rho(c{=}1)$ = 96.5 / 833 (5×2 start / end), 26.8 / 98.6 (6×2 start / end); optimal $c^\*$ = 0.011 / 0.0012 / 0.046 / 0.010 with $\rho^\*$ = 0.988 / 0.9988 / 0.941 / 0.990. At $c=1$ the 1%-smallest-$p$ configurations' share of the variance falls from 16–31% to $\le1\%$: the tail is cut, but the bulk variance rises ~100–800×.
* **Why this kills it**: $\rho^\*\approx0.99$ at both sizes, so eff $\approx3\ge0.8$ everywhere. $(H\partial\psi)/\psi-E\,O$ does not vanish at an eigenstate ($H\partial\psi\ne E\partial\psi$), while the bare $O_c(E_{\rm loc}-E)$ does; near convergence the bare form is the zero-variance one.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: per-parameter (non-scalar) $c_k$; ZV terms built from $\partial E_{\rm loc}$ for the metric part; continuum systems, where the near-node tail is infinite rather than finite.
* **Strongest case against the kill**: a scalar $c$ is a weak control; but the optimum is $c^\*\approx0.01$, i.e. $D$ is nearly uncorrelated with $g$ in the bulk, and a per-component fit cannot use a direction that carries 100× more variance than it removes. Unresolved for $c_k$ only.
* **Gate 4 (explicit)**: the tail cut would scale with support; the bulk penalty is a constant ~100×; measured gain is none at either size.
* **Gate 0**: ZVZB is standard for nuclear forces; not found as an SR parameter-gradient control on lattices (searches 2026-11-18). G1–G3 PASS.
* **Checked by**: not yet reviewed.
* **Lesson**: proposed library entry (with R51.25): Hamiltonian-generated zero-mean controls cannot reduce the variance of an SR force, which is already a covariance with $E_{\rm loc}-E$.
