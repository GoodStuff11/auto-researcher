# R51.13 — SVRG anchor control variate for the SR step (svrg) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Evaluate each step's draws also at an earlier parameter set (the anchor), where the step direction is known, and subtract that importance-weighted, mean-zero copy from the current estimate. Consecutive SR steps are tiny (metric length 0.005), so per-sample terms at the two parameter sets should be strongly correlated and their difference low-variance; in recursive form (SARAH/STORM) the anchor is the previous step and no full pass is needed.
* **Lineage**: round 5 batch, cross-field translation from stochastic optimisation (Analogy Map B2); repaired as R51.18.
* **Source analogy**: SVRG (Johnson & Zhang, NeurIPS 2013), SARAH (Nguyen et al., ICML 2017), STORM (Cutkosky & Orabona, NeurIPS 2019): variance falls because the per-sample gradient at a nearby point is cheap to recompute on the same data. Correspondence: per-sample gradient → per-sample ridge-step term $A\,O_c r$; data → draws from $q_\theta$. Gap: draws are tied to $|\psi_\theta|^2$ (weights $p_{\tilde\theta}/p_\theta$), and the SR direction is a solve, not a sample mean. No SVRG/STORM use in VMC/NQS found (searches 2026-11-17).
* **Outcome**: KILLED at T1, for mixing weight $a$=0.1 (2026-11-17).
* **Kill rule**: `tests/P5.1_r05_gen/RULE_svrg.md` (one-step bound) then `RULE_storm.md`: "KILL if R_plat(STORM 2k) ≤ R_plat(plain 4k) (no better than spending the second evaluation on fresh draws)".
* **Evidence**: 5x2 start (`che`, $P$=1832), guided draws, exact sums. One-step bound with an exact anchor: remaining error fraction $\rho$ = 0.044 one step back, 0.45 five, 0.99 twenty (weight $\chi^2$ $1.7\times10^{-4}$, 0.074, $1.7\times10^4$) → MARGINAL. The 4x3 one-step arm never finished (hit the 600 s CPU limit, `logs/kq_S4x3_k1.txt`). Multi-step (`storm.py`, identity-checked against `ns_run.py` to 15 digits; 120 steps, $\tau$ 0.005, seeds as R51.8): STORM on the direction, $N$=2048, $a$=0.1: $R_{\rm plat}$ 0.211; plain guided $N$=4096 (equal CPU) 0.274; $N$=16,384 0.403.
* **Why this kills it**: STORM at $a$=0.1 and equal CPU lost 0.063 to plain draws. The recursion carries the previous direction's error forward through a ridge solve that is nonlinear in the samples (2048 draws hold about 920 unique configurations, fewer than $P$, so each solve's error lies in different poorly sampled directions), and at $a$=0.1 that carried error dominates.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: larger $a$ (only 0.1 tried), larger $N$, other sizes; recursion on linear estimators (R51.18).
* **Strongest case against the kill**: $a$=0.5. Linear theory with $\rho(1)$=0.044 predicts a step variance of about $0.24\sigma^2$ against $0.5\sigma^2$ for $2N$ fresh draws (a gain), because a larger $a$ carries less old error forward. Unresolved: the kill is scoped to $a$=0.1.
* **Checked by**: per-leaf critic (2026-11-17): kill upheld as scoped to $a$=0.1; objections 5, 9, 12 applied.
* **Lesson**: proposed library entry: direction-level variance-reduced recursion at $a$=0.1 carries solve errors forward in sampled SR; route-specific until $a$=0.5 or R51.18's rerun is scored.
