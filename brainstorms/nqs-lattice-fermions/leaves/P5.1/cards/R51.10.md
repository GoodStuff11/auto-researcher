# R51.10 — ridge-regression noise-to-signal ratio as sample-need predictor (kappa) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: The SR direction $x=(S+\lambda)^{-1}f$ is a ridge regression of the local energy on the log-derivatives. Learning theory gives its sample complexity in closed form: $N$ draws from $q$ leave an expected squared error $\mathrm{tr}[A C_q A S]/N$ in the metric norm, $A=(S+\lambda)^{-1}$, $C_q$ the importance-weighted covariance of the per-sample residual terms. Its ratio to $x^{\mathsf T}Sx$, $\kappa_q$, is exact from one state in minutes. If its size growth tracked the multi-step need's, a sampled $\kappa$ at 24 sites would replace ladders.
* **Lineage**: Operation B (round 4), cross-field translation from statistical learning theory (barrier B2 and the size question); scored from R51.8's arms.
* **Source analogy**: random-design ridge regression and kernel ridge regression, where the excess risk is (effective dimension $d_\lambda=\mathrm{tr}\,S(S+\lambda)^{-1}$) × (noise variance) / $N$ (Hsu, Kakade & Zhang, Found. Comput. Math. 14, 569, 2014; Bach, COLT 2013). Correspondence: features → $O$, response → $E_{\rm loc}$, design covariance → $S$, ridge → SR shift. Gap: the theory assumes light tails and one solve; here $O$ diverges at nodes and the step is iterated.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at T2 (2026-11-18), as a predictor of the sample need's size growth.
* **Kill rule**: `RULE_kappa.md` rev. 1 (before any R51.8 arm ran): "per size step, the kappa class is compared with R51.8's measured class: a disagreement at a step where R51.8 is not AMBIGUOUS KILLS R51.10 as a predictor; agreement at both steps PASSES it for use at 24 sites."
* **Evidence**: T1 (763903; exact sums at each trained start, guided $q$): $\kappa_{\rm guided}$ $1.08\times10^5$ (5×2), $7.61\times10^4$ (6×2), $1.99\times10^5$ (7×2), $1.24\times10^5$ (4×3); $k_1=0.70\le1.728$, $k_2=1.84\le2.744$ → KAPPA-POLY, predicting $N_{\rm needed}(6\times2)\approx22$k. T2 (769376, R51.8 rev. 2, one seed): $N_{\rm needed}$ 31,280 (5×2) → 118,600 (6×2), ×3.79, SUPPORT-LIKE (margin 1.08×).
* **Why this kills it**: R51.8's step is decided (SUPPORT-LIKE, not AMBIGUOUS) and its class differs from KAPPA-POLY. The need came out 5.4× above the $\kappa$-tracking value, so the one-state second moment does not carry the multi-step need's size trend.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: $\kappa$ as a within-size predictor of the need at fixed $V$; a tail-robust (quantile) version of the noise-to-signal ratio; the 6×2 → 7×2 step, unmeasured.
* **Translation repair** (round 16, dismissed at T0): restoring a stationary design by taking $\kappa$ along the trajectory fails on stored data: endpoint $\kappa_{\rm guided}$ 10.5k (5×2) vs 1.89M (6×2), ×180, against ×0.70 at the starts and ×3.79 measured (`vr_S*_end.json`); a weighting hitting 3.79 would be fitted after the result (L51).
* **Strongest case against the kill**: the 6×2 verdict rests on one seed per arm with a 1.08× margin and an exact reference still descending (z = −3.7). Answer: a 5×2-sized seed shift moves ln $N_{\rm needed}$ by 0.03–0.05 (margin 0.074), and at the threshold the need is still 5× the $\kappa$ value; an AMBIGUOUS second seed would void, not reverse, the kill.
* **Checked by**: per-leaf critic round 15 (2026-11-18): upheld; objections 4 (translation repair), 7 (stored endpoint kappa also disagrees, x180).
* **Lesson**: L67 (proposed extension): a one-state exact noise-to-signal ratio, like the one-step proxies, missed the multi-step sample need, here in the size trend (×0.70 against ×3.8).
