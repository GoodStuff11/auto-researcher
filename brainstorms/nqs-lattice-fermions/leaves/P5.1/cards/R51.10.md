# R51.10 — ridge-regression noise-to-signal ratio as sample-need predictor (kappa) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: The SR direction $x=(S+\lambda)^{-1}f$ is a ridge regression of the local energy on the log-derivatives. Learning theory gives its sample complexity in closed form: $N$ draws from $q$ leave an expected squared error $\mathrm{tr}[A C_q A S]/N$ in the metric norm, $A=(S+\lambda)^{-1}$, $C_q$ the importance-weighted covariance of the per-sample residual terms. Its ratio to $x^{\mathsf T}Sx$, $\kappa_q$, is computable exactly from one state at each exact-sum size in minutes. If its size growth tracked the multi-step need's, a sampled $\kappa$ at 24 sites would replace ladders.
* **Lineage**: Operation B (round 4), cross-field translation from statistical learning theory (barrier B2 and the size question); scored from R51.8's arms.
* **Source analogy**: random-design ridge regression and kernel ridge regression, where the excess risk is (effective dimension $d_\lambda=\mathrm{tr}\,S(S+\lambda)^{-1}$) × (noise variance) / $N$ (Hsu, Kakade & Zhang, Found. Comput. Math. 14, 569, 2014; Bach, COLT 2013). Correspondence: features → $O$, response → $E_{\rm loc}$, design covariance → $S$, ridge → SR shift. Gap: the theory assumes light tails and one solve; here $O$ diverges at nodes and the step is iterated.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at T2 (2026-11-18), as a predictor of the sample need's size growth.
* **Kill rule**: `RULE_kappa.md` rev. 1 (before any R51.8 arm ran): "per size step, the kappa class is compared with R51.8's measured class: a disagreement at a step where R51.8 is not AMBIGUOUS KILLS R51.10 as a predictor; agreement at both steps PASSES it for use at 24 sites."
* **Evidence**: T1 (763903; exact sums at each trained start, guided $q$): $\kappa_{\rm guided}$ $1.08\times10^5$ (5×2), $7.61\times10^4$ (6×2), $1.99\times10^5$ (7×2), $1.24\times10^5$ (4×3); $k_1=0.70\le1.728$, $k_2=1.84\le2.744$ → KAPPA-POLY, recorded as the prediction "R51.8 POLY at 5×2 → 6×2, $N_{\rm needed}(6\times2)\approx22$k if it tracked $\kappa$". T2 (769376, R51.8 rev. 2, one seed): $N_{\rm needed}$ 31,280 (5×2) → 118,600 (6×2), ×3.79, SUPPORT-LIKE (RESOLVABLE, 26.5 detrended sd_w; margin 1.08× over the 110,100 threshold).
* **Why this kills it**: R51.8's step is decided (SUPPORT-LIKE, not AMBIGUOUS) and its class differs from KAPPA-POLY. The need came out 5.4× above the $\kappa$-tracking value, so the one-state second moment does not carry the multi-step need's size trend.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: $\kappa$ evaluated along the trajectory (e.g. at the exact arm's step-60 state) rather than at the start; $\kappa$ as a within-size predictor of the need at fixed $V$; a tail-robust (quantile) version of the noise-to-signal ratio; the 6×2 → 7×2 step, unmeasured.
* **Strongest case against the kill**: the 6×2 verdict rests on one seed per arm with a 1.08× margin (the 65k rung is inside the second-seed clause) and an exact reference still descending (z = −3.7). Answer: a 5×2-sized seed shift moves ln $N_{\rm needed}$ by 0.03–0.05 against a 0.074 margin, and at the threshold the need is still 5× the $\kappa$ value; a still-descending reference pushes the need up. A second seed landing in AMBIGUOUS would void, not reverse, the kill.
