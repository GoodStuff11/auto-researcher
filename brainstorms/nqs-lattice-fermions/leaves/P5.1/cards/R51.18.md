# R51.18 — STORM recursion on the metric and force (storm-sf) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Repair R51.13 by applying STORM where SGD applies it, to sample means: keep recursive estimates of the metric $S$ and the force $f$, updated with the same draws at the current and previous parameters, and solve $(S_{\rm rec}+\lambda)^{-1}f_{\rm rec}$ each step, so $S$ and $f$ keep their shared-sample error cancellation (P4.1 R37) and the solve sees less noise.
* **Lineage**: translation repair of R51.13 (recursion on linear estimators instead of on the solved direction), round 5; first T1 withdrawn as a code artefact (per-leaf critic, objection 1), rerun under a pre-registered rule, killed (round 7).
* **Source analogy**: as R51.13 (STORM, Cutkosky & Orabona, NeurIPS 2019); the property restored is linearity in the samples. Gap: a recursed covariance need not stay positive semi-definite.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at T1 (2026-11-17), under this length rule.
* **Kill rule**: card, 2026-11-17, before rerun data: "`storm.py --mode sf` with one change: the metric length is taken in the fresh sample metric, $d=-\tau v/\sqrt{v^{\mathsf T}S_nv}$ … KILL if $R_{\rm plat}\le0.2740$ or the run diverges; PASS if $R_{\rm plat}\ge0.3383$ ($0.2740+0.5G$); MARGINAL otherwise."
* **Evidence**: 5x2 start (`che`, $P$=1832), guided draws, $N$=2048 (about 920 unique), $a$=0.1, $\tau$=0.005, 120 steps, seeds 9000+$n$ (`tests/P5.1_r05_gen/storm_v2.py`; $a$=1 reproduces plain SR to 15 digits). $R$ = fraction of the gap from the start energy to the exact ground energy $E_0=-6.5507$; $R_{\rm plat}$ = its mean over steps 101–120. Arm: $R_{\rm plat}=-3.47$; energy rose on 82 of 120 steps, median $|\Delta E|$ per step $2.3\times10^{-4}$ (plain-SR steps $1.5$–$4.5\times10^{-5}$). Foils: plain guided $N$=4096 (equal CPU) 0.274, $N$=16,384 0.403. Steps 1–40 (`logs/storm_sf_v2_diag40.txt`, `_lenratio40.txt`): $S_{\rm rec}+\lambda$ indefinite at every step 2–40 (median min eigenvalue $-0.037$); exact-to-sample length ratio $r=v^{\mathsf T}S_{\rm exact}v/v^{\mathsf T}S_nv$ median 1.13 (range 0.34–1.53), so the exact metric length was $\tau\sqrt r\le1.24\tau$ (fresh plain direction: $r$ median 0.67); exact first-order term downhill on 24 of 40 steps, yet 20 of those 24 rose (36 of 40 overall); median second-order part of $\Delta E$ $3.4\times10^{-4}$.
* **Why this kills it**: $R_{\rm plat}=-3.47\le0.274$ meets the KILL clause. The step was not oversized in the exact metric (at most 1.24$\tau$), so $v$ itself is bad: the solve through an indefinite $S_{\rm rec}$ (unique draws $<P$) loads $v$ onto directions where the energy curves upward steeply per unit metric length, so even downhill-pointing steps raise the energy by 5–15 times a plain step's typical change.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: other length or step rules (line search, curvature-aware); PSD-projected $S_{\rm rec}$; recursion on $f$ only; larger $a$; unique draws above $P$; other sizes.
* **Strongest case against the kill**: length normalisation in a rank-deficient $S_n$ could understate the true length; answered by the exact ratio (median 1.13, max 1.53). A PSD-projected $S_{\rm rec}$ or $a$=0.5: unresolved; the kill is scoped to this length rule, $a$=0.1, $N$=2048, 5x2.
* **Checked by**: per-leaf critic re-check (2026-11-17): upheld; objection 1 (scope narrowed); per-leaf critic round 15 (2026-11-18): narrowed kill upheld; objection 11 (summary's causal wording).
* **Lesson**: L70 (with R51.13): at $a$=0.1 and unique draws $<P$, STORM recursion with this length rule failed on the direction and on $S$ and $f$.
