# R51.18 — STORM recursion on the metric and force (storm-sf) (LIVE)
* **The idea**: Repair R51.13 by applying the STORM recursion where SGD applies it, to plain sample means: keep recursive estimates of the metric $S$ and the force $f$, each updated with the same draws evaluated at the current and previous parameters, and solve $(S_{\rm rec}+\lambda)^{-1}f_{\rm rec}$ each step, so $S$ and $f$ keep their shared-sample error cancellation (P4.1 R37) and the solve sees lower-noise inputs.
* **Lineage**: translation repair of R51.13 (replace the component: recursion on linear estimators instead of on the solved direction), round 5; killed at T1 in round 5, reopened to LIVE by the per-leaf critic (2026-11-17, objection 1).
* **Source analogy**: as R51.13 (STORM, Cutkosky & Orabona, NeurIPS 2019); the property restored is linearity of the recursed quantity in the samples. Remaining gap: a recursed covariance is a signed combination of sample covariances and need not stay positive semi-definite.
* **Outcome**: LIVE (T1 rerun owed, about 2 CPU-min interactive; 2026-11-17).
* **First T1 (round 5, withdrawn as a code artefact)**: 5x2 start (`che`, $P$=1832), $N$=2048 guided draws, $a$=0.1 (`storm.py --mode sf`, which took the metric length in $S_{\rm rec}$, as the `RULE_storm.md` addendum said): diverged within 10 steps, NaN sampling distribution; plain guided $N$=4096 reaches $R_{\rm plat}$ 0.274. Objection: $S_{\rm rec}=S_n+(1-a)(S_{\rm rec}-S'_{n-1})$ is indefinite whenever the two sample sets disagree in a poorly sampled direction (2048 draws hold about 920 unique configurations, fewer than $P$), but the blow-up came from the step length $\tau/\sqrt{\max(v^{\mathsf T}S_{\rm rec}v,10^{-300})}$, which reaches $\tau/10^{-150}$ when $v^{\mathsf T}S_{\rm rec}v\le0$. Indefiniteness is structural; the divergence is not.
* **Pre-registered rerun rule (2026-11-17, before any rerun data)**: `storm.py --mode sf` with one change: the metric length is taken in the fresh sample metric, $d=-\tau v/\sqrt{v^{\mathsf T}S_nv}$, while $v=(S_{\rm rec}+\lambda)^{-1}f_{\rm rec}$ is unchanged. Same arm (5x2 start, guided draws, $N$=2048, $a$=0.1, $\tau$=0.005, 120 steps, seeds 9000+$n$), same foils (plain guided $N$=4096, $R_{\rm plat}$ 0.2740; $N$=16,384, 0.4026; $G$=0.1286). KILL if $R_{\rm plat}\le0.2740$ or the run diverges (NaN or non-finite energy); PASS if $R_{\rm plat}\ge0.3383$ ($0.2740+0.5G$) → T2 at 4x3 against R51.1's arms at equal CPU; MARGINAL otherwise.
* **Sharpest Gap**: with an indefinite $S_{\rm rec}$ the solve can amplify noise along negative directions even with a sane length; a PSD projection would add a bias of the size of the negative part.
* **Gate 4 claim**: two evaluations of each draw (current and previous parameters), so about twice plain SR's per-sample cost at any size; the gain must exceed that factor.
