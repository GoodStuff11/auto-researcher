# R51.18 — STORM recursion on the metric and force (storm-sf) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Repair R51.13 by applying the STORM recursion where SGD applies it, to plain sample means: keep recursive estimates of the metric $S$ and the force $f$, each updated with the same draws evaluated at the current and previous parameters, and solve $(S_{\rm rec}+\lambda)^{-1}f_{\rm rec}$ each step, so $S$ and $f$ keep their shared-sample error cancellation (P4.1 R37) and the solve sees lower-noise inputs.
* **Lineage**: translation repair of R51.13 (replace the component: recursion on linear estimators instead of on the solved direction), round 5.
* **Source analogy**: as R51.13 (STORM, Cutkosky & Orabona, NeurIPS 2019); the property restored is linearity of the recursed quantity in the samples. Remaining gap: a recursed covariance matrix is a signed combination of sample covariances and need not stay positive semi-definite.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at T1 (2026-11-17).
* **Kill rule**: `tests/P5.1_r05_gen/RULE_storm.md` addendum (written before the run): same arm and thresholds as R51.13, "KILL if R_plat(STORM 2k) ≤ R_plat(plain 4k)".
* **Evidence**: 5x2 start, $N$=2048, $a$=0.1, guided draws (`storm.py --mode sf`): the run diverged within its first 10 steps; $S_{\rm rec}$ became indefinite, the metric length $v^{\mathsf T}S_{\rm rec}v$ went negative, the step blew up and the next sampling distribution was NaN. Plain guided $N$=4096 reaches $R_{\rm plat}$ 0.274 from the same start.
* **Why this kills it**: a diverged run is below any foil; the cause is structural: $S_t+(1-a)(S_{\rm rec}-S'_{t-1})$ subtracts a sample covariance at $N<P$, whose null space differs from step to step, so negative eigenvalues appear as soon as the two sample sets disagree in a poorly sampled direction.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: projecting $S_{\rm rec}$ onto the positive cone or recursing only on $f$ with a fresh $S$ (but replacing $f$ alone broke the $S$–$f$ cancellation in P4.1 R37); $N\gg P$, where sample covariances are full rank.
* **Strongest case against the kill**: a PSD projection is a standard fix; unresolved, but it would add a bias of the size of the negative part, which at $N<P$ is the noise the recursion was meant to remove.
* **Checked by**: round 5 worker (2026-11-17); per-leaf critic owed.
* **Lesson**: with R51.13, proposed library entry (variance-reduced recursions do not transfer to sampled SR as tried).
