# R51.18 — STORM recursion on the metric and force (storm-sf) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Repair R51.13 by applying the STORM recursion where SGD applies it, to plain sample means: keep recursive estimates of the metric $S$ and the force $f$, each updated with the same draws evaluated at the current and previous parameters, and solve $(S_{\rm rec}+\lambda)^{-1}f_{\rm rec}$ each step, so $S$ and $f$ keep their shared-sample error cancellation (P4.1 R37) and the solve sees lower-noise inputs.
* **Lineage**: translation repair of R51.13 (replace the component: recursion on linear estimators instead of on the solved direction), round 5; first T1 withdrawn as a code artefact by the per-leaf critic (objection 1), rerun under a pre-registered rule and killed (round 7).
* **Source analogy**: as R51.13 (STORM, Cutkosky & Orabona, NeurIPS 2019); the property restored is linearity of the recursed quantity in the samples. Gap: a recursed covariance is a signed combination of sample covariances and need not stay positive semi-definite.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at T1 (2026-11-17).
* **Kill rule**: card, 2026-11-17, before any rerun data: "`storm.py --mode sf` with one change: the metric length is taken in the fresh sample metric, $d=-\tau v/\sqrt{v^{\mathsf T}S_nv}$ … KILL if $R_{\rm plat}\le0.2740$ or the run diverges; PASS if $R_{\rm plat}\ge0.3383$ ($0.2740+0.5G$); MARGINAL otherwise."
* **Evidence**: 5x2 start (`che`, $P$=1832 parameters), guided draws, $N$=2048, $a$=0.1, $\tau$=0.005, 120 steps, seeds 9000+$n$ (`tests/P5.1_r05_gen/storm_v2.py`; $a$=1 identity check reproduces the stored plain $N$=4096 arm's first three energies to 15 digits). $R$ = fraction of the exact-sum reference gain reached, $R_{\rm plat}$ its mean over steps 101–120. Arm: $R_{\rm plat}=-3.47$ (energy rose every step from step 2; finite, no NaN; 1.9 CPU-min). Foils: plain guided $N$=4096 (equal CPU) 0.274, $N$=16,384 0.403. Diagnostic (40 steps, same trajectory): $S_{\rm rec}+\lambda$ had a negative eigenvalue at every step 2–40 (median $-0.037$, minimum $-0.146$, against $\lambda=10^{-3}$); the cosine between $v$ and the fresh force $f_n$ had median 0.003, and $v$ pointed uphill ($f_n\cdot v<0$) on 31% of steps.
* **Why this kills it**: $R_{\rm plat}$ = $-3.47$ ≤ 0.274 meets the KILL clause by a wide margin. With the step length now sane, the failure is the direction: once $S_{\rm rec}$ is indefinite (from step 2 on, because 2048 draws hold about 920 unique configurations, fewer than $P$, and the two sample sets disagree in poorly sampled directions), the solve amplifies the negative-eigenvalue directions, so $v$ is nearly orthogonal to the force and the run ascends.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: a PSD-projected or truncated $S_{\rm rec}$ (adds a bias of the size of the negative part), recursion on $f$ only with a fresh $S_n$, larger $a$ (only 0.1 tried), $N$ with unique draws above $P$, other sizes.
* **Strongest case against the kill**: the failure may be specific to $a$=0.1 at $N<P$ unique draws, where any recursed covariance is indefinite; at $a$=0.5 the carried part halves. Unresolved; the kill is scoped to $a$=0.1, $N$=2048 at 5x2.
* **Checked by**: worker score against the pre-registered rule (2026-11-17); per-leaf critic review owed.
* **Lesson**: proposed library entry (with R51.13): at $a$=0.1 and fewer unique draws than parameters, STORM-type recursion in sampled SR fails whether applied to the direction (carried solve error) or to $S$ and $f$ (indefinite metric); otherwise route-specific.
