### The question and the argument
A fermionic neural quantum state (NQS) is trained by variational Monte Carlo: each step draws $N$ configurations, estimates the energy gradient (force $f$) and the metric $S$ (covariance of the log-derivatives), and moves along $S^{-1}f$ (SR; MinSR solves the same step in sample space, identical when $N\ge P$, the parameter count). On the 12-site state exact-sum optimisation reaches 75% of the gap to the exact energy (L61). "Guided" means a flattened distribution, a published sampler (arXiv:2507.05352); the Question's SR draws from $|\psi|^2$.

*Fixed step, sampling-limited (R51.1).* With the step fixed, the 120-step shortfall fell as $N^{-0.87}$ and barely responded to halving the step, so noise slows the path; at equal total samples fewer per step won within 120 steps, yet only $4N$ and $16N$ reached 90%.

*The size question (R51.8, R51.10; R51.9 killed).* Polynomial cost is decided by how the needed $N$ grows with size against each size's noise-free run; one-step proxies missed it by 2–4× (R51.9). The fixed step bounces near the minimum, so the statistic was re-registered (rev. 2) before any 12-site ladder data; under it the 10-site need is resolvable (31,280 samples per step) and the 12-site ladder is running. Ridge-regression theory ($\kappa$) puts 10 → 12 → 14 sites ($\times0.70$, $\times1.84$) on the polynomial side, a prediction recorded before the ladders.

*Alternatives.* Sampling $\propto|\psi|$ has 4.7× lower $\kappa$ than guided (R51.11), but its reweighting variance grows with size (effective samples per draw 45% → 31% from 10 to 12 sites, Gate 4 FAIL at T0 on two volumes); a growing samples-per-step schedule (R51.15); recursion on $S$ and $f$ (R51.18) failed with a length that was correct in the exact metric: the recursed metric is indefinite and the solved direction raises the energy. Direction recursion at $a$=0.1 (R51.13), reuse and reshuffling (R51.14, R51.17) and sketched leverage (R51.12) lost. Median-of-means (R51.5) reopened; step decay (R51.7) shelved, its trigger now met at 10 sites.

*Strongest objections.* (1) The only Born-SR comparison is at 10 sites (one Born seed, guided 9.3–9.4× fewer); R51.16 tests guided against Born SR at 12 sites with $N\ge P$; MinSR's $N<P$ regime is untested. (2) 10 to 14 sites can exclude support-like growth, not prove a polynomial.

*Limits.* Exact i.i.d. draws (no Markov chains), one seed per arm, one network family, no 24-site evidence.
