* **Answer**: Not known. ### The question and the argument
A fermionic neural quantum state (NQS) is trained by variational Monte Carlo: each step draws $N$ configurations, estimates the energy gradient (force $f$) and the metric $S$ (covariance of the log-derivatives), and moves along $S^{-1}f$ (SR; MinSR solves the same step in sample space, identical when $N\ge P$, the parameter count). On the 12-site state exact-sum optimisation reaches 75% of the gap to the exact energy (L61). "Guided" means a flattened distribution, a published sampler (arXiv:2507.05352); the Question's SR draws from $|\psi|^2$.

*Fixed step, sampling-limited (R51.1).* With the step fixed, the 120-step shortfall fell as $N^{-0.87}$ and barely responded to halving the step, so noise slows the path; at equal total samples fewer per step won within 120 steps, yet only $4N$ and $16N$ reached 90%.

*The size question (R51.8, R51.10; R51.9 killed).* Polynomial cost is decided by how the needed $N$ grows with size against each size's noise-free run; one-step proxies missed it by 2–4× (R51.9). The statistic was re-registered (rev. 2) for the fixed step's bounce before any 12-site data; the 10-site need is 31,280 samples per step and the 12-site need 118,600: ×3.79, above the occupied-support growth ×3.52 and far above $V^3$ (×1.73), so this step is support-like (one seed; margin 1.08×). Ridge-regression theory ($\kappa$, R51.10) had predicted the polynomial side ($\kappa$ ×0.70, about 22k) before the ladders and is killed as a predictor.

*Alternatives.* Sampling $\propto|\psi|$ has 4.7× lower $\kappa$ than guided (R51.11) and at 12 sites needed 0.43× guided's total samples (Q-FEWER), but its reweighting variance grows with size (effective samples per draw 45% → 31% → 22% at 10, 12, 14 sites; shape unresolved). A growing samples-per-step schedule (R51.15) was killed (its gain was censoring); recursion on $S$ and $f$ (R51.18) failed because the recursed metric is indefinite. Direction recursion (R51.13), reuse, reshuffling and sketched leverage (R51.14, R51.17, R51.12) lost. Median-of-means (R51.5) was killed (block steps too often uphill); step decay (R51.7) is shelved, its trigger met at 10 sites.

*Strongest objections.* (1) Born-SR comparisons have one Born seed per arm (10 sites: guided 9.3–9.4× fewer; 12 sites: ≥ 3.3× on 4×3, ≥ 4.3× on the 6×2 ladder); MinSR's $N<P$ regime is untested. (2) 10–14 sites can exclude support-like growth, not prove a polynomial; conversely the support-like 10 → 12 step is one seed over a size factor 1.2, where ×3.8 is also $V^{7.3}$, so it shows growth far above $V^3$ at this step, not an exponential.

*Limits.* Exact i.i.d. draws, one seed per arm, one network family, nothing at 24 sites.
