# R2.7 — Conical-intersection Berry-phase tracking in the $(t',h)$ plane (round-2 Route 2.7) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Track the geometric (Berry) phase of the variational state around loops in the plane of $t'$ and a field $h$ to locate the degeneracy seam, and decide from the side of the seam the target lies on, using topology instead of energy differences.
* **Lineage**: Operation B.1, round 2. Revisited as R3.3 with certified loops.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at G2 (2026-11-11).
* **Kill rule**: Gate 2, "does a cheap baseline or the foil reach the same metric?"
* **Evidence**: The phase locates the degeneracy seam, not the sign of the diabatic gap at the target, and a biased $2\times2$ model has the same topology with the seam moved by $\Delta b$.
* **Why this kills it**: The biased seam sits where the biased energies cross, the foil's boundary, so the phase carries no information beyond energy comparison.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: phases of exact or certified states (R3.3).
* **Strongest case against the kill**: The phase is robust to small perturbations, so a small bias moves the seam only slightly. Answer: "slightly" is $\Delta b/\lvert\nabla\delta\rvert$, the same shift as the foil; not raised by a critic.
