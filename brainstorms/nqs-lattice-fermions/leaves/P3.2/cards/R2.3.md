# R2.3 — Two-sided conjugate-field response (round-2 Route 2.3) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Train each branch under $\hat H-h\hat c$ for fields $h$ of both signs and locate the field $h_c$ where the lower variational envelope switches order; the side of $h=0$ on which $h_c$ falls would decide the order, using the response to the field rather than raw energies.
* **Lineage**: Operation B.2 (active probing), round 2.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at G2 (2026-11-11).
* **Kill rule**: Gate 2, "does a cheap baseline or the foil reach the same metric?"
* **Evidence**: The envelope crossing sits at $h_c\approx\delta/\Delta c$ plus the same differential bias divided by $\Delta c$ (the character difference), and a basin-trapped ansatz answers either field sign with its own order, as in P3.1's pinning-field kill.
* **Why this kills it**: Its decision boundary is the energy comparison's boundary $\delta+\Delta b=0$, so it can only match the foil, and it can be faked by basin trapping.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: field response of an unbiased (exact) solver, which is standard.
* **Strongest case against the kill**: Large fields could pull branches out of their basins and remove the trap. Answer: then the field-free point is reached by continuation, which is R2.4; not raised by a critic.
