* **Why this kills it**: Its decision boundary is again $\delta+\Delta b=0$.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: stability probes of an ansatz whose bias is known to be small.
* **Strongest case against the kill**: Basin width, not depth, controls stability, and width could correlate with the true order. Answer: no argument or measurement links width to $\delta$; not raised by a critic.
