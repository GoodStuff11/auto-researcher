# R2.6 — Bayesian evidence comparison of the two branches (round-2 Route 2.6) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Treat the two branches as models and compare their Bayesian evidence built from energies and variances, so that the decision carries a posterior probability rather than an ad hoc threshold.
* **Lineage**: Operation B.3, cross-field translation from Bayesian model selection, round 2.
* **Source analogy**: In statistics, Bayes factors compare models through the likelihood of the data, penalising complexity automatically; it works because a likelihood exists. Gap: a variational energy is not a likelihood of any data.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at G1 (2026-11-11).
* **Kill rule**: Gate 1, "is it consistent with the structure of the problem?" (no likelihood exists).
* **Evidence**: Any evidence built from $(E,\sigma^2)$ needs an assumed error model, which is the calibrated extrapolation of R1.1 under another name.
* **Why this kills it**: Without a likelihood the posterior is defined only by the error model chosen, so the route adds nothing over R1.1.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: Bayesian treatment of the sampling noise of each energy, which is ordinary error analysis.
* **Strongest case against the kill**: A hierarchical model over many runs and seeds could learn the bias distribution. Answer: the bias of a branch is not random over seeds (round 4 showed systematic pull toward one order); not raised by a critic.

* **Lesson**: route-specific — a relabelling of R1.1.
