# R5.1 — Eckart-certified direct decision, ECD (round-5 Route 5.1) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Train one projected NQS from an unbiased cold start and commit to the sign of its order character only when $\lvert c\rvert$ exceeds a bound on $\lvert c(\psi)-c(\psi_0)\rvert$ derived from the Eckart fidelity bound $1-F\le\eta=\varepsilon/\Delta_1$ ($\varepsilon$ the energy error, $F$ the fidelity with the ground state). The decision would then be certified, never wrong when it commits.
* **Lineage**: Operation A on the failed component of R3.5, round 5 (2026-11-14). Mutated into R5.2.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at T1 (2026-11-14), after a Gate 0 failure as a standalone method.
* **Kill rule**: requested PASS clause, quoted: "$\mathrm{DEC}\ge0.5$ on the inner targets".
* **Evidence**: Exact precheck (`tests/P3.2_biased_ensemble_r5/precheck.py`, 11 s) with the exact $\varepsilon$ and $\Delta_1$ and the best of four control branches from job 451852: to commit at $\lvert c\rvert=\lvert c_0\rvert$ the rigorous bound needs $\eta\le0.03$–$0.08$, i.e. $\varepsilon\le4.6\times10^{-5}t$ at $d=\pm0.0002$; measured $\eta=0.19$–$0.55$ at width 8. Certified commits on the inner targets: 0 of 12 at width 4 and at width 8 for both the rigorous and the variance bound; 6 of 12 at width 8 only for a two-level bound that needs exact $c_1$ and $O_{01}$; 0 wrong commits throughout.
* **Why this kills it**: Even with exact inputs, an upper bound on what any sampled certificate can do, the inner decision rate is 0, so the PASS clause fails before any training; width 6 lies between the measured widths, so its array was not run.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: the abstain rule as a component where $\eta\lesssim0.05$ (at $\lvert d\rvert\ge0.002$ at width 8); certificates with much more accurate states.
* **Strongest case against the kill**: The certificate is sound (0 wrong commits), which no other rule guaranteed. Answer: soundness without power is what the naive rule with sampling abstention also gives; not raised by a critic.
