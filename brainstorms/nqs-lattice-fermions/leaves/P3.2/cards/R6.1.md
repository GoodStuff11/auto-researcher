# R6.1 — Excited-state Ritz with one penalty-trained state, K1 (round-6 ESR) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Train the ground state $\psi$ and one penalty-orthogonal in-irrep excited state $\phi_1$ by sequential penalty VMC (Choo et al., PRL 121, 167204, 2018; P3.1's round-7 machinery), diagonalise $\hat H$ in their span, and decide from the order character of the lowest Ritz vector. The Ritz rotation was claimed to be non-energy information that escapes the $2\times2$ lemma.
* **Lineage**: transfer of P3.1's ESR0 mechanism (round 6, 2026-11-14), triggered by the round-5 Saturation Diagnostic's "non-energy information" lever.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at G2 (2026-11-14), confirmed empirically by job 619080.
* **Kill rule**: Gate 2, "does a cheap baseline or the foil reach the same metric?"; the kill check's pre-registered clause, quoted: "KILL-CONFIRMED if, on every instance in $N$, both K1 and ESR0 are wrong or abstain, and the K1 decision equals the lower-diagonal prediction on every instance".
* **Evidence**: Lemma: for a real symmetric $2\times2$ matrix the lowest eigenvector has weight at least $\tfrac12$ on the basis vector with the lower diagonal, whatever the coupling $w$, so the K1 decision is the energy rule between $\psi$ and $\phi_1$. Off-diagonal information is bounded by the diabatic coupling $W\approx5\times10^{-4}t$ against measured $\Delta b=3.6$–$5.4\times10^{-3}t$ on two of three width-4 seeds. Job 619080 (84 CPU-h, width 4, 9 instances): K1 equals the lower-diagonal prediction on 9 of 9 and is right on 4 of 9; every error is a truth-$B$ instance with $\psi$ in $A$. The pre-registered verdict was INCONCLUSIVE because the naive rule never committed wrong ($\lvert N\rvert=0$) and the positive control missed 4 of 9.
* **Why this kills it**: The lemma makes K1 the energy rule between two trained states with a differential bias at least that of the order-seeded pair, so it can only match the foil; the measurement agrees on every instance.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: Ritz with exact excited states where the trained ground state is wrong (the positive control fixes those), and lattices where $W$ is not small compared with $\Delta b$.
* **Strongest case against the kill**: The pre-registered verdict was INCONCLUSIVE, not KILL-CONFIRMED. Answer: both triggering clauses were design artefacts (the naive rule abstained instead of erring; the positive control was a perfect partner only when $\psi$ was wrong), and the lemma held on 9 of 9; the critic accepted the kill.
