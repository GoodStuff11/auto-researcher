# R6.4 — Symmetric block-Lanczos step on both trained states, BL (round-6 alternative) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Ritz in $\mathrm{span}\{\psi,(\hat H-E_\psi)\psi,\phi_1,(\hat H-E_1)\phi_1\}$: one Lanczos step applied to both low states. The larger-variance branch gains more energy, so the differential bias shrinks, a symmetric energy (capacity) lever rather than non-energy information.
* **Lineage**: alternative named by the round-6 lemma analysis; reported in-sample in job 619080. Its sampled forms were tested as R6.5 and R6.6.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at G0 (2026-11-14) as a route; adopted as recommended practice and later scoped.
* **Kill rule**: Gate 0, "is it known, and is the adoption barrier fixed?"
* **Evidence**: VMC plus Lanczos steps is Sorella (PRB 64, 024512, 2001), used in mVMC competing-order studies (Ido, Ohgoe & Imada, arXiv:1712.05490) and applied to NQS (Chen, Hendry, Weinberg & Feiguin, arXiv:2206.14307). In-sample in job 619080, with exact matrices, BL was right on 9 of 9 width-4 instances.
* **Why this kills it**: It is existing practice; its success is an energy-lever result, not a new mechanism.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: BL as practice in exact arithmetic at ED size; whether a sampled BL keeps the gain (tested as R6.6).
* **Strongest case against the kill**: Practice has never used it as a decision rule at a same-irrep crossing, and 9 of 9 is a strong result. Answer: in-sample and exact only; the sampled versions lose (R6.5, R6.6); not raised by a critic.
