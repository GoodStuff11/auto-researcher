# R2.4 — Thermodynamic integration from a field-dominated anchor (round-2 Route 2.4) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Anchor each diabat at a large field $\pm h_{\max}$ where the order is unambiguous, then obtain $E_X(0)=E(\pm h_{\max})+\int\langle\hat c\rangle_X\,dh$ by integrating the measured order parameter along each branch, avoiding optimisation at $h=0$.
* **Lineage**: Operation A.4 (continuation in $h$) from R2.3, round 2.
* **Source analogy**: not a declared cross-field translation; it mirrors Frenkel–Ladd integration from an exact reference (Analogy Map B3).
* **Outcome**: KILLED at G1 (2026-11-11).
* **Kill rule**: Gate 1, "does it violate an asymptotic or accuracy bound?"
* **Evidence**: Variational energies have errors second order in the wave-function error, while observables such as $\langle\hat c\rangle$ have first-order errors, and the anchors are themselves variational.
* **Why this kills it**: The integral replaces a second-order-accurate quantity by an accumulation of first-order errors, so the result is less accurate than the energy comparison it was meant to improve.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: integration with Hellmann–Feynman-exact integrands (exact states).
* **Strongest case against the kill**: Errors in $\langle\hat c\rangle$ might be common to both diabats and cancel in the difference. Answer: no evidence either way; not raised by a critic.
* **Checked by**: round-2 per-leaf critic (2026-11-11), no objection recorded.
* **Lesson**: route-specific — a standard accuracy-order argument.
