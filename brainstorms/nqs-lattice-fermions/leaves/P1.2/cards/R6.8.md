# R6.8 — One-shot projected-vote sign read-out (Route 6.8) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Apply the vote once, without training, to the symmetry-projected state, where its lift (precision over base rate) was 129, and read the sign off the result, using the projection to make the vote reliable.
* **Lineage**: Operation B.2 refill after R5.1's VOTE-INERT result (Round 6, 2026-11-16); its cheap local form became R8.1.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at G0 (2026-11-16).
* **Kill rule**: Gate 0: is it new, and does it fix the known method's adoption barrier?
* **Evidence**: a sign set by one exact local application of $H$ to a projected state is the sign of a one-step power or Lanczos state (Becca et al., arXiv:1412.2656; Loehr & Clark, arXiv:2510.26906).
* **Why this kills it**: known, and not size-consistent at a fixed number of steps (R2.3's kill).
* **What the kill does not rule out**: votes inside a size-consistent product (R2.2's family).
* **Strongest case against the kill**: the selective (thresholded) version is not the plain Lanczos step. Not raised by a critic; the threshold version was later rejected as R8.5 on the same ground.
* **Checked by**: round-6 per-leaf critic (2026-11-16, objection 9).
* **Lesson**: route-specific — a Lanczos-step relabel.
