# R5.3 — FRD on a Slater–Jastrow base against an NNBF control (Route 5.3) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Keep the round-3 design, FRD factors on a Slater–Jastrow reference, and compare it with an NNBF control on the 4×4 instance, testing whether the vote factors alone can do what backflow does by deforming orbitals.
* **Lineage**: the round-3 test design of R2.2, re-examined in Round 5 (2026-11-12); replaced by R5.1.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at CRITIC (2026-11-12).
* **Kill rule**: round-3 critic item (a): the contrast must isolate the sign layer, so FRD must sit on the same base as its control.
* **Evidence**: the design compares "FRD on no backflow" with "backflow", two changes at once.
* **Why this kills it**: a FAIL would say nothing about FRD and a PASS is implausible by construction, so the test cannot change a decision.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: FRD on an NNBF base (R5.1).
* **Strongest case against the kill**: none; the confound is structural.
* **Checked by**: round-3 per-leaf critic (2026-11-12, item a).
* **Lesson**: route-specific — a confounded design, already a standard fair-comparison rule.
