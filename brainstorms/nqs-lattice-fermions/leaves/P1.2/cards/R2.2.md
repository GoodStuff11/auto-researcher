# R2.2 — FRD-K, factorized-ratio dressing on hop edges of a Slater–Jastrow reference (Routes 2.2 and 3.1) (MERGED)
* **The idea**: Multiply a Slater–Jastrow reference $\Phi$ by $\prod_e[1+\alpha_e(\mathbf n)g(R_e)]$ over all hop edges, with $g(R)=R/\sqrt{1+R^2}$ and $\alpha_e$ from a network. A factor turns negative, flipping $\Phi$'s sign at $\mathbf n$, only when the Hamiltonian neighbour across $\Phi$'s node outvotes $\Phi$, so the eigen-equation vote is built into the ansatz at roughly determinant cost.
* **Lineage**: Operation A.1 from R2.1, R2.3 and R2.4 (Round 2, 2026-11-11); re-audited in Round 3 as Route 3.1 (size-consistency claim withdrawn; sign capability only in the nonlinear regime $\alpha_eg<-1$); its 4×3-torus test was cancelled after pilot 410216 returned NO-TARGET (the projected control left $\Delta E_{\mathrm{sign}}\le10^{-5}$); ported onto an NNBF base as R5.1, and its old design was killed as R5.3.
* **Outcome**: MERGED into R5.1.
