# R5.1 — NNBF×FRD-K, hop-edge vote factors on a neural backflow base (Routes 5.1, ported from R2.2) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Put FRD-K's factors $\prod_e[1+\alpha_e(\mathbf n)g(R_e)]$ on a width-4 NNBF base $\Phi$, with exact neighbour ratios from the base's own backflow, so that the base moves nodes by deforming orbitals and the factors add per-configuration sign flips wherever a hole-hop neighbour across the base's node outvotes it.
* **Lineage**: ported from R2.2 by Operation A.2 on round-3 critic item (a) (Round 5, 2026-11-12); its cost wall seeded R5.4 (FRD-F); its VOTE-INERT read-out seeded R6.3 (AO-F) and R6.7.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at T2 (2026-11-16).
* **Kill rule**: pre-registered T2-a rule (`t2_readout.py`): with $v=(\Delta E_{\mathrm{sign}}[\lvert F\rvert]-\Delta E_{\mathrm{sign}}^{K})/\mathrm{gap}$, where $\lvert F\rvert$ removes every flip, "VOTE-INERT if $v<0.25$, VOTE-CARRIES if $v\ge0.5$"; and the T3 final rule "PASS iff 5-seed $\rho_{\mathrm{sign}}\le0.7$, at least 4 of 5 seeds below the control median, $\rho_{\mathrm{tot}}\le1.0$, $\kappa\le10$, MC_OK; else FAIL".
* **Evidence**: on the projected 4×4 instance, 3 seeds (job 451916), FRD-K (1719 parameters) against NNBF width 6 (1734): $\rho_{\mathrm{sign}}=0.84$ ($\Delta E_{\mathrm{sign}}$ median 0.0101 against 0.0120), $\rho_{\mathrm{tot}}=0.92$, 3 of 3 paired wins, AMBIGUOUS. Removing every flip from the trained state moved $\Delta E_{\mathrm{sign}}$ from 0.009751 to 0.009757 ($v=0.003$, job 697584); flip weight at most $3.7\times10^{-3}$ in all six runs. With all ten control runs stored, the 5-seed $\rho_{\mathrm{sign}}\ge0.782>0.7$ whatever seeds 44 and 55 gave. Cost: $(1+E_{\mathrm{act}})$ times NNBF's passes per local energy (522 against 32 at $L=4$, 1867 against 62 at $L=6$).
* **Why this kills it**: the route's novelty is a sign-capable vote, and the trained state does not use it ($v=0.003$), so its 16% gain is an amplitude effect; the decisive rule could no longer pass; and its cost overhead grows as $\mathcal O(V)$, so a matched-parameter win cannot become a matched-compute win (G4 cost fails too).
* **What the kill does not rule out**: votes that are used (tested as R6.7 and R8.1), and the amplitude effect itself (tested as R6.3).
* **Strongest case against the kill**: VOTE-INERT reads one SR-trained state, not the architecture; a different optimiser might use the flips (round-6 critic objection 2). Answered only partly: the projected vote applied without training was usable on that state ($z=-1.33$, job 698178), but every route that tried to train or cheapen it died (R6.7, R6.7F, R8.1).
* **Checked by**: round-6 per-leaf critic (2026-11-16, objections 1, 2 and 8).
* **Lesson**: L38, L50 (`library.md`)
