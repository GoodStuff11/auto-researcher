# R6.6 — Node-targeted sampling (Route 6.6) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Bias the Monte Carlo sampler toward configurations near the nodes, where the sign gradients live, and reweight, so training concentrates on the configurations whose signs need to change.
* **Lineage**: Operation B.2, active probing (Round 6, 2026-11-16).
* **Outcome**: KILLED at T0 (2026-11-16).
* **Kill rule**: T0: can a sampler change what SR converges to?
* **Evidence**: every test in this leaf uses exact-sum forces, so no sampler enters; in sampled SR the fixed point does not depend on the sampling distribution, only the variance does (P1.3's sign-flip-aware Metropolis kill).
* **Why this kills it**: it can change noise, not the trained nodes.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: variance reduction near nodes as an efficiency aid.
* **Strongest case against the kill**: with finite samples and a finite step budget, the sampler changes which minimum is reached. Not raised by a critic; unresolved, but P1.3 found no basins to steer between.
* **Checked by**: round-6 per-leaf critic (2026-11-16, objection 9).
