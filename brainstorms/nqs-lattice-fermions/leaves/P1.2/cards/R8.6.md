# R8.6 — Matrix-product-state sign factor along a snake path (unnumbered in the Round 7 refill) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Multiply the foil by a sign factor computed by a matrix product state along a snake path through the lattice, a well-understood, trainable, non-local sign correlator whose cost is set by its bond dimension.
* **Lineage**: rejected at T0 during the Round 7 refill (2026-11-16).
* **Outcome**: KILLED at G3 (2026-11-16).
* **Kill rule**: Gate 3: does it work only by removing the Hard Core (2D)?
* **Evidence**: a snake-path matrix product state has bounded entanglement across cuts; on a 2D lattice the bond dimension needed grows exponentially with the width (area law).
* **Why this kills it**: it is efficient only in quasi-1D, which the Hard Core excludes.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: 2D tensor-network sign factors (PEPS), at much higher cost.
* **Strongest case against the kill**: a sign factor may need far less entanglement than the full state. Not raised by a critic; unresolved.
* **Checked by**: not reviewed.
* **Lesson**: route-specific — a known area-law limit.
