# R2.4 — Brick layer of two-site fermionic gates, the lattice Baeriswyl projector (Route 2.4) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Apply a brick layer of configuration-dependent two-site gates $\prod_be^{\theta_b(\mathbf n)\hat K_b}$ to the reference, the lattice analogue of the Baeriswyl kinetic projector $e^{-\lambda\hat T}$, so kinetic correlations move the nodes the way a short imaginary-time evolution would.
* **Lineage**: Operation B.3, cross-field translation from fermionic tensor-network gate layers and the Baeriswyl projector (Round 2, 2026-11-11); its kill fed R2.2 ("keep only the first-order, single-bond content and factorize it").
* **Source analogy**: In tensor networks and in Baeriswyl or Gutzwiller–Baeriswyl variational states (Hetényi, Tanatar & Martelo, PRB 93, 174518 (2016), arXiv:1605.02698), a layer of local gates or a kinetic projector $e^{-\lambda\hat T}$ acts on a reference. Correspondence: gate layer ↔ sign-changing dressing; auxiliary branch configurations ↔ sampled intermediate states. It works for bosons because the branch sum is positive; the gap is that for interacting fermionic gates the branch sum carries signs.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at G1 (2026-11-11).
* **Kill rule**: Gate 1: is the amplitude computable in polynomial time without a new sign problem?
* **Evidence**: with interacting gates the exact amplitude is a signed sum over $\mathcal O(2^{N_b})$ branches ($N_b$ bonds); sampling the branches (the Hetényi or off-diagonal-wave-function route) reintroduces a sign problem; with quadratic gates the layer is an orbital rotation and adds nothing. The uniform-$\lambda$ form is also published (Gate 0).
* **Why this kills it**: either exponential cost, a new sign problem, or no new expressivity.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: a factorized, first-order version (R2.2), and gate layers on bosonic or stoquastic problems.
* **Strongest case against the kill**: a shallow layer with few non-commuting gates could be summed exactly for small depth. Not raised by a critic; unresolved, but one layer already has $2^{N_b}$ branches.
* **Checked by**: not reviewed.
* **Lesson**: route-specific — the branch-sum sign problem of fermionic gate layers is textbook, not a finding of this run.
