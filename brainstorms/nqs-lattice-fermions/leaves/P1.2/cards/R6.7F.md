# R6.7F — Frozen-frame vote on the trained foil (Route 6.7F) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Make the vote cheap by computing it from frozen ratios, one inverse per amplitude, and flip the trained matched-width foil's sign wherever its own hop vote fires, with no gate and no added parameters, so the comparison with the foil is exact.
* **Lineage**: Operation A on R6.7's cost kill (Round 7, 2026-11-16).
* **Outcome**: KILLED at T1 (2026-11-16).
* **Kill rule**: pre-registered in `t1_vote67.py`: with $q$ the median ratio of flipped to unflipped $\Delta E_{\mathrm{sign}}$, "KILL iff $q_{\mathrm{frozen}}\ge1.0$ at 2 or more counted sizes including the largest".
* **Evidence**: on unprojected ladders, $q_{\mathrm{frozen}}=5.22$ (uncounted $V=8$), 1.75 ($V=10$), 1.31 ($V=12$); the exact vote, reported only, gave $q_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.81$, 0.90, 0.92.
* **Why this kills it**: the frozen vote raises the foil's sign error by 30% to 75% at the counted sizes.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: the same frozen ratios as learned features (the round-7 critic found them informative, AUC 0.93), and the exact vote (R8.1).
* **Strongest case against the kill**: $V=10$ is near-degenerate (gap $0.0041t$). Raised by the round-7 critic for the DRF kills; here the $V=12$ result alone is above 1.
* **Checked by**: round-7 per-leaf critic (2026-11-16, objection 8 and the restated anti-pattern).
