# R2.1 — Product of plaquette sign factors times the foil (Route 2.1) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Answer the round-1 critic's first objection by replacing one threshold with a product of independent plaquette-local sign or phase factors $\prod_pc_p(\mathbf n_p)$ times the Slater–Jastrow reference, trained by SR from the energy, so independent local sign repairs can multiply.
* **Lineage**: Operation A.1 from R4 after its G0 kill (Round 2, 2026-11-11); its failure fed R2.3 and R2.2.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at G0 (2026-11-11).
* **Kill rule**: Gate 0: is it new, and does it fix the reason it is not standard?
* **Evidence**: it is CPS times Slater (Changlani et al., PRB 80, 245116; Neuscamman et al., PRB 84, 205132 (2011)) and, with complex factors, a complex restricted Boltzmann machine times a pair product (Nomura et al., PRB 96, 205152).
* **Why this kills it**: local occupation factors cannot know where the reference's nodes are, which is why CPS signs have not replaced backflow; this route adds nothing against that.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: local factors whose inputs include the reference's amplitude ratios (R2.2).
* **Strongest case against the kill**: CPS was never tested as a sign fixer on doped $t'\ne0$ clusters. Not raised by a critic; unresolved.
