# R3.0 — Local-energy Jastrow, the first-order limit of FRD (Route 3.0) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Re-gate FRD against its first-order expansion: for small ratios $\prod_e[1+\alpha_eg(R_e)]\approx\exp(\sum_e\alpha_eR_e)$, which with uniform $\alpha_e=-\lambda$ is $\Phi e^{-\lambda E^{\mathrm{kin}}_{\mathrm{loc}}}$, a Jastrow factor of the reference's own kinetic local energy, checking whether this regime alone could carry the sign gain.
* **Lineage**: Round-3 re-gating of R2.2 after the round-2 critic's size-consistency objection (2026-11-11); its kill moved FRD's claim to the nonlinear regime and later seeded AO-F (R6.3).
* **Outcome**: KILLED at G2 (2026-11-11).
* **Kill rule**: Gate 2: can it change the sign, or is it in the foil's sign class?
* **Evidence**: $\exp(\cdot)>0$ at every $\mathbf n$, so $\mathrm{sgn}\,\psi=\mathrm{sgn}\,\Phi$; it also differs from the Baeriswyl state at order $\lambda^2$ by the local variance of the hopping.
* **Why this kills it**: P1.1 found the sign to be the binding error, and a positive factor cannot change any sign.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: positive factors that act through a symmetry projection or by freeing backflow capacity (tested and killed as R6.3 and R7.1).
* **Strongest case against the kill**: under $P_\chi$ a positive factor can move projected nodes. Raised by the worker in Round 6 (R6.3) and tested there; it lost to width.
