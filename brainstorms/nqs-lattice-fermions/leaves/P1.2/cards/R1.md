# R1 — Jordan–Wigner sign times a learned sign network (Route 1) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Drop the determinant and write the sign as the fixed Jordan–Wigner ordering sign of the site-ordered basis times a learned sign or phase network over occupations (optionally times a fixed determinant), hoping that a network learns the residual nodes directly, without any orbital anchor.
* **Lineage**: initial batch (Round 1, 2026-11-10); not mutated.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at G0 (2026-11-10).
* **Kill rule**: Gate 0: is it new, and if known, does it fix the reason it is not standard practice?
* **Evidence**: The construction is published as a determinant-free exchange sign plus a feed-forward network (Inui, Kato & Motome, arXiv:2108.08631), as a complex restricted Boltzmann machine times a pair product (Nomura et al., PRB 96, 205152 (2017)), and in second-quantized chemistry (Choo et al., Nat. Commun. 11, 2368 (2020)).
* **Why this kills it**: Known methods are admitted only with a mechanism against their adoption barrier. Here the barrier is that the 2D Jordan–Wigner string is non-local and generic sign networks generalise poorly from samples (Szabó & Castelnovo, PRR 2, 033075); the route proposes nothing against either.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: a sign network with an input or inductive bias that does generalise, such as the ratio-fed carriers of R9.0 and R9.1.
* **Strongest case against the kill**: newer sign networks may generalise better than the cited ones, so "generalises poorly" may be dated. Not raised by a critic; unresolved, but every later sign carrier in this leaf that did have a structured input still tied width.
* **Checked by**: not reviewed (the round-1 critic's items 5 and 6 concern R4 only).
