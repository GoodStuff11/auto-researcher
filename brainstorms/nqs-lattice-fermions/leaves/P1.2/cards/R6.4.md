# R6.4 — Complex detour, real part of a complex backflow (Route 6.4) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Train a complex backflow $\psi_c=A+iB$ and read out $\mathrm{Re}\,\psi_c=A$, so a sign can change by rotating the phase instead of driving $\lvert\psi_c\rvert$ through zero, which would remove the zero-crossing barrier.
* **Lineage**: Operation B.1, real-to-complex frame (Round 6, 2026-11-16); repaired as R9.1 (one shared amplitude).
* **Outcome**: KILLED at T0 (2026-11-16).
* **Kill rule**: T0 lemma at Gate 2: does the objective couple the parts so the readout gains?
* **Evidence**: for real symmetric $H$, $E[\psi_c]=(\langle A\rvert H\lvert A\rangle+\langle B\rvert H\lvert B\rangle)/(\langle A\vert A\rangle+\langle B\vert B\rangle)$, a weighted mean of $E[A]$ and $E[B]$.
* **Why this kills it**: the energy never couples $A$ and $B$ except through shared parameters, so a node crossing in either part still costs that part's own barrier.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: a phase on one shared amplitude, $\psi e^{i\varphi}$, where the energy does couple the parts (R9.1).
* **Strongest case against the kill**: shared parameters do couple $A$ and $B$. Not raised by a critic; true but uncontrolled, and the critic sharpened the lemma's wording (objection 9).
