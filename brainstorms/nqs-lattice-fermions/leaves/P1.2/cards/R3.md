# R3 — Slave-fermion holon determinant times the Marshall sign (Route 3) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Use the slave-fermion sign $s=\kappa(\mathbf n)M(\mathbf n)\,\mathrm{sgn}\det X_{[\text{holes}]}$, with $\kappa$ a hole-position gauge, $M=(-1)^{N_\downarrow(A)}$ the Marshall sign ($N_\downarrow(A)$ down spins on sublattice $A$) and $X$ a small holon orbital matrix. It costs $\mathcal O(N_h^3)$ for $N_h$ holes and is not an electron mean field, so it might avoid the electron anchor.
* **Lineage**: initial batch (Round 1, 2026-11-10); revived with holon backflow as R6.5.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at G2 (2026-11-10).
* **Kill rule**: Gate 2: does the foil (optimized Slater or BCS sign) already match or beat it?
* **Evidence**: On a $3\times4$ open lattice with 2 holes and $t'=0$, the minimum of $W[Mf]$ over **all** hole-only functions $f$ is 0.0001 at $J/t=100$, 0.0100 at $J/t=10$, 0.195 at $J/t=2$ and 0.342 at $J/t=0.4$; the foil's optimized Slater sign has $W=0.073$ at $J/t=0.4$ (`holon_check`, `kill_evidence_output.txt`).
* **Why this kills it**: the bound covers every holon determinant with any hole-only backflow, so at the physical $J/t=0.4$ the whole family is wrong on about 4.7 times more weight than the foil. The cause is the phase string, which ties the sign to joint spin and hole positions. It is good only as $J/t\to\infty$, where the Marshall sign already cures the sign problem (Gate 3 also fails).
* **What the kill does not rule out**: anchors that use spin-dependent hole functions (R6.5) or a slave-particle sign as a feature rather than as the sign.
* **Strongest case against the kill**: one 12-site cluster at $t'=0$; at $t'\ne0$ the comparison could change. Not raised by a critic; unresolved, but $t'\ne0$ adds frustration that the Marshall sign does not capture, so it should only worsen the anchor.

* **Lesson**: route-specific — the anchor quality of one slave-particle construction.
