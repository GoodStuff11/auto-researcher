# R6.3 — AO-F, sign-inert ratio Jastrow on frozen ratios (Route 6.3) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Since FRD-K's gain was amplitude-borne, keep only that part: $\psi=\Phi_{\mathrm{NNBF}}\exp(\sum_e\alpha_e(\mathbf n)g(R^F_e))$, a positive factor that cannot flip signs itself but may move the projected nodes through image amplitudes in $\sum_g\chi_g\psi(g\mathbf n)$ or free backflow capacity for the nodes.
* **Lineage**: Operation A.3 on R3.0's kill, after R5.1's VOTE-INERT read-out (Round 6, 2026-11-16); sibling AO-L is R7.1.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at T1 (2026-11-16).
* **Kill rule**: pre-registered in `t1_ao.py`: with $r^\ast=\max(r_J,r_W)$, $r_J$ the ratio to a matched NNBF times neural Jastrow and $r_W$ to matched width, "KILL if $r^\ast\ge0.9$ at 2 or more counted sizes, including the largest".
* **Evidence**: unprojected ladders, 300 exact SR steps, seeds 11 and 22; the exact-ratio arm `aoK` (the most informative family member) against width: $r_W=2.04$, 0.95, 1.19 at $V=8,10,12$; against the neural Jastrow it won ($r_J=0.56$, 0.67, 0.48). Any positive factor lost to width ($r_{JW}$ 1.4 to 3.6).
* **Why this kills it**: the ratio features carry real information (they beat a plain Jastrow), but at matched parameters a positive factor loses to backflow width at every size, so the "frees capacity" mechanism is refuted; the projection mechanism is a fixed-$\lvert G\rvert$ finite-size effect (G4).
* **What the kill does not rule out**: the same features entering the orbitals (R7.2), and projected training at larger $V$.
* **Strongest case against the kill**: 2 seeds with large spreads (width's $\Delta E_{\mathrm{sign}}$ 0.011 and 0.026 at $V=12$), unprojected, 300 steps. Raised by the round-6 critic as caveats; unresolved but the losses are by factors of 1.2 to 2.
* **Checked by**: round-6 per-leaf critic (2026-11-16, who ran the test; objections 4 and 5).
