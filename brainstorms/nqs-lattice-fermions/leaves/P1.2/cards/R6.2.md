# R6.2 — FRD-H, vote factors on every off-diagonal move (Route 6.2) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Fix FRD-X's narrow vote by putting FRD factors on all off-diagonal moves, hops and exchanges together (132 edges on the 4×4 instance), so each configuration's vote is cast by the full eigen-equation rather than by its minority exchange part.
* **Lineage**: Operation A on R5.2's failure (Round 6, 2026-11-16).
* **Outcome**: KILLED at T1 (2026-11-16).
* **Kill rule**: pre-registered R2 in `t1_vote.py`: kill FRD-H if the recall of its summed vote is at most hop-only recall at $V=8$ and $V=10$.
* **Evidence**: on UHF anchors of ladders with $V=8,10,12$, recall was 0.095 against 0.221 and 0.221 against 0.255 at the first two sizes. On the trained 4×4 base, however, recall was 0.380 against 0.347.
* **Why this kills it**: the rule fired as written; the critic kept the kill on two other grounds: it carries 1.6 times R5.1's $\mathcal O(V)$ cost wall, and its vote would be as unused as R5.1's.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: all-edge ratios as continuous features (R7.4).
* **Strongest case against the kill**: the recall rule is contradicted at $V=16$ on a trained base (round-6 critic objection 6). Answered by re-basing on Gate 4 cost and VOTE-INERT.
* **Checked by**: round-6 per-leaf critic (2026-11-16, objection 6).
* **Lesson**: route-specific — inherits R5.1's cost and inert-vote failures.
