# R7.6 — Transcorrelated frame for NNBF (Route 7.6) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Train an NNBF $\Phi$ on the similarity-transformed Hamiltonian $\tilde H=e^{-J}He^{J}$ with a pre-optimised diagonal $J$, so that the backflow only has to describe what the correlator leaves, hoping its nodes then come out better.
* **Lineage**: Operation B.3, cross-field translation from transcorrelated FCIQMC (round-6 critic refill, 2026-11-16).
* **Source analogy**: In quantum chemistry and lattice FCIQMC, the transcorrelated method (Dobrautz, Luo & Alavi, PRB 99, 075119 (2019)) absorbs a Jastrow factor into a non-Hermitian $\tilde H$, which compacts the ground state in the configuration basis. Correspondence: Jastrow ↔ positive correlator; compact CI vector ↔ easier backflow target. It worked because projective methods benefit from compactness; the gap is that a variational ansatz represents $e^J\Phi$ either way.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at G2 (2026-11-16).
* **Kill rule**: Gate 2: does a cheap baseline already reach the same family?
* **Evidence**: on a lattice with diagonal $J$, the represented state $e^J\Phi$ is exactly the NNBF-times-Jastrow family (`nnbfJ`), which lost to width at T1 (R6.3's test, $r_{JW}$ 1.4 to 3.6).
* **Why this kills it**: it is the losing baseline with a non-variational objective; the objective question is P1.3's scope.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: transcorrelation with non-diagonal (off-diagonal) correlators.
* **Strongest case against the kill**: a different objective can reach different minima. Not raised by a critic; unresolved, and P1.3 found no basins.
* **Checked by**: not reviewed.
* **Lesson**: route-specific — reduces to a killed baseline.
