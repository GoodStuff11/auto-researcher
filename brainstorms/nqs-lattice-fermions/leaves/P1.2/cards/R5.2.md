# R5.2 — NNBF×FRD-X, exchange-edge vote factors on a neural backflow base (Routes 5.2, ported from R2.5) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: The same vote factors as R5.1, but on spin-exchange edges (48 on the 4×4 instance), where in $t$–$J$ the exchange is part of $H$ itself; the hope was that exchange votes, which stay within one hole configuration, carry the spin sign structure more reliably than hole hops.
* **Lineage**: ported from R2.5 (Round 5, 2026-11-12); its failure seeded R6.2 (FRD-H, all edges).
* **Outcome**: KILLED at T3 (2026-11-12, job 451916).
* **Kill rule**: pre-registered: "FAIL if $\rho_{\mathrm{sign}}\ge0.9$, or $\rho_{\mathrm{tot}}>1.1$, or $\kappa>10$, or MC_FAIL", with $\kappa$ the ratio of local-energy kurtosis to the control's.
* **Evidence**: 3 seeds on the projected 4×4 instance against NNBF width 6 (1656 against 1734 parameters): $\rho_{\mathrm{sign}}=1.29$, $\rho_{\mathrm{tot}}=1.24$, $\kappa=11.9$.
* **Why this kills it**: it has more sign error and more total error than the matched foil, and its local-energy tails are 12 times heavier, which would make sampled training noisier still.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: exchange-ratio information used as continuous features (R7.3, later killed for other reasons).
* **Strongest case against the kill**: on the toys exchange votes were *more* informative than hop votes (lift 3.2 against 2.0 at $V=12$), so the failure is trainability, not information. Raised by the Round-6 worker; it confirms rather than overturns the kill.
