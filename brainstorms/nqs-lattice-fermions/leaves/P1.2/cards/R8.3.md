# R8.3 — DRF-WS, warm-started second backflow pass (Route 8.3) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Remove DRF's cold-start and narrow-pass confounds: train pass 1 to convergence as the foil, then add a second pass as wide as the first, starting exactly at the identity ($W_4=0$), so its frozen-ratio input is exactly the feature set shown to locate the foil's remaining wrong signs.
* **Lineage**: Operation A on R7.2's optimisation sub-component (Round 7 refill, 2026-11-16).
* **Outcome**: KILLED at T1 (2026-11-16, Round 8).
* **Kill rule**: pre-registered by the round-7 critic before any code: "KILL if $r\ge0.9$ at 2 or more counted sizes including the largest", with $r=\Delta E_{\mathrm{sign}}[\mathrm{ws}]/\Delta E_{\mathrm{sign}}[\mathrm{wide}]$.
* **Evidence**: gap-asserted sizes, both arms from the same trained foil, 300 more SR steps, seeds 11 and 22: at 6×2 $5+5$ ($V=12$) `ws` 0.00858 against `wide` 0.00866 ($r=0.991$); at 4×3 $5+5$ 0.03766 against 0.03801 ($r=0.991$); seed ranges overlap. $\Delta E_{\mathrm{tot}}$ fell faster for `ws` (0.052 against 0.065 at 6×2). On the same foil the features locate its wrong signs with held-out AUC 0.93 against 0.65 for occupations plus the foil's hidden units.
* **Why this kills it**: with every confound removed, the second pass trains, lowers the total energy, and leaves the sign error equal to width's: the new capacity goes to amplitudes.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: projected training (later tested as R9.2), horizons beyond 300 steps, $V>12$, compiled-cost comparisons. On these toys about 90% of the unprojected sign error is symmetry contamination (projected foil 0.0045 and 0.0017 against 0.050 and 0.011).
* **Strongest case against the kill**: a 300-step, unprojected, $V\le12$ statement (cross-node critic Majors 3 and 4). Accepted as a scope limit; the projected re-check could not be run (NO-TARGET-P), and the projected narrow-foil test R9.2 did not overturn it.
