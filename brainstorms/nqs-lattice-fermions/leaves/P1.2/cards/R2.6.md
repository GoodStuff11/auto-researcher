# R2.6 — Backflow in a learned rotated orbital frame (Route 2.6) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Sample and run backflow in a learned single-particle basis $\hat c^\dagger_k=\sum_iU_{ik}\hat c^\dagger_i$ (for example momentum or natural orbitals), so the nodes are defined in a basis where the sign structure may be simpler.
* **Lineage**: Operation B.1, frame change (Round 2, 2026-11-11); not mutated.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at G0 (2026-11-11).
* **Kill rule**: Gate 0: is it new, and does it fix the reason it is not standard?
* **Evidence**: orbital rotations co-optimized with NQS (Robledo Moreno et al., arXiv:2302.11588) and basis optimization for hidden-fermion states (Gauvin-Ndiaye et al., PRL 134, 076502 (2025)) are published; a rotated Hubbard interaction has $\mathcal O(N_\uparrow N_\downarrow V^2)$ connected elements per sample.
* **Why this kills it**: known, and its adoption wall (the dense local energy of a rotated two-body term, leaf P2.2) is not addressed.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: rotations restricted to keep the interaction sparse.
* **Strongest case against the kill**: a partial rotation might keep most sparsity. Not raised by a critic; unresolved.
* **Checked by**: not reviewed.
* **Lesson**: route-specific — inherits P2.2's cost wall.
