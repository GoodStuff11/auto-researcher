# R7.2 — DRF-K, determinant-ratio-feature backflow on hop edges (Route 7.2) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Move the vote's information into the orbitals: a first NNBF pass gives orbitals whose inverse yields frozen hop ratios; these feed a second backflow pass that outputs the final orbitals. The nodes then move by continuous orbital deformation, avoiding both flips (which SR declined) and positive factors (which lose to width).
* **Lineage**: Operation B.3, cross-field translation from continuum iterative backflow (round-6 critic refill, 2026-11-16); siblings R7.3 and R7.4; warm-start and tied repairs are R8.3 and R8.4; exact-ratio form R8.2.
* **Source analogy**: In continuum quantum Monte Carlo, iterative backflow (Taddei et al., PRB 91, 115106 (2015); network form, Ruggeri et al., arXiv:1711.01993) feeds a first set of backflow coordinates into a second transformation. Correspondence: electron coordinates ↔ occupations; second-iteration input ↔ Green's-function ratios of the first pass. It worked there because the second iteration sees correlated positions the first could not express; the gap is that on a lattice the input is a discrete occupation vector, and generic depth already saturates (Loehr & Clark).
* **Outcome**: KILLED at T1 (2026-11-16).
* **Kill rule**: pre-registered T1b in `t1b_drf.py`: "KILL if $r_W\ge0.9$ at 2 or more counted sizes, including the largest".
* **Evidence**: on unprojected ladders (UHF start, 300 exact SR steps; 3 seeds for this arm), $r_W=1.52$ at $V=10$ and 2.14 at $V=12$ ($\Delta E_{\mathrm{sign}}$ 0.0238 against 0.0156, and 0.0200 against 0.0093). Its untrained premise had passed: ratio features added 0.03 to 0.13 of held-out AUC over occupations, rising with $V$.
* **Why this kills it**: the trained second pass has about twice the sign error of the same parameters spent on width at both counted sizes.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: a warm-started, wider second pass (R8.3), tied re-entry (R8.4), exact ratios (R8.2). The round-7 critic noted the design gave pass 2 only 2 hidden units and a moving input.
* **Strongest case against the kill**: cold start, narrow pass 2, and a near-degenerate counted size ($V=10$, gap $0.0041t$) where seed 33 alone decided it (round-7 critic Majors 2 and 3). Answered by R8.3, which removed all three confounds and still tied.
