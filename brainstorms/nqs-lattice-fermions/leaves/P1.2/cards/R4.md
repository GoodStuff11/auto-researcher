# R4 — Residual Walsh sign layer on the foil determinant (Route 4) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Keep the foil's determinant sign and multiply it by a learned sign $c(\mathbf n)=\mathrm{sgn}(b+\sum_Sw_S\chi_S(\mathbf n))$ built from Walsh characters $\chi_S$ of degree at most 3 inside $2\times2$ plaquettes, smoothed to a phase in training. The hypothesis was that the foil's residual sign error is local and low-degree, so a cheap local layer fixes it without moving orbitals.
* **Lineage**: initial batch (Round 1, 2026-11-10), the only Round-1 survivor of the gates; after its kill it mutated into R2.1, then R2.3 and R2.2.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at G0 (2026-11-11, by the round-1 critic).
* **Kill rule**: Gate 0 after the critic: a local sign-capable diagonal correlator times a Slater or BCS reference is the published correlator-product-state (CPS) times Slater ansatz, so it must fix CPS's adoption barrier; and the pre-registered test must not depend on oracle labels.
* **Evidence**: The smoke preview ($3\times4$ open, $t'=-0.3$, layer fitted by logistic regression to the exact residual sign on 1500 samples) lowered $W$ from 0.073 to 0.072 and raised the fixed-node energy: $M_{\mathrm{local}}=1.03$ against $M_{\mathrm{null}}=1.15$ for a non-local control (both above the FAIL threshold 0.9). The critic added two structural objections: one threshold over an extensive sum cannot express a product of independent local corrections, and the layer is CPS times Slater (Changlani et al., PRB 80, 245116 (2009)).
* **Why this kills it**: it is a known ansatz with no fix for its known barrier (local occupation factors cannot see where the reference's nodes are), its single threshold cannot represent independent local repairs, and its only evidence used labels from $\psi_0$, which VMC does not have.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: a product of local factors conditioned on the reference's own amplitudes (R2.2), and residual layers trained by the energy (R8.3).
* **Strongest case against the kill**: the decisive $4\times4$ run was never made; the preview was a reduced 12-site run. Not raised by a critic; the structural objections hold at any size, and the preview pointed the same way.
* **Checked by**: round-1 per-leaf critic (2026-11-10, items 5 and 6).
