# R3.3 — Projected-reference FRD (Route 3.3) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Answer the round-2 critic's symmetry objection by building every FRD ratio from the symmetry-projected reference, $R_e=c_e(P_\chi\Phi)(\mathbf m_e)/(P_\chi\Phi)(\mathbf n)$, so the votes are cast by a state that already has the right point-group symmetry.
* **Lineage**: Operation A.2 on the round-2 critic's symmetry objection to R2.2 (Round 3, 2026-11-11).
* **Outcome**: KILLED at G1 (2026-11-11).
* **Kill rule**: Gate 1: does it keep the locality and asymptotics the route needs?
* **Evidence**: each ratio needs $\lvert G\rvert$ determinants ($\lvert G\rvert$ the group order), and every factor then depends on the whole group orbit of $\mathbf n$, so a translated image of a distant region enters every factor.
* **Why this kills it**: it loses even the approximate multiplicative separability that made FRD's correction local, while projecting the whole FRD state, $P_SP_\chi\psi$, reaches the symmetry goal at the same cost.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: projecting the full state (what the round-3 and round-5 tests did).
* **Strongest case against the kill**: on small clusters locality matters little. Not raised by a critic; true, but the route then has nothing over full projection.
* **Checked by**: not reviewed.
* **Lesson**: route-specific — dominated by projecting the whole state.
