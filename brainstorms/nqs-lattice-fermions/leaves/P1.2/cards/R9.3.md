# R9.3 — Fixed-node objective for the nodes, self-healing DMC on the lattice (Route 9.3) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Optimise the network's nodes directly against a fixed-node objective (the energy of the best state with those signs), as self-healing diffusion Monte Carlo does in the continuum, so node updates follow the quantity that matters rather than the variational energy.
* **Lineage**: Operation B.3, cross-field translation from self-healing DMC, built from reopening ingredient I3 (Reopening review, 2026-11-16).
* **Source analogy**: In continuum quantum Monte Carlo, self-healing DMC (Reboredo, Hood & Kent, arXiv:0808.3417) iteratively improves nodes from the fixed-node projected walker distribution. Correspondence: nodal surface ↔ sign of the network; fixed-node DMC ↔ lattice fixed-node Green's-function Monte Carlo. It works there as a systematic improvement in a large basis; the gap is that a 2026 assessment (arXiv:2609.01301) finds it converges to a wrong node in any finite basis, and the lattice form needs FN-GFMC with the network as guide.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at G0 (2026-11-16).
* **Kill rule**: Gate 0: is it known, and does it fix the known adoption barrier?
* **Evidence**: self-healing DMC is published, its finite-basis failure is documented, and its lattice form is the P1.1 Gate 0 kill (fixed-node GFMC with an NQS guide; Loehr & Clark, arXiv:2510.26906).
* **Why this kills it**: known, with a documented convergence failure, and no new mechanism.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: using $\Delta E_{\mathrm{sign}}$-like fixed-sign energies as diagnostics (as this leaf does).
* **Strongest case against the kill**: the lattice may behave differently from continuum finite bases. Not raised by a critic; unresolved.
* **Checked by**: critic sign-off of the Leaf Status Summary (2026-11-16).
* **Lesson**: route-specific — a known method with a documented failure.
