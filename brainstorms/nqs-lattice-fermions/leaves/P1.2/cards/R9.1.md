# R9.1 — RPC, ratio-fed phase carrier on the frozen foil (Route 9.1) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Escape the zero crossing entirely: $\psi=\psi_F(\mathbf n)e^{i\varphi(\mathbf n)}$ with the trained foil $\psi_F$ frozen and $\varphi=\pi\sum_v[\tanh(\phi_vW_k+b_k)\cdot a+c]$ from a weight-shared kernel (57 parameters) on per-site occupations and ratio features; a sign changes by rotation, and the energy couples real and imaginary parts through the shared amplitude.
* **Lineage**: Operation B.1 (real frame to phase frame), from reopening ingredients I3 and U3 (Reopening review, 2026-11-16); translation repair of R6.4 (one shared amplitude instead of two independent parts).
* **Source analogy**: Signal-processing phase retrieval relaxes a lost discrete phase to a continuous variable (Candès, Strohmer & Voroninski, arXiv:1109.4499). Correspondence: lost phase ↔ sign of $\psi$; known magnitudes ↔ frozen foil amplitudes. It works there because random measurements make the relaxed problem well posed; the gap is that here the objective is a fixed local Hamiltonian, so whether a descending phase direction lies in the carrier's span is not guaranteed.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at T1 (2026-11-16).
* **Kill rule**: the t1c rule against the matched foil `wid`, written before the run; plus, after the RPC kill and before its own run, the phase-family premise: FAMILY-KILL if the curvature $\lambda(S)=\min_{g\in S}q(g)/\mathrm{var}_{\psi^2}(g)$ is positive for the candidate spans at the counted sizes, where $q(g)=-\frac{1}{2N}\sum_{\mathbf n\mathbf m}\psi_{\mathbf n}\psi_{\mathbf m}H_{\mathbf n\mathbf m}(g_{\mathbf n}-g_{\mathbf m})^2$ is the second-order energy change.
* **Evidence**: in every run the kernel returned to $\lvert\cos\varphi\rvert\ge0.996$ with zero flipped weight, so RPC equals the foil ($r=1.30$ at 6×2, 1.16 at 4×3). An unrestricted per-configuration phase did descend (flipping 1.7% of the weight at 4×3, $r_{\mathrm{free}}=0.77$, but 1.17 at 6×2). Curvature: $\lambda$ for linear ratio spans 0.35 and 0.31, quadratic 0.26 and 0.22, occupations 0.37 and 0.33, against $-1.76$ and $-1.70$ for the unrestricted direction (6×2, 4×3).
* **Why this kills it**: the foil is a saddle in phase space, but every extensive feature span tried has positive curvature, so no carrier on these features can leave $\varphi=0$; the obstruction is the non-locality of the needed correction in feature space, not the zero crossing.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: spans with $\lambda(S)<0$, for example multi-hop features coupling ratios across neighbouring edges (the restated missing ingredient).
* **Strongest case against the kill**: only per-site linear and quadratic spans were screened. Not raised by a critic; accepted as the scope of the kill, and it defines the leaf's open ledger row.
