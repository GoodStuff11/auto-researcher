# R8.1 — 6.7L, exact vote on a finite-receptive-field backflow (Route 8.1) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Fix R6.7's cost by giving pass 1 a finite receptive field of radius $r$: a hop then changes only nearby orbital rows, so each exact neighbour ratio is a rank-$\mathcal O(r^d)$ determinant-lemma update, and the exact vote costs a constant factor; the factors are initialized to flip where the exact vote fires and trained by SR.
* **Lineage**: Operation A on R6.7's G4 kill (Round 7 refill, 2026-11-16).
* **Outcome**: KILLED at G0 (2026-11-16, by the round-7 critic).
* **Kill rule**: Gate 0 (relabel) and Gate 4 (finite-size gain): at initialization, is it a known method, and does its gain survive growing $V$?
* **Evidence**: at initialization the ansatz is the trained base times a flip wherever the exact local-energy vote fires, which is R6.8's one-step Lanczos sign; training adds only trainable flips, which SR left unused ($v=0.003$, R5.1); on the trained foil the exact vote's own gain is $q_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.90$ and 0.92 at the counted sizes, at the route's 0.9 kill threshold before training and weakening with size.
* **Why this kills it**: locality fixes R6.7's cost, not R6.8's novelty, and the starting gain is already at the threshold and falling with $V$.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: exact ratios as continuous features (R8.2).
* **Strongest case against the kill**: SR from a vote-initialized start might keep and extend the gain. Not tested; the critic judged it unlikely from R5.1's inert flips.
* **Checked by**: round-7 per-leaf critic (2026-11-16, Major 4).
* **Lesson**: route-specific — a cheap form of the R6.8 relabel.
