# R2.3 — Low-rank-response AGP (Route 2.3) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Move the hidden-fermion idea into the pairing frame: the amplitude is a determinant of a pair function $F_0+PQ(\mathbf n)^{\top}$ restricted to occupied up and down sites, which contains projected BCS (the Foil). A one-sided low-rank correction keeps each ratio at $\mathcal{O}(N_er^2)$, so the local energy stays quadratic.
* **Lineage**: Operation B.1 (frame change from particle–hole to particle–particle), round 2.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at G0 (2026-11-11).
* **Kill rule**: Gate 0: is it known?
* **Evidence**: it is the hidden-fermion Pfaffian state (HFPS, arXiv:2507.10705, applied in arXiv:2511.07566) restricted to $S_z=0$ singlet pairing; HFPS already has a configuration-dependent rank-$\tilde N$ block, a Jastrow and low-rank updates. The two-sided version is cubic, because of a new–new contraction block of cost $\mathcal{O}(N_e^2r)$ per hop.
* **Why this kills it**: the one-sided form is a published family, and the two-sided form misses the cost target.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: a sum of terms over the pairing reference (R3.2) and local hidden rows in the pairing frame (R7.1).
* **Strongest case against the kill**: the carried-table ratio is cheaper than HFPS's own; recorded under R2.1 and R3.2, not a reason to keep this card.
* **Checked by**: round-2 per-leaf critic (2026-11-11).
* **Lesson**: route-specific — a restriction of HFPS.
