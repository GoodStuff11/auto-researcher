# R2.1 — Split-capacity hidden-fermion backflow, SC-HFDS (Route 2.1) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Use the Liu–Clark hidden-fermion correction $\det(\Phi_0+\Phi_hC(\mathbf n))$ with a narrow "sign trunk" network for $C$ and spend most of the parameters on a separate positive amplitude network $e^{J}$, on the hypothesis that the known deficit of low-rank corrections is amplitude, not sign. Carried tables make each ratio $\mathcal{O}(N_e)$, so $C_E=\mathcal{O}(N_e^2)$.
* **Lineage**: Operation A from R3 after its kill at G0 (round 2); after its own kill, its carried-table component was mutated into R3.3 and its frame into R3.2.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at G0 (2026-11-11, round-2 per-leaf critic).
* **Kill rule**: Gate 0: is it a relabel of known constructions?
* **Evidence**: HFDS times a Jastrow already exists (Liu–Clark; HFPS takes a Jastrow from the same network, arXiv:2507.10705), and the ratio is the Lrux low-rank kernel. The round-2 test was also loose: rank $r=2$ against $N_\sigma\in\{4,5\}$ electrons per spin, so the restriction did not bite.
* **Why this kills it**: every component is published, and the only new claim (deficit is amplitude) was not testable on the round-2 clusters.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: a sum of such terms (R2.2) and the pairing frame (R3.2).
* **Strongest case against the kill**: the capacity split is a claim no paper had measured; not answered except by noting that a measured claim on an untestable instance has no value.
* **Checked by**: round-2 per-leaf critic (2026-11-11).
* **Lesson**: route-specific — a relabel.
