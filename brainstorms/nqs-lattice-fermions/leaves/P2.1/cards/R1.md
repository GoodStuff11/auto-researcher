# R1 — Finite-range convolutional backflow with Woodbury updates (Route 1) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Restrict NNBF to a single convolution of range $r$, so that a hop changes only the $\mathcal{O}(r^2)$ orbital rows of particles near the two touched sites. The determinant ratio then follows from a carried inverse by the Woodbury identity as a small determinant, giving $C_E=\mathcal{O}(N_e^2)$ and $C_{\mathrm{sw}}=\mathcal{O}(N_e^3)$, the Foil's asymptotic cost, instead of NNBF's $\mathcal{O}(N_e^4)$.
* **Lineage**: initial batch (round 1, 2026-11-10); its locality mechanism reappears in every later route that adds a global channel (R3, R6.1′, R7.1).
* **Outcome**: KILLED at G0 (2026-11-10).
* **Kill rule**: Gate 0: is the route known, and does it fix the reason the known version is not standard practice?
* **Evidence**: SCALE (arXiv:2604.25775) is this construction with affected-site caching and Woodbury updates, and Lrux (arXiv:2602.05255) supplies the update kernels. SCALE's own $16\times16$, doping $1/8$ energies sit about $3$–$4\times10^{-3}t$ per site above transformer and deep-convolution backflow of the same paper, larger than the $10^{-3}t$ per-site scale that separates competing orders in the Hard Core.
* **Why this kills it**: the construction is published, its adoption barrier is the accuracy loss caused by locality, and the route contains nothing that addresses that loss.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: local backflow plus a mechanism that carries long-range correlation at the same cost class; R3, R6.1′, R6.1″, R7.1 and R7.3 are such variants.
* **Strongest case against the kill**: SCALE's gap could be an optimisation effect rather than a limit of locality; not raised by a critic, unresolved, and irrelevant to the novelty verdict.
* **Checked by**: round-1 per-leaf critic (2026-11-11), which accepted the gate kills.
* **Lesson**: route-specific — a novelty kill of one published construction.
