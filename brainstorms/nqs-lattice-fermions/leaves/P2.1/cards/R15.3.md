* **Strongest case against the kill**: a two-point exponent is a weak extrapolation, and LU efficiency saturates at some $N$; unresolved, but a direct $L=32$ timing (about 1 CPU-hour per arm) is predicted to miss by a factor of about 1.4, which fails the compute gate's G2.
