# R4 — Unbiased stochastic ratio estimator for unrestricted NNBF (Route 4) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: The local energy is linear in the amplitude ratios, so an unbiased estimator of each ratio $\det(I+A^{-1}\Delta)$ would keep the energy and gradients unbiased. Estimate the log-determinant with stochastic Chebyshev probes and exponentiate it without bias by Russian-roulette truncation, avoiding the $\mathcal{O}(N_e^3)$ determinant per connection.
* **Lineage**: initial batch (round 1); cross-field translation from randomised numerical linear algebra.
* **Source analogy**: randomised linear algebra estimates $\log\det$ of large matrices by a Chebyshev expansion and Hutchinson trace probes (Han, Malioutov and Shin, arXiv:1503.06394); it works because the log-determinant is a trace, hence additive and unbiasedly estimable. Correspondence: $\log\det(I+A^{-1}\Delta)$ is the log-ratio. Translation gap: the local energy needs the ratio itself, and an unbiased exponential of a noisy log has variance exponential in its magnitude.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at G1 (2026-11-10).
* **Kill rule**: Gate 1: does the route respect the complexity bound F1 and the variance properties of the local energy?
* **Evidence**: forming $\Delta(\mathbf n')$ for unrestricted NNBF already costs $\Omega(N_e^2)$ per connection, so $C_E=\Omega(N_e^3)$ before any estimator runs; the signed Russian-roulette series has variance growing like $\exp(\mathcal{O}(\lVert\log(I+X)\rVert))$, which diverges near nodes ($\rho\to0$) and for large ratios.
* **Why this kills it**: it misses the cost target by the matrix-output bound and destroys the zero-variance property exactly where the local-energy variance is set.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: multi-proposal or sign-stratified estimators (master note on P2.2′) and estimators for restricted families with low-rank $\Delta$.
* **Strongest case against the kill**: control variates could tame the variance; not raised by a critic; unresolved, but the F1 cost bound alone suffices.
* **Checked by**: round-1 per-leaf critic (2026-11-11); also in the master global anti-pattern "single-proposal stochastic replacement of an exact non-stoquastic sum".
