# R6.2 — Product of local rank-1 determinant-ratio factors, PLR (Route 6.2) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Replace SK-LR's sum by a product over sites of local factors $1+h_m(\mathbf n)\cdot Z[:,m]$, each equal to a one-hidden-fermion determinant ratio over the reference, $\det(A_0+\phi_mc_m^{\top})/\det A_0$. A product of block factors factorises over decoupled blocks, so the sign capacity is size-consistent, and all $V$ factors refresh in $\mathcal{O}(VF)$ per hop.
* **Lineage**: Operation B.3, cross-field translation from coupled-cluster theory (round 6).
* **Source analogy**: coupled cluster is size-extensive while truncated configuration interaction is not, because the exponential (product) form factorises over non-interacting fragments (Bartlett and Musiał, Rev. Mod. Phys. 79, 291, 2007). Correspondence: SK's sum of terms is truncated CI; PLR's product is the exponential form. Translation gap: each factor is normalised by $\det A_0$, so the product divides by the reference determinant once per site.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at G1 (2026-11-16, round-6 per-leaf critic; an earlier timing-guard kill was voided).
* **Kill rule**: Gate 1: is the amplitude a well-behaved fermionic function (no singularities that break sampling)?
* **Evidence**: per spin $\psi_{\mathrm{PLR}}=\det(A_0)^{1-M}\prod_m\det(A_0+\phi_mc_m^{\top})$, a pole of order $M-1$ at every reference node; the critic's check measured $\lvert\psi\rvert\propto\varepsilon^{-35.11}$ against the predicted $\varepsilon^{-35}$ at $M=V=36$, $\max\log_{10}\lvert\prod_mf_m\rvert$ of $+22$ at $L=6$ and $+78$ at $L=8$ over 800 random blocks, and a T1 local energy near $-9\times10^{20}$ at $L=8$. It is also dominated by R6.1′, which puts the same factors in one determinant and is pole-free.
* **Why this kills it**: under $\lvert\psi\rvert^2$ sampling the chain is drawn to the reference nodes and the local energy has unbounded variance, with an extensive pole order.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: product forms that are polynomials in bounded entries (boundary condition B7.2), such as R6.1′.
* **Strongest case against the kill**: random weights exaggerate the poles; the order of the pole is structural, so training cannot remove it.
