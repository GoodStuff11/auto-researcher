# R2.2 — Shared-reference low-rank multi-determinant, SK-LR (Route 2.2) (MERGED)
* **The idea**: Sum $K$ hidden-fermion terms, each a configuration-dependent low-rank correction over one shared reference determinant, $\psi=e^J\prod_\sigma\det A_{0\sigma}\sum_kw_k\prod_\sigma\det(I_r+C_{k\sigma}(\mathbf n)Y_{k\sigma})$. One carried inverse serves all terms, so $C_E=\mathcal{O}(N_e^2)$, while the sum gives more nodal freedom than one term (a $K$-secant of the minor map, by Cauchy–Binet).
* **Lineage**: Operation B, cross-field translation from the table method of continuum quantum Monte Carlo (round 2); retained unchanged as R3.1 with a new test in round 3.
* **Source analogy**: multi-Slater–Jastrow QMC evaluates many excited determinants that share one reference with one inverse, quadratic in the electron number and linear in the number of excitations (Clark, Morales, McMinis, Kim and Scuseria, J. Chem. Phys. 135, 244105, 2011); it works because the excitations and CI coefficients are fixed. Correspondence: excitations are the $K$ low-rank terms, CI coefficients are the network outputs $C_k(\mathbf n)$. Translation gap: the coefficients here depend on the configuration, and a sum of terms is not size-consistent (found later, R3.1).
* **Outcome**: MERGED into R3.1 (2026-11-11).
