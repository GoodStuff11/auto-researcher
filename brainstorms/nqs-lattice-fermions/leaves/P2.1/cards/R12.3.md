# R12.3 — Checkerboard `half` at larger hidden width $F$ (Round 12 option 3; Rounds 12c and 12d) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Buy back the sign capacity that R11.1 lost by giving each checkerboard hidden row more features ($F=16$ instead of 4). The expensive accepted-move update depends on the matrix dimension $N+V_h$, not on $F$, which enters only the cheap ratio terms, so the cost advantage over dense HFPS should largely survive while the rows become more expressive.
* **Lineage**: Operation A on R11.1 after its T1 kill (Round 12 decision tree, option 3); cost-gated in 12c, accuracy-tested in 12d (job 706240, cancelled at the pause) and completed at $V=15$ in Round 14 (job 731409). No successor yet; generation round 15 is in progress.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at T1 (2026-11-17, job 731409).
* **Kill rule**: Round 12d comparison A, written before any scored run: with $\rho(V)=\Delta E_{\mathrm{sign}}(\texttt{half\_F16})/\Delta E_{\mathrm{sign}}(\texttt{full\_F4})$ on the counted sizes, KILL by the trend clause if $\rho$ rises strictly over the counted sizes and $\rho(V_{\max})>1.15$ (marginal, critic first, if $\rho(V_{\max})<1.5$); otherwise PASS-TO-T2 at unmatched parameters.
* **Evidence**: OBC $t$–$t'$–$J$ toys ($t'=-0.25t$, $J=0.4t$), unprojected SR to a plateau, seed 11, all four sizes converged. $\rho=0.038$, 0.886, 1.110 and 1.915 at $V=8$, 12, 14 and 15 (jobs 706240 and 731409); at $V=15$, $\Delta E_{\mathrm{sign}}=2.83\times10^{-3}$ for `half_F16` against $1.48\times10^{-3}$ for `full_F4`. Cost (12c, compiled, untrained parameters, $L=16$, against dense HFPS): $R_D=5.34\pm1.47$ at $F=16$, GATE-PASS.
* **Why this kills it**: The ratio rises strictly with size and its largest-size value, 1.915, is above both the 1.15 kill line and the 1.5 marginal line, so the pre-registered trend clause fires outright. Since `half_F16` already has 1.6 to 1.8 times the parameters of `full_F4`, a matched-parameter comparison could only make it worse, and quadrupling $F$ delayed the capacity loss of checkerboard rows by about two sites (from $\rho=2.07$ at $V=14$ at $F=4$ to 1.92 at $V=15$ at $F=16$) without removing it.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: other ways to keep fewer than one hidden row per site with more capacity per row (for example hole-gated rows at $F=8$, R13.7, shelved); trained-VMC accuracy at $L\ge16$, where no test exists; projected training; more seeds (one seed per size).
* **Strongest case against the kill**: one seed per size, and $V=8$ is sector fitting (820 parameters against a 560-dimensional space), so the trend rests on three counted non-trivial sizes with no seed spread. Answered by the margin: $\rho(15)=1.915$ is 0.77 above the kill line and the rise from $V=14$ (1.110) is larger than any seed effect seen in this leaf's earlier ladders. Unresolved: a second seed was not run.
* **Checked by**: pre-registered rule applied by the orchestrator, 2026-11-17 (not marginal, so no critic referral was required by the rule); per-leaf critic, 2026-11-17, had upheld the rule as stated before the result.
* **Lesson**: L28 (`library.md`): fewer than one hidden row per site loses sign capacity on 12- to 15-site clusters; this card shows the loss persists at $F=16$.
