# R6.1′ — Local-hidden-row HFDS with a plus-shaped patch, LH-HFDS (Route 6.1′) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: One hidden fermion per site $m$, whose hidden row $C_m(\mathbf n)=h_m(\mathbf n)\Omega_m$ depends only on the features of a small patch around $m$, coupled to the physical orbitals by a learned global linear map $K$: $A=\Phi_0[\mathrm{occ}]+K[\mathrm{occ},:]C(\mathbf n)$. A hop changes only the $b$ hidden rows whose patch it touches, so ratios are $(1+b)$-dimensional determinants, $C_E=\mathcal{O}(N_e^2)$, and decoupled blocks factorise.
* **Lineage**: Operation A on R6.1 (round 6); its reported-only $3\times3$ arm became R6.1″; its frame change became R7.1 and its resolvent extension R7.3.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at T2 (2026-11-16, job 698229).
* **Kill rule**: pre-registered T2 rule (round 7): "KILL if $\rho_N\ge1.5$, or if $\rho_F>0.85$ (the route does not beat the one-site lattice-backflow foil)", with $\rho_F=\Delta E_{\mathrm{sign}}(\texttt{lh\_plus})/\Delta E_{\mathrm{sign}}(\texttt{lh\_site})$ and $\rho_N$ the ratio to parameter-matched NNBF.
* **Evidence**: instance 4 ($5\times3$ OBC $t$–$t'$–$J$, $6+6$ electrons), seed 11, 75 projected SR steps, $P\approx1626$: $\Delta E_{\mathrm{sign}}$ 0.0655 (route), 0.0755 (one-site foil), 0.0880 (NNBF), so $\rho_F=0.868$ and $\rho_N=0.744$; single seed, no error bar. Round 8b had also given KILL-COST (uncertain): cost per decorrelated sample against NNBF of width 6 at $L=16$, $R(16)=0.96\pm0.42$.
* **Why this kills it**: the plus patch does not beat one-site features (a known long-range lattice backflow class, Tocchio–Becca) by the pre-registered margin, so the patch nonlinearity adds no demonstrated sign quality.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: larger patches (R6.1″), other frames (R7.1), and longer training; the 75-step horizon measures learning speed.
* **Strongest case against the kill**: a single seed at 75 steps cannot resolve 0.868 from 0.85; unresolved, but the rule was pre-registered and the route's successor R6.1″ was tested instead.
