# R13.6 — Delayed acceptance with `half` as surrogate, DA (Round 13.6) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Screen proposals with the cheap `half` wave function (R11.1) and evaluate the expensive all-sites ratio only for proposals that pass, so most of the sweep runs at `half`'s cost while the chain samples the all-sites distribution exactly.
* **Lineage**: Operation B (Round 13), a re-proposal of R2.5's delayed-acceptance idea with a new surrogate.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at G4 (2026-11-16, T0).
* **Kill rule**: Gate 4: operation count of the full sweep at size.
* **Evidence**: every accepted move still pays the all-sites update, and on the 11c engine accepted-move work is about 70% of the sweep, so the saving is bounded by the 30% ratio share, at most a factor 1.4; the local energy still needs all full ratios.
* **Why this kills it**: the bound is far below the factor-3 cost bar.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: delayed acceptance for families where ratios, not updates, dominate.
* **Strongest case against the kill**: none raised.
* **Checked by**: no per-leaf critic yet.
* **Lesson**: route-specific — the update, not the ratio, dominates here.
