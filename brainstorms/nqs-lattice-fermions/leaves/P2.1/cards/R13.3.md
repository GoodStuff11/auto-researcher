# R13.3 — Checkerboard plus hole-gated rows, `cbh` (Round 13.3) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Hidden rows on the checkerboard always, and on the other sublattice when that site is empty, so the family contains `half` (R11.1) and adds rows where holes move. Its active-row count lies between `half` and the all-sites route, aiming to recover capacity at intermediate cost.
* **Lineage**: Operation A combining the levers of R11.1 and R13.2 (Round 13).
* **Outcome**: KILLED at T1 (2026-11-16).
* **Kill rule**: Round 13 T1 cost rule: "PASS-cost if $R_D(16)-\sigma\ge3$; KILL-cost otherwise".
* **Evidence**: $L=16$: $R_D=1.71\pm0.43$, $R_D-\sigma=1.27$; the `half` arm on the same Schur engine was slower than `full_S` (0.056 s against 0.035 s per sweep), because the gathered active-row loops are scalar code.
* **Why this kills it**: it is not cheaper than dense HFPS by the pre-registered factor, so it has no cost case.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: the same family with a matrix–matrix gather for the active rows, which would remove the scalar-loop penalty.
* **Strongest case against the kill**: the kill is partly an implementation artefact (scalar loops), recorded in the leaf; unresolved, and the route would also inherit `half`'s capacity loss.
