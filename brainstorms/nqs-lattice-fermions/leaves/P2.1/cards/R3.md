# R3 — Local plus global-bottleneck backflow, LGB-BF (Route 3) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Each orbital row is a mean-field orbital plus a full-rank local term from a one-layer convolution plus a gated product of $K$ global channels pooled from all local features. A hop changes the rows within range $r$ fully and all other rows only through a rank-$2K$ change, so a carried projected Green's function gives exact ratios at $C_E=\mathcal{O}(N_e^2)$ while the global channels carry long-range information.
* **Lineage**: initial batch (round 1); the round-2 autopsy mutated it into R2.1 and its global channel into the Liu–Clark block of R3.4.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at G0 (2026-11-11, by the round-1 per-leaf critic at Gates 0 and 2).
* **Kill rule**: Gate 0 (is the family new, or a subfamily of a known one?) and Gate 2 (can the planned test fail against the Foil?).
* **Evidence**: the pooled $\tanh$ global term is a strict subfamily of the Liu–Clark rank-$r$ correction (arXiv:2311.09450), whose coefficient block is an arbitrary function of the configuration and which already underperforms full NNBF; the local part is SCALE. The planned 24-hour test measured only the timing slope of $C_E$ on random weights, which the Foil also meets ($C_E=\mathcal{O}(N_e^2)$).
* **Why this kills it**: the family adds no expressive mechanism beyond two published ones, and the test could not separate it from the Foil, so even a PASS would have shown nothing.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: a global channel at least as rich as the Liu–Clark block (R2.1, R3.4) and an accuracy metric; both were adopted as boundary conditions B1 and B2 of round 2.
* **Strongest case against the kill**: the combination (full-rank local plus global bottleneck) might train better than either component; not raised by a critic; unresolved, but a combination of known parts needs an accuracy test to claim anything, which this route did not have.
* **Checked by**: round-1 per-leaf critic (2026-11-11).
* **Lesson**: route-specific — a subfamily of a known weaker family plus an unfalsifiable test.
