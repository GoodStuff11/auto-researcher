# R15.2 — Checkerboard rows with a radius-2 receptive field (Round 15.2) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: If `half` fails at $V=15$ because no hidden row sees an odd-centred patch, give each even-site row features from its own plus patch and from the four neighbouring odd-centred plus patches (a radius-2 diamond of 13 sites). The row count stays $V/2$, so the carried dimension stays $n=N+V/2$, while each row now sees every feature that the all-sites route's rows see.
* **Lineage**: Operation A on R12.3, Round 15 (the $V=15$ data say width is not the missing ingredient; receptive field might be). A sibling of R7.2 (two-layer local trunk) restricted to the checkerboard. No successor.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at G4 (2026-11-17, T0).
* **Kill rule**: Gate 4 (operation count at size): is the predicted $R_D(16)$ high enough to clear $R_D(16)-\sigma\ge3$ at this leaf's typical $\sigma\approx1.0$ to 1.3, and is it below the measured cost of a foil with full capacity?
* **Evidence**: receptive-field lemma: a hop changes every hidden row whose receptive field contains a touched site. Counted on the square lattice with the 8b proposal (nearest-neighbour and diagonal hops equally likely): plus-patch `half` has $b=4.0$ (matching the 12c measurement of 4.0), plus-patch `full` $b=8$ (measured $k=9.6$), radius-2 `half` $b=0.5\cdot9+0.25\cdot12+0.25\cdot6=9.0$, so $k\approx9.8$, the same as `full`. Converting with two two-term models fitted to the measured 11c costs at $L=16$ (`full` $R_D=1.78$, `half` $R_D=5.05$): $c\propto\alpha n^2k+\beta n^2$ gives $R_D(16)\approx4.2$; $c\propto\alpha n^2k+\gamma$ gives 3.3. Both are below the 4.3 needed to clear the bar at $\sigma=1.3$, and the scalar feature loops grow with a 13-site receptive field (Round 12c measured that feature loops cost 4 to 5 times their operation share), which only lowers them.
* **Why this kills it**: once the receptive field is wide enough to restore the missing features, the update rank equals the all-sites route's, and the only remaining saving is the $n^2$ factor; the predicted cost lands at or below R13.1's measured $3.50\pm0.85$, a foil that has the full capacity and already failed the bar.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: placement that adapts to the configuration with $b$ held near 4 (R15.6, since killed at T1), and a GPU or batched engine with different kernel speeds.
* **Strongest case against the kill**: the first model's central value (4.2) is within 0.1 of the threshold, and both models are two-point fits; unresolved, but even a pass would need an accuracy test that L39 limits to kill-only at $V\le15$ (a radius-2 diamond covers 13 of 15 sites), so a T1 pass could not promote it.
* **Checked by**: self-gated by the round-15 route generator (2026-11-17); no per-leaf critic yet.
* **Lesson**: L58 (`library.md`): in patch-local hidden-row families the update rank is the number of rows whose receptive field contains a touched site, so thinning rows while widening receptive fields saves only the matrix dimension.
