# R2.1 — A-Ritz on real states with an abstention-penalised rule (Route 2.1) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Keep A-Ritz but score it on SR-trained NNBF and foil states, estimate every matrix element by VMC local estimators, use a first-order rescaling $\hat\delta=(a_\phi-a_\psi)/(1-\sigma_\phi/\sigma_\psi)$ and count abstentions as misses, so the energy decides how much of the order direction to add.
* **Lineage**: Operation A.3 on R1.4 (Round 2). Its physical point (the deciding direction is $(\hat A-a)\psi$, weighted by $\hat H$) passed to R3.2.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at CRITIC (2026-11-11)
* **Kill rule**: Round 2 per-leaf critic objections, accepted in Round 3.
* **Evidence**: $\hat\delta$ is algebraically a two-point linear extrapolation in $\sigma$ through $\psi$ and the Ritz state, i.e. the killed R1.1 mechanism in another variable. The smoke run ($3\times2$, $N_s=5000$) showed the quantum-subspace-expansion noise: AR tracked 0.78 with exact matrices (against 0.56 for L1 and L2) but only 0.33 when sampled.
* **Why this kills it**: The estimator inherits the Krylov blindness, and its sampled generalized eigenproblem is too noisy to issue certificates.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: The observable direction used with a different engine (a trained or solved response, R3.2 to R10.1d).
* **Strongest case against the kill**: The exact-mode smoke favoured AR over L1 and L2. Answered: one toy cluster, and the sampled mode is the method.
