# R7.1 — Excited-state subspace Ritz (ESR0, Route 7.1) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Train $K$ in-sector excited states $\varphi_k$ with the ground state's architecture (orthogonality penalties), do Rayleigh–Ritz in $\mathrm{span}\{\psi,(\hat H-E)\psi,\varphi_1..\varphi_K\}$ and report $\hat\delta=a(\text{Ritz})-a(\psi)$. Proposition S3: the step removes the fraction $\omega_k/(\omega_k+\varepsilon_k)$ of each low component, where $\varepsilon_k$ is the excited state's own energy error, so the low window supplies exactly the information that $\hat H$-moments lack.
* **Lineage**: Round 7, from the Saturation Diagnostic (the error lives in a few low levels). Variants: R7.1v (ESR), R7.1a (K=6 extension), R7.1b (GO-6 step 2), and the windows R8.3, R8.4, R8.9.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at G4 (2026-11-16); kept as an ED-scale diagnostic.
* **Kill rule**: Round 8 Gate 4 rule, written before the run: FAIL if the median $K_{1/2}$ (window size needed for the oracle ratio to stay in $[\tfrac12,2]$) at $4\times3$ is at least twice the $3\times2$ median or not reached.
* **Evidence**: T1 (`t1_portfolio.py`, toys at $3\times2$, $3\times3$, $6\times2$, $4\times3$): median $K_{1/2}$ for the pair aggregate 2, 1, 2, 17.5 and for charge 1, 1, 2, 13; on the real seed-11 $4\times4$ state $K_{1/2}$ = 2 (pair), 6 (spin), 11 (charge). T0: the number of in-sector levels below a fixed $\Omega$ grows as $e^{s(\Omega)V}$. Positive side: job 454376 INJECT-PASS (16 of 21, $G=0$; L1R 1 of 21) and pilot 456094 GO (trained $\varepsilon_k/\omega_k=0.13$–0.18; exact ESR0/ORC 1.17/1.06 for pair).
* **Why this kills it**: The method's cost is $K_{1/2}$ trainings at about $1.5\times$ the ground state each, and $K_{1/2}$ grows with level density, so it is not a scalable error bar for real-space correlators.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: Observables whose error concentrates on $\mathcal O(1)$ levels (tested as R8.3 and found NO-TARGET on real states); use at fixed ED size as a diagnostic.
* **Strongest case against the kill**: The toy error model was mis-calibrated (twice the real error weight, found Round 10), so the toy growth is over-stated. Answered: the kill also stands on the T0 level-count argument, and the real $K_{1/2}=11$ already exceeds every toy value at smaller sizes.
* **Checked by**: per-leaf critic, Round 7 (Gate 0 downgrade to diagnostic); worker Gate 4 rule, Round 8; Round 9 per-leaf critic (kill stands).
