# R3.5 — Provable sign witness on projected states (Route 3.5) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Build a witness of wrong signs that remains valid after symmetry projection, following the critic's direction, so that the sign part of the observable error can be detected and corrected on the states practitioners actually use.
* **Lineage**: critic direction, Round 3.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at G2 (2026-11-12)
* **Kill rule**: Gate 2: does the trivial baseline already match?
* **Evidence**: P1.1-P partial data: projected NNBF has sign-mismatch weight at most $5\times10^{-7}$ and sign fraction $f_C\le0.11$ on the $4\times3$ clusters.
* **Why this kills it**: The baseline "sign error is zero" is already right within error bars.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: Instances where projection leaves a sign fraction of at least 0.5 (stated reopening condition).
* **Strongest case against the kill**: The $4\times4$ OBC instance later kept $f_E=0.22$–0.24 after projection. Unresolved: the witness was not revisited there.
* **Checked by**: per-leaf gate pass, Round 3.
* **Lesson**: route-specific — depends on the projected sign fraction of one instance family.
