# R7.6 — Ritz-trained single auxiliary network (RTA, Route 7.6) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Train one auxiliary network to minimise the lowest Ritz value of $\mathrm{span}\{\psi,\hat H\psi,\chi\}$, so the auxiliary captures whatever correction the energy prefers, and read the error from the improved Ritz vector.
* **Lineage**: Round 7 batch.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at G2 (2026-11-12)
* **Kill rule**: Gate 2.
* **Evidence**: The optimal auxiliary is $Q\psi_0$, so RTA is a two-member capacity ladder without extrapolation; it reports the improvement at doubled capacity, an under-estimate whenever the doubled family shares the bias.
* **Why this kills it**: It measures what more capacity buys, not the remaining error.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: Auxiliaries trained against an observable-specific objective (R10.1d).
* **Strongest case against the kill**: None.
* **Checked by**: per-leaf critic, Round 7.
* **Lesson**: L41 (`library.md`)
