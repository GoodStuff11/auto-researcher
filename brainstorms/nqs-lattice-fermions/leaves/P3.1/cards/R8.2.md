# R8.2 — Field-ladder eigenvector continuation (Route 8.2) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Do Ritz over states trained at several fields $\hat H+h_i\hat A$ (eigenvector continuation), so that the field-dependent states span the direction in which the observable error lies.
* **Lineage**: Operation A on R7.2 and R7.6 (Round 8).
* **Outcome**: KILLED at T0 (2026-11-16)
* **Kill rule**: T0 argument (Gates 0 and 1).
* **Evidence**: Known method (Frame et al., PRL 121, 032501 (2018); NN-VMC with continuation arXiv:2606.12998). To first order in $h$ the trained states differ from $\psi$ by tangent vectors, so Brillouin makes the recovered fraction zero; at finite $h$ the shared bias $e(h)\approx e(0)+he'$ cannot cancel because the coefficients sum to about 1.
* **Why this kills it**: Zero at first order and blind to shared bias.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: Continuation in a parameter that changes the bias direction.
* **Strongest case against the kill**: None.
* **Checked by**: worker T0 argument, Round 8; Round 9 critic.
