# R6.4 — Architecture disagreement calibrated by an independent-error model (Route 6.4) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Train several architectures, assume their errors are independent, and calibrate the observable's error from their disagreement, which is how intertwined-order disputes between NQS architectures are currently argued in the literature.
* **Lineage**: Round 6 batch; its capacity ordering was kept as the CLX ladder.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at G0 (2026-11-12)
* **Kill rule**: Gates 0 and 2.
* **Evidence**: It is architecture consensus (Viteritti et al., arXiv:2604.21978) and seed spread (arXiv:2606.02794). Shared bias is invisible; later measured: all three real seeds have the same sign of $\delta$ in every channel (job 698163).
* **Why this kills it**: Agreement between biased families is not an error bar.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: Disagreement between methods with provably different bias.
* **Strongest case against the kill**: None.
* **Checked by**: per-leaf gate pass, Round 6; Round 9 critic.
