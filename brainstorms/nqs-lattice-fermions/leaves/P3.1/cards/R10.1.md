# R10.1 — Self-consistent response Ritz with a fixed base (SCR, Route 10.1) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Rebuild the trained responses on the Ritz-corrected state each pass instead of on $\psi$, then do Ritz again in a span that keeps the original $\psi$ as base, so the response converges toward the response of $\psi_0$ (which tracked 0.93).
* **Lineage**: Operation A on R9.1 (Batch 10); mutated into R10.1u.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at G4 (2026-11-16)
* **Kill rule**: Round 9 per-leaf critic, Gate 4 (size consistency).
* **Evidence**: Critic toy of $M$ decoupled copies, exact arithmetic: with the base kept, the recovered fraction falls 0.95 to 0.81 (infidelity 0.08 to 0.29) and 0.91 to 0.53 (0.20 to 0.59); with the base updated each pass it stays 0.99–1.01. At the real per-site quality the infidelity reaches 0.15 at $V=32$ and 0.28 at $V=64$.
* **Why this kills it**: A linear correction of a fixed state by $m$ global vectors recovers less per region as the number of erroneous regions grows (the CI-versus-coupled-cluster defect).
* **What the kill does not rule out**: The base-updated form (R10.1u, R10.1d).
* **Strongest case against the kill**: T1b toys at fixed overlap did not show the defect. Answered: fixed overlap lowers per-site error as $V$ grows, hiding it.
* **Checked by**: Round 9 per-leaf critic (2026-11-16).
