# R1.3 — Pinning or pairing-field response extrapolation (Route 3) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Run VMC at $\hat H-h\hat O$ for several pinning or pairing fields $h$ and extrapolate the induced order parameter to $h\to0$, as is standard in sign-free quantum Monte Carlo, to obtain the order parameter and its error.
* **Lineage**: initial batch (Round 1); its in-family variants are R6.3, R7.2 and R8.2.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at G2 (2026-11-10)
* **Kill rule**: Gates 0, 2 and 3.
* **Evidence**: Gate 0: Assaad & Herbut, PRX 3, 031010 (2013). Gate 2 argument: a projected-BCS state with a built-in gap responds linearly at every $h$ and extrapolates to its own order. Gate 3: a non-selective field needs $h\ll\Delta$, below variational resolution.
* **Why this kills it**: The method returns the ansatz's mean-field reference, the Unacceptable Outcome, and no mechanism against that is proposed.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: Pinning fields with an unbiased solver; field responses used as information about the response rather than the offset.
* **Strongest case against the kill**: Symmetry projection removes the reason an ordered ansatz answers with its own order. Answered in R7.2: the capacity-limited offset does not enter $\partial_ha$ at first order.
* **Checked by**: per-leaf gate pass, Round 1; restated by R7.2 (Round 7).
* **Lesson**: L6 (`library.md`)
