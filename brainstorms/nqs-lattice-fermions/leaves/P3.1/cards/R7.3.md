# R7.3 — Certificate from Weinstein or Temple bounds in the low window (Route 7.3) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Issue a rigorous certificate on $\delta$ using Temple or Lehmann bounds restricted to the low in-sector window, combining the trained excited energies with the ground-state energy to bound how much low-energy error the state can carry.
* **Lineage**: Round 7 batch.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at G1 (2026-11-12)
* **Kill rule**: Gate 1.
* **Evidence**: Rigorous lower bounds on $E_0$ and $E_{K+1}$ are needed; variational excited states give upper bounds (the wrong direction), and ground-energy lower bounds are QMA-hard in general. Even with oracle energies, the energy-window certificate must cover every state with energy at most $E_\psi$: job 454376 measured that floor $C^*/\lvert\delta\rvert=81$ (pair), 8.6 (spin), 29 (charge).
* **Why this kills it**: Any honest certificate is 9 to 81 times the true error.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: Certificates using observable-specific information beyond energies.
* **Strongest case against the kill**: SDP lower bounds exist (Wang et al., PRX 14, 031006 (2024)). Unresolved for doped $t$–$J$ at $0.05t$ precision.
* **Checked by**: per-leaf critic, Round 7.
* **Lesson**: L6 (`library.md`)
