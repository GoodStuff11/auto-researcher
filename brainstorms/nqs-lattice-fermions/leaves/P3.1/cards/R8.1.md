# R8.1 — Response-window Ritz (RWR, Route 8.1) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Add the static response vector $y_A=(\hat H-E_0)^{-1}Q(\hat A-a_0)\psi_0$ of each observable to the Ritz span; a first-order lemma shows the recovered error then equals $\sum_nc_nA_{n0}$ exactly, however many levels carry it, so one vector per observable replaces a growing excited-state window.
* **Lineage**: Operation A on the R3.2 and R5.1 kills (Round 8); mutated into R8.8 and R9.1.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at T1 (2026-11-16)
* **Kill rule**: Pre-registered: KILL if RWR* (exact response) tracks under 0.9 of scored items, or RWR-R (grade-R training noise: 30% in-window, 50% junk) under 0.5, or RWR-R at $4\times3$ more than 0.25 below $3\times2$.
* **Evidence**: `t1_portfolio.py`: RWR* tracked 0.93 (0.84 at $4\times3$); RWR-R tracked 0.80 at $3\times2$ but 0.37 at $4\times3$; grade H 0.23 overall.
* **Why this kills it**: Noise in the response puts low-energy weight into the Ritz denominator from the dense window, and the precision needed grows with level density, which is Gate 4 in disguise.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: Krylov-cleaned (R9.1) or self-consistent, base-updated responses (R10.1u).
* **Strongest case against the kill**: The toy error weight was twice the real one and the 3x3 anchors were confounded (Round 9 critic). Answered by T1b at the real weight: the single-shot response still lost 0.27.
* **Checked by**: pre-registered rule; Round 9 per-leaf critic (kill stands).
