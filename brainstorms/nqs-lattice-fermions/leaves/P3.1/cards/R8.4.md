# R8.4 — Observable-ordered excited-state window (AOW, Route 8.4) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Train excited states in order of their coupling to $(\hat A-a_\psi)\psi$ divided by their energy, a quantity a targeted trainer can see, instead of in energy order, so the window reaches the levels that carry the error first.
* **Lineage**: Operation A on R7.1 (Round 8).
* **Outcome**: KILLED at T1 (2026-11-16)
* **Kill rule**: Pre-registered: KILL if for at least 2 of 3 observables the $4\times3$ median $K^{\mathrm A}_{1/2}$ is at least twice the $3\times2$ median or not below the energy-ordered value.
* **Evidence**: Median $K^{\mathrm A}_{1/2}$ for spin went from 1.5 to 9 and for charge from 1 to 19 (above the energy-ordered 13).
* **Why this kills it**: The coupling to $\psi$ does not identify which levels carry $c_nA_{n0}$, because the error's own coefficients decide that.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: Ordering by an estimate of the error coefficients themselves.
* **Strongest case against the kill**: Toy model mis-calibration (Round 10). Not re-run; the mechanism argument stands.
* **Checked by**: pre-registered rule; Round 9 per-leaf critic.
* **Lesson**: L4 (`library.md`)
