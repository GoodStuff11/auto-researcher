# R1.2 — Susceptibility (Legendre) certificate (Route 2) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Use the exact inequality $\lvert a_\psi-a_0\rvert\le\sqrt{2\chi_A(E_\psi-E_0)}$, where $\chi_A$ is the static susceptibility of the ground energy to a field $h\hat A$, measuring $\chi_A$ from VMC runs in small fields. It replaces the unknowable gap by an observable-specific curvature.
* **Lineage**: initial batch (Round 1). Its inequality is reused as the sanity bound of R6.5 and the certificate floor of R7.3.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at G2 (2026-11-10)
* **Kill rule**: Gate 2: can the Minimal Contrast Foil or a cheap baseline game the metric?
* **Evidence**: Argument: $\chi_A$ is only accessible through the variational family; a Jastrow–projected-BCS family cannot represent the competing-order fluctuation that makes the true $\chi_A$ large, so its constrained energy profile is stiffer, $\hat\chi_A<\chi_A$.
* **Why this kills it**: The bound shrinks exactly on a wrong, stiff foil, so it certifies the mean-field order, which is the Unacceptable Outcome.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: The inequality itself, with an independently known $\chi_A$ (for example from an unbiased method).
* **Strongest case against the kill**: A flexible NQS family might estimate $\chi_A$ well. Unresolved; no measurement was made, but any in-family estimate stays vulnerable on the foil.
* **Checked by**: per-leaf gate pass, Round 1; per-leaf critic, Round 2 (2026-11-11).
* **Lesson**: L6 (`library.md`)
