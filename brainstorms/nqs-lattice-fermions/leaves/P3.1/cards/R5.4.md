# R5.4 — Local imaginary-time step (Route 5.4) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Apply a Gutzwiller-type local imaginary-time step $e^{-\tau\hat H_{\mathrm{loc}}}$ to the trained state and read the change in the observable as its error, a cheap local projector toward the ground state.
* **Lineage**: Round 5 batch.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at CRITIC (2026-11-12)
* **Kill rule**: Round 5 requirement (from the Round 3 critic): no $\hat H$-generated direction.
* **Evidence**: The step is a polynomial-type filter in $\hat H$ pieces; on the real state one and two Lanczos steps barely moved $\delta$ (R1.1 evidence).
* **Why this kills it**: An $\hat H$-generated direction is blind to the low-energy error.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: Rational (shift-inverted) filters.
* **Strongest case against the kill**: A local step is not a global Krylov step. Unresolved; not tested.
* **Checked by**: per-leaf critic rule, Round 5.
* **Lesson**: L3 (`library.md`)
