# R6.2 — Twist or boundary-condition averaging as an error signal (Route 6.2) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Train the state at several boundary twists and use the spread of the observable across twists as a signal of its variational error, borrowing twist averaging from finite-size studies.
* **Lineage**: Round 6 batch.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at G2 (2026-11-12)
* **Kill rule**: Gate 2.
* **Evidence**: Twist averaging reduces finite-size error of $a_0$, not variational bias; each twist carries its own error, so the spread is a seed-spread analogue. The shared instance is OBC and has no twist.
* **Why this kills it**: It measures the wrong error and cannot be applied to the instance.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: Nothing about variational bias.
* **Strongest case against the kill**: None.
* **Checked by**: per-leaf gate pass, Round 6.
* **Lesson**: route-specific — twist averaging targets finite-size error.
