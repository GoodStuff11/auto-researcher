# R3.1 — Ensemble-calibrated covariance extrapolation (ECC, Route 3.1) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Regress $a_k$ over many independent projected trajectories on the signed, first-order covariance $c_k=\mathrm{Cov}(E_{\mathrm{loc}},A_{\mathrm{loc}})$ (zero for any eigenstate) with one common intercept; the slope supplies the scale that one state cannot, and the residual anchors the error so shared bias is not invisible.
* **Lineage**: Operation A.3 on R2.3 (Round 3). Its single-state ablation became R5.3.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at CRITIC (2026-11-12)
* **Kill rule**: Round 3 per-leaf critic; then the pilot rule of job 410398 (NO-GO if fewer than 2 instances have converged projected states with a scorable error).
* **Evidence**: $c\approx\sum_ne_nw_n\omega_n$ is the first Lanczos moment, so it reaches a competitor only with weight $\propto\omega$. Pilot 410398: 0 of 6 $4\times3$ instances met the target criterion (projected states nearly exact there).
* **Why this kills it**: Its regressor is an $\hat H$-moment, blind to low-energy error, and on the planned instances there was nothing to estimate.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: Ensemble calibration on a regressor that is not an $\hat H$-moment.
* **Strongest case against the kill**: The smoke had ECC at 0.80 tracked. Answered: the smoke states were barely trained (error at $\omega_k=10$–$41t$), the regime where L1 also wins (1.00).
* **Checked by**: per-leaf critic, Round 3 (2026-11-12).
* **Lesson**: L3 (`library.md`)
