# R3.3 — Seed non-orthogonal CI (seed NOCI, Route 3.3) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Do Rayleigh–Ritz in the span of independently trained seeds and extrapolate $a$ in $1/K$ seeds to remove the seed-independent error, using the seeds as a basis that might span the missing correlations.
* **Lineage**: Operation B.2 (active ensemble probing), Round 3.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at G1 (2026-11-12)
* **Kill rule**: Gate 1: conservation and asymptotic cost.
* **Evidence**: The cross-overlap estimator $\langle\psi_2/\psi_1\rangle$ has relative variance $(1-F)/F$ with seed–seed fidelity $F\sim e^{-\epsilon V}$; this is P3.2's killed NOCI mechanism.
* **Why this kills it**: The sample cost is exponential in $V$.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: Ritz over vectors generated from one state (no cross-state overlaps), as in R10.1d.
* **Strongest case against the kill**: None.
* **Checked by**: per-leaf gate pass, Round 3; same mechanism as P3.2's critic.
* **Lesson**: L7 (`library.md`)
