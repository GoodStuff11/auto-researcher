# R3.6 — Laplace or Fisher posterior error bar (Route 3.6) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Treat the energy Hessian $\mathcal H$ as an inverse covariance of the parameters and report $\delta a^2_{\mathrm{rms}}=\nabla_\theta a^{\mathsf T}\mathcal H^{-1}\nabla_\theta a$, a standard Bayesian error bar translated from statistics to the variational parameters.
* **Lineage**: Operation B.3, Round 3.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at G2 (2026-11-12)
* **Kill rule**: Gate 2.
* **Evidence**: It lives in the tangent space (lemma), and a stiff foil has large curvature along $\nabla_\theta a$, so it gets a small error bar exactly when it is wrong (the R1.2 mechanism).
* **Why this kills it**: It measures parameter uncertainty within the family, not the family's bias.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: Posterior error bars over a family large enough to contain $\psi_0$.
* **Strongest case against the kill**: None.
* **Checked by**: per-leaf gate pass, Round 3.
