# R2.6 — Zero-variance zero-bias improved estimators (Route 2.6) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Use the Assaraf–Caffarel zero-variance zero-bias estimators, which add a control variate built from an improved trial function, to correct $\langle\hat A\rangle$ and read the size of the correction as the error of the trained state.
* **Lineage**: Round 2 batch; mutated into R3.4 (in-family improved function).
* **Outcome**: KILLED at G0 (2026-11-11)
* **Kill rule**: Gate 0.
* **Evidence**: Established method (arXiv:cond-mat/9911396; arXiv:physics/0310035); its bias stays linear in the trial-function error.
* **Why this kills it**: It sharpens the statistical estimate of $a_\psi$ but does not estimate $\delta$.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: A zero-bias correction with an improved function outside the tangent space (R3.2).
* **Strongest case against the kill**: None.
* **Checked by**: per-leaf gate pass, Round 2.
* **Lesson**: route-specific — a known property of the cited estimator.
