# R2.3 — Cross-width zero-variance extrapolation (Route 2.3) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Fit $a$ against $\sigma^2$ across NNBF widths and read the zero-variance intercept as the corrected value, which is how NQS correlation biases are judged in practice, turning a width scan into an error bar.
* **Lineage**: Operation B.3, translation of current practice (Round 2). Kept as control arm XW; mutated into R3.1.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at G2 (2026-11-11)
* **Kill rule**: Gates 0 and 2, with the pre-registered prediction "XW under-states on at least two thirds of the widest NNBF states".
* **Evidence**: P1.1 (job 329057): width removes amplitude error so $\sigma^2$ falls two- to threefold, while the sign-borne largest bond-pair error moves only from 0.0273 (width 8) to 0.0255 (width 128) on the $N_\uparrow=N_\downarrow=4$ cluster. Smoke: XW under-stated on 3 of 3 widest states.
* **Why this kills it**: The fit follows a nearly flat line and certifies the current correlation.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: Ladders whose error falls monotonically without rotating (tested as R6.1).
* **Strongest case against the kill**: It is used successfully for energies. Answered: the energy error is second order and unsigned, the observable error first order and signed.
* **Checked by**: per-leaf gate pass and smoke prediction, Round 2.
