# R3.4 — In-family zero-bias correction (Route 3.4) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Take the improved function of a zero-bias estimator to be the variational response of the trained parameters to the field $h\hat A$, so that the correction uses only the ansatz the run already has and no extra network.
* **Lineage**: Operation A.1 on R2.6 (Round 3); motivated R3.2.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at G1 (2026-11-12)
* **Kill rule**: Gate 1.
* **Evidence**: Tangent-space blindness lemma (Round 3): at a stationary point $\langle\partial_k\psi\vert r\rangle=0$ for every parameter, so $\langle r\vert y\rangle=0$ for any $y$ in the tangent span.
* **Why this kills it**: The correction vanishes identically for a converged state.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: Responses outside the tangent space.
* **Strongest case against the kill**: Real states are not exactly stationary. Answered: the correction is then of the size of the optimisation residual, not of $\delta$.
* **Checked by**: per-leaf gate pass, Round 3 (proof in the leaf).
* **Lesson**: L13 (`library.md`)
