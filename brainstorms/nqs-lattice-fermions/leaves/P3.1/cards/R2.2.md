# R2.2 — Sign-defect Ritz (Route 2.2) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Mark configurations where the local sign-consistency field $\kappa=(\hat H_{\mathrm{off}}\psi)/\psi$ is positive (empty for every low eigenstate by a lemma), add the restriction of $\psi$ to that set to the Lanczos span, and let the Ritz energy decide how strongly those configurations flip sign.
* **Lineage**: Operation B.1 (Krylov space to sign-gauge space), Round 2, motivated by P1.1 (sign carries most of the pair error). Its first draft is R2.4.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at CRITIC (2026-11-11)
* **Kill rule**: Round 2 per-leaf critic objection: is $\kappa>0$ a sign witness?
* **Evidence**: For $\psi=\psi_0(1+\varepsilon)$ with exact signs, $\kappa$ is unbounded with either sign near nodes, so the set fires on amplitude error; a coherent wrong-sign domain has $\kappa<0$ inside. After symmetry projection the target vanished: P1.1-P measured sign-mismatch weight at most $5\times10^{-7}$.
* **Why this kills it**: The set is a set of local-energy outliers, not a witness, and there was no sign error left to witness on the projected states.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: A sign witness on states that keep a projected sign fraction of at least 0.5 (R3.5's reopening condition).
* **Strongest case against the kill**: The lemma that $\kappa<0$ for eigenstates is exact. Answered: its converse fails; the code never measured overlap with the true wrong-sign set.
* **Checked by**: per-leaf critic, Round 2 (2026-11-11).
* **Lesson**: route-specific — the failure is the missing converse of one lemma about local energies.
