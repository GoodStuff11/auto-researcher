# R7.4 — Diagonal-ensemble (real-time dephasing) estimator (Route 7.4) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Evolve the state in real time; the time average of $a(t)$ removes the cross terms between $\psi_0$ and the excited components, so its difference from $a_\psi$ reveals the error with an effective filter degree $WT$ far beyond the S2 limit.
* **Lineage**: Round 7 batch.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at G1 (2026-11-12)
* **Kill rule**: Gate 1.
* **Evidence**: With an NQS the dynamics is the projected (TDVP) flow; at the trained energy minimum the force vanishes, so the state is a fixed point, $a(t)=a_\psi$ and $\hat\delta=0$. A kick gives the tangent-space linear response (R7.2).
* **Why this kills it**: The variational dynamics cannot leave a converged minimum.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: Exact or non-variational real-time evolution.
* **Strongest case against the kill**: None.
