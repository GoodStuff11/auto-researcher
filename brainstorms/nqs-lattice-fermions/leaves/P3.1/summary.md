* **The question in plain terms**: A variational calculation of a doped Hubbard or $t$–$J$ model gives a trained state $\psi$ whose energy can be excellent while its long-range pair, spin or charge correlation $a_\psi=\langle\hat A\rangle_\psi$ is wrong; that is the failure mode the brainstorm exists to avoid. The leaf asks whether the error $\delta=a_0-a_\psi$ against the exact value $a_0$ can be estimated, or honestly refused, from cheap information a VMC run can afford, at cost polynomial in the volume $V$. On the shared instance ($4\times4$ open $t$–$t'$–$J$, $6+6$ electrons), converged width-6 projected NNBF states have relative tail errors of 31% (pair), 15% (spin) and 22% (charge) at squared overlap 0.94 with the ground state (job 451837).
* **Why the obvious tools fail**: Write $\psi=\alpha\psi_0+\sum_nc_n\lvert n\rangle$ over in-sector eigenstates at excitation $\omega_n$. Then $\delta\approx-2\alpha\sum_nc_nA_{n0}$ depends on the signs of the $c_n$, while every energy moment depends only on $\lvert c_n\rvert^2$ (S1), and a degree-$K$ polynomial filter in $\hat H$ changes a component by at most $2K^2\omega_n/W$ of the spectral width $W$ (S2). On the real state 83–141% of $\delta$ sits below $1t$, against 31% of the energy error (job 452272), and one and two Lanczos steps halve the energy error while moving the pair error by under 10%. This single mechanism explains the kills of variance extrapolation (R1.1), covariance and moment regressors (R3.1, R3.7, R5.3, R6.3), width, seed and field ensembles that share their bias (R2.3, R6.4, R7.7, R9.4), and all tangent-space or variational-dynamics estimates, which vanish at a converged minimum (R3.4, R3.6, R7.4, R7.5, R8.2). S1 and S2 are established sub-results; the per-leaf critic left them standing when it refused the close.
* **What does carry the information, and its price**: Two carriers see the low-energy error. (i) A window of trained excited states (R7.1, ESR0): trained with the ground state's own network they reach $\varepsilon_k/\omega_k=0.13$–0.18 and match the exact window (pair ratio 1.17 against 1.06; job 456094), but the window needed grows with the level density (toy median 2 to 17.5 levels for pair; 11 levels for charge on the real state), so it fails Gate 4. (ii) A per-observable static response $y\propto(\hat H-s)^{-1}(\hat A-a)\phi$, which reaches every low level at once. Applied once to a fixed base it is not size-consistent (R10.1) and its training noise is amplified by dense windows (R8.1, R9.1); iterated with the base updated each pass (R10.1u, carrier R10.1d) it tracked 0.89, 0.93 and 1.00 of toy items at $V=10$, 12, 14 and 8 of 8 real $4\times4$ tail errors with exact responses (job 699756).
