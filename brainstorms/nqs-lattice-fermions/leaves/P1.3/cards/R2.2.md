# R2.2 — Fidelity-capped deflation excursions, FCD (round-2 Route 2.2) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: From the incumbent state, run $X$ SR steps on $E(\theta)+\lambda\max(0,F(\theta)-c)^2$, where $F$ is the normalised fidelity with the incumbent, $c$ a cap and $\lambda$ a strength. This pushes the whole network a bounded distance out of its basin; it is then released for $r$ plain SR steps, scored by the fixed-node energy and kept if better. Unlike R2.1 it moves the network, not the reference.
* **Lineage**: Operation B.3, cross-field translation from SCF metadynamics and deflation (round 2). Repaired in round 3 as R3.2 (projection added).
* **Source analogy**: SCF metadynamics biases a self-consistent-field search away from known solutions (Thom and Head-Gordon, PRL 101, 193001, 2008); deflation removes known solutions of nonlinear PDEs so Newton's method finds new ones (Farrell, Birkisson and Funke, SIAM J. Sci. Comput. 37, A2026, 2015). Correspondence: known solution ↔ incumbent NQS; deflation factor ↔ capped fidelity penalty. It works there because every distinct solution is wanted. Gap: a ground-state search wants a lower state, and full deflation drives the network to an excited state; the cap was meant to bridge that (Analogy Map B1).
* **Outcome**: KILLED at G0 (2026-11-11, by the round-2 critic).
* **Kill rule**: Gate 0: is it known, and is the adoption barrier fixed?
* **Evidence**: The critic found that a capped penalty is SCF metadynamics with a cap and has no independent mechanism; its acceptance is R2.1's fixed-node score, killed at the same time. On the smoke run the excursion was accepted once (cap 0.8) and still ended worse than plain SR (sign-error reduction $-0.038$, one seed, uninformative by design).
* **Why this kills it**: Without a mechanism beyond the cap, the route is a tuning of a known method, and its acceptance is not usable.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: the same excursion inside the target sector with a sampled acceptance (R3.2, killed separately).
* **Strongest case against the kill**: a bounded excursion followed by release is a different dynamic from full deflation, which converges to excited states (not raised by a critic). Unresolved as a mechanism question; no test isolated the cap.
* **Checked by**: the round-2 per-leaf critic (2026-11-11).
* **Lesson**: route-specific — a novelty kill of one construction; the general point about deflation is carried by R3.2's card.
