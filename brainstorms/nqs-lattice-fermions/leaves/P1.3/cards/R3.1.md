# R3.1 — Symmetric natural-orbital re-anchoring of a projected NNBF with sampled-energy acceptance, SNO-PE (round-3 Route 3.1) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Train the symmetry-projected state $\psi_P=P_SP_\chi\psi_\theta$ (summed over the lattice group, irrep $\chi$, and projected to total spin $S$). Each cycle, estimate its one-body density matrix from 2000 samples, symmetrise and diagonalise it, and build four candidate references from the natural orbitals. Relax each for 40 projected-SR steps and accept a candidate only if its sampled mean energy beats the incumbent by two combined standard errors. All costs count against the budget, and a null runs the same loop without density-matrix feedback.
* **Lineage**: Operation A.1 (acceptance) and A.3 (baseline) on R2.1 (round 3). Kept dormant in round 5 pending a BASIN verdict that never came.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at T3 (2026-11-15: its premise failed the pre-registered basin tests).
* **Kill rule**: Pre-registered in round 3: "Routes are evaluated only under BASIN. Under NO-BASIN their verdict is 'N/A (premise falsified)'". Round 5: SNO-PE "is the first candidate to re-audit if the verdict is BASIN", and "If width 6 gives CAPACITY through K2 or K3, P1.3 closes as CAPACITY", with the width-trend tie-break "if the error is width-limited ($C_{\mathrm{best}}(10)\le0.5\,C_{\mathrm{best}}(4)$) the verdict is CAPACITY".
* **Evidence**: Job 410199 (T2, 4×3 Hubbard torus, $4+4$, in-sector gaps $0.234t$ and $0.273t$): all 9 projected cold runs reached $\psi_0$, with energy error at most $2.7\times10^{-5}t$ and overlap at least 0.999998. Jobs 507179 and 625182 (T3, 4×4 open $t$–$t'$–$J$ cluster, $6+6$, $t'=-0.25$, $J=0.4$, three seeds per width): the best cold sign error $C_{\mathrm{best}}$ was 0.0192, 0.0117 and 0.0070 at widths 4, 6 and 10 (1284, 1734 and 2634 parameters). An oracle arm W, first trained toward $\psi_0$ and then released for the same energy steps, ended at 0.0189, 0.0115 and 0.0070, inside margins of 0.003 to 0.005. Every width stayed UNCLEAR-CONV after its 600-step resume, so the tie-break applied: $0.0070\le0.5\times0.0192=0.0096$, verdict CAPACITY.
* **Why this kills it**: The route escapes basins; on every probed instance no basin was found (none on the tori, a capacity-limited error on the 4×4 instance), so by the pre-registered rule it is never evaluated and has no target.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: basins beyond ED size, where projection is partial; same-sector competitors at gaps near $10^{-3}t$ per site; a basin on the 4×4 instance found by a direct probe (R7.7, shelved).
* **Strongest case against the kill**: CAPACITY came from a tie-break with every width unconverged, and W never started below the cold level (its start was 0.0213, 0.0195 and 0.0138 against cold 0.0194, 0.0117 and 0.0075), so the basin was never directly probed (cross-node critic, 2026-11-16). Partly answered by the round-6 probe and R7.1 on a toy; unresolved on the 4×4 instance.
