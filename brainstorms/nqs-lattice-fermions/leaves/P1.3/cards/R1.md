# R1 — Lanczos-target sign refit (round-1 Route 1) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Every $K$ SR steps, build the one-step Lanczos state $\psi_L=\psi_\theta+\alpha H\psi_\theta$, with $\alpha$ chosen by a $2\times2$ Rayleigh–Ritz problem, and fit only the determinant (sign-carrying) parameters of the NQS to $\psi_L$ by maximising the fidelity, keeping the result if the energy drops. It was expected to work because a finite projector step can flip signs, which an infinitesimal SR step never does.
* **Lineage**: initial batch (round 1, 2026-11-10). Not mutated further.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at G0 (2026-11-10).
* **Kill rule**: Gate 0: is the idea known, and is the reason it is not standard practice fixed?
* **Evidence**: Fitting a network to a finite projector step is supervised wave-function optimisation (SWO, Kochkov and Clark, arXiv:1811.12423) and the self-learning power method (Giuliani et al., DOI:10.22331/q-2023-08-29-1096); holding the target fixed until the energy drops is Ledinauskas and Anisimovas (arXiv:2307.15521). The ratio $\psi_L(\mathbf n)/\psi_\theta(\mathbf n)=1+\alpha E_{\mathrm{loc}}(\mathbf n)$, with $E_{\mathrm{loc}}=(H\psi_\theta)/\psi_\theta$ the local energy, changes sign only where $\alpha E_{\mathrm{loc}}(\mathbf n)<-1$, a condition on single configurations.
* **Why this kills it**: The two differences from prior art (the Lanczos-optimal $\alpha$ and the restriction of the fit to the determinant) leave untouched the reason these methods have not replaced SR: the fit is still a gradient flow in the same parametrisation, and the target flips signs only locally, while fermionic sign basins differ by global moves of the nodal surface.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: targets built from several Krylov steps, whose sign changes are not local; and refits in a different parametrisation of the sign.
* **Strongest case against the kill**: a fit restricted to the determinant might move the nodal surface globally even when the target flips only local signs, because a determinant couples all configurations (not raised by a critic). Unresolved as stated, and moot at ED size after R3.1's premise tests found no basin to escape.
