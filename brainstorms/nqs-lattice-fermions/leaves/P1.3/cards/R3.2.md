# R3.2 — In-sector orthogonal-complement probe, OCP (round-3 Route 3.2) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: After 600 projected-SR steps, train a second projected NNBF from a Fermi-sea reference on its energy plus $\lambda=2t$ times its fidelity with the incumbent. Both are in the ground state's sector, so the probe ends either in a lower state of that sector (an escape) or at the same-sector competitor (which certifies the incumbent). The two are compared by sampled energy and the winner continues; projection removes the failure of unprojected probes, which land in another sector.
* **Lineage**: Operation B.2 (active probing), round 3; a translation repair of R2.2 (deflation) by adding sector projection.
* **Source analogy**: deflation in numerical analysis (Farrell, Birkisson and Funke, SIAM J. Sci. Comput. 37, A2026, 2015) and overlap-penalty excited states in VMC and NQS (Pathak et al., J. Chem. Phys. 154, 034101, 2021; Choo et al., arXiv:1807.03325). There the target is another eigenstate; here the probe was to certify or beat the ground state of one sector. Gap: the probe still finds a different state, not a lower one, unless the incumbent is not the sector's ground state (Analogy Map B1).
* **Outcome**: KILLED at G0 (2026-11-12, round-3 per-leaf critic).
* **Kill rule**: Gate 0: is it known, and is the adoption barrier fixed?
* **Evidence**: The critic found it is SCF metadynamics or deflation, the family already killed as R2.2; arXiv:2605.30944 already combines symmetry projection with an orthogonality penalty; and it cannot escape a basin that is a mixture of two diabatic states. Its premise also failed: 410199 found no trapped run on the tori. On the smoke run its penalty ablation changed nothing (median difference 0.000 over 2 seeds).
* **Why this kills it**: The construction is known, and no instance had a basin for it to escape.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: its use as a certificate that the incumbent is the sector's ground state, which is a diagnostic, not an escape.
* **Strongest case against the kill**: no source found uses the probe as a ground-state escape inside a matched budget (the leaf's own Gate-0 note). Answered by the critic: the use is new but the mechanism is not, and the premise was false.
