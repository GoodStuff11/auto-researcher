# R7.1 — Second-order sign-oracle reachability probe (reopening Route R7.1) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Fit a projected width-3 NNBF with Levenberg–Marquardt (LM, a damped Gauss–Newton method using the exact Jacobian over the whole basis) to the sign-oracle target $\mathrm{sgn}(\psi_0)\lvert\psi_P(S)\rvert$: the cold state S's own amplitudes with the exact signs. If even this fit cannot halve the sign error, no markedly better-signed state exists in the family and the round-6 miss was not a first-order artefact; if it can, release under energy SR decides whether a basin exists.
* **Lineage**: Operation A on reopening ingredient I2 (with I1 and I3), 2026-11-16. A premise probe, not an optimiser route; it repairs the planted-solution test of the round-5 oracle arm (Analogy Map B3).
* **Outcome**: KILLED at T1 (2026-11-16).
* **Kill rule**: Pre-registered in the docstring of `t1_reopen.py`: LM-MISS (leaf stays closed) if the median best LM checkpoint is above half the cold start's sign error, here $0.5\times0.01090=0.00545$.
* **Evidence**: Projected 4×3 open $t$–$J$ toy, $5+5$ electrons, Hilbert dimension 16,632, gap $0.085t$, 675 parameters, seeds 11, 22 and 33. Cold start S: 0.01098, 0.00930 and 0.01090. First-order SR oracle (round 6): 0.00950, 0.00793 and 0.00916. LM best: 0.00905, 0.00776 and 0.00854 (median 0.00854, 22% below cold), with fidelity 0.9920 to 0.9921 against SR's 0.9913 to 0.9917. The LM damping had fallen to $10^{-10}$ to $10^{-15}$ (pure Gauss–Newton) while the fidelity moved only in the fourth decimal. Released for 300 SR steps, the states returned to the cold level (0.01054, 0.00852, 0.01072 against cold 0.01039, 0.00805, 0.01125) at energies $0.7$ to $1.2\times10^{-3}t$ above the cold continuation. Cost 489 CPU-s for the whole review.
* **Why this kills it**: LM beat SR on fidelity on 3 of 3 seeds, so the fit is valid, and it still missed the threshold by a wide margin while taking full Gauss–Newton steps, so the plateau belongs to the width-3 family. With no markedly better-signed state reachable, there is no basin to probe on this toy.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: wider networks on instances that still have a target, above all the 4×4 instance at widths 4 to 10 (R7.7); other ansatz families.
* **Strongest case against the kill**: the toy sits at the sector-fitting edge (675 parameters against 724 symmetry- and spin-adapted states), so its result may not transfer to the 4×4 instance (per-leaf critic, 2026-11-16). Unresolved; the 4×4 probe is R7.7.
