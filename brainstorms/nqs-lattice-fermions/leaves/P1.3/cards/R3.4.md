# R3.4 — Projector annealing (round-3 Route 3.4) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Train $(1-\alpha)\psi_\theta+\alpha P\psi_\theta$ with the symmetry projector $P$ and anneal $\alpha$ from 1 to 0, so training enjoys the projected landscape but the final state is unprojected and costs one amplitude per configuration instead of $\lvert G\rvert$, the size of the symmetry group.
* **Lineage**: Operation A.2 (round 3). Handed to P4 as a cost idea. Its pattern recurred as R7.4.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at G2 (2026-11-11).
* **Kill rule**: Gate 2: can a cheap baseline or the foil reach the same metric? Here: can the test measure the route's only claim?
* **Evidence**: An unprojected final state cannot beat the fully projected control on accuracy, so its only claim is cost. In the exact-sum tests projection costs nothing extra (round-3 rule 5), so the cost claim cannot be measured.
* **Why this kills it**: The projected control dominates on the measurable metric, and the unmeasurable one is not this leaf's question.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: a cost gain at sizes where $\lvert G\rvert$ is large (512 on 8×8), measured with sampled amplitudes.
* **Strongest case against the kill**: at 8×8 the cost is exactly what matters (not raised by a critic). Agreed; that is why it was handed to P4.
