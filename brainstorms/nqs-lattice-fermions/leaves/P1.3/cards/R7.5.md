# R7.5 — Replica-exchange SR (reopening Route R7.5) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: Run several SR replicas at different noise or driver strengths (temperatures) and periodically swap their parameters, as in parallel tempering, so a hot replica crosses barriers between sign basins and hands a better basin to the cold replica.
* **Lineage**: Operation B.3, cross-field translation from statistical-physics Monte Carlo (2026-11-16).
* **Source analogy**: parallel tempering for rugged landscapes (Hukushima and Nemoto, J. Phys. Soc. Jpn. 65, 1604, 1996): temperature ↔ SR noise; replica ↔ network copy. It works there because a better basin exists and swaps cost little. Gap: each NQS replica is a full optimisation, and no probed instance had a better basin (Analogy Map B1).
* **Outcome**: KILLED at G0 (2026-11-16; also at T0 by dominance).
* **Kill rule**: Gate 0 (known, adoption barrier not fixed), and a T0 dominance argument: tempering helps only if a better basin exists.
* **Evidence**: Quantum-inspired tempering for NQS exists (arXiv:2210.11405; adaptive temperatures in arXiv:2410.23018); it is not standard because each replica is a full optimisation and gains are shown only at exact sizes. No probed instance has a better basin (410199; 507179 and 625182; round 6; R7.1; R7.3).
* **Why this kills it**: The route fixes nothing about its known cost, and its benefit needs a basin that the evidence says is absent at ED size.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: tempering at sizes where basins may exist.
* **Strongest case against the kill**: tempering could also help convergence speed (not raised by a critic). That is not this leaf's question.
* **Checked by**: not reviewed by a critic.
* **Lesson**: L2 (`library.md`)
