# R7.3 — Same-sector competitor capture at a small in-sector gap (reopening Route R7.3) (KILLED)
* **The idea**: After projection, the only basin that can survive is a same-sector competitor, an eigenstate with the ground state's symmetry labels. On a gapped toy with the smallest in-sector gap available, run projected cold SR from three references and count runs that settle on the competitor; an oracle arm started at $\psi_0$ and released tests whether the right state is kept.
* **Lineage**: Operation A on reopening ingredient I6 (2026-11-16), the competitor half of the Hard Core.
* **Outcome**: KILLED at T1 (2026-11-16).
* **Kill rule**: Pre-registered in `t1_reopen.py`: a run is captured if its energy error is at least $0.5\Delta_{\mathrm{in}}$ and its overlap with the competitor exceeds its overlap with $\psi_0$; NO-BASIN if no cold run is captured.
* **Evidence**: Same 4×3 toy at $t'=-0.15$, in-sector gap $\Delta_{\mathrm{in}}=0.1217t$ (a singlet competitor), width 3, 300 energy steps per arm. Cold runs (UHF seed 11, UHF seed 22, Fermi sea): energy errors 0.0635, 0.0688 and 0.0438, overlaps with $\psi_0$ 0.925, 0.842 and 0.880, with the competitor 0.042, 0.127 and 0.103; none captured. The released oracle kept a better sign (0.0052 against 0.0084 to 0.0158) but sat $0.018t$ above the best cold run in energy, level with the UHF runs (one seed).
* **Why this kills it**: Zero of three cold runs was captured at a gap twice as close to the Hard Core as job 410199's, so on this toy there is no competitor basin to escape.
* **What the kill does not rule out**: capture at gaps near $10^{-3}t$ per site, which ED-size clusters do not offer; larger sizes, where same-sector gaps shrink and captures can only become more likely.
* **Strongest case against the kill**: three runs and one oracle seed are few, and the cold runs' residual lies mostly along the competitor (overlap 4–13%), so a partial capture exists (not raised by a critic). Answered only in scope: the pre-registered rule counts full captures.
