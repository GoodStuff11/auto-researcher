# Subproblem: P1.1 Sign versus amplitude error budget
**Parent Chain**: P1 -> P1.1  **Status**: Completed
### 1. Mathematical Abstraction & Barriers
* **Formulation**:
  - *Inputs*: a number-conserving Hubbard Hamiltonian $\hat H$ on $V$ sites with $(N_\uparrow,N_\downarrow)$ fixed, written as a real symmetric matrix $H_{\mathbf n\mathbf n'}$ in the Jordan–Wigner-ordered occupation basis $\mathcal B$ (dimension $D$); its ground state $\psi_0$ with energy $E_0$; a real variational state $\psi_\theta$ (NNBF, HFDS or the foil) with energy $E_\theta$.
  - *Sign structure*: $s_\theta(\mathbf n)=\mathrm{sgn}\,\psi_\theta(\mathbf n)$, $S_\theta=\mathrm{diag}(s_\theta)$, and the sign-gauged matrix $K_\theta=S_\theta H S_\theta$.
  - *Sign-limited energy* (best energy any amplitude can reach with the signs of $\psi_\theta$):
$$E_{\mathrm{sign}}(s_\theta)=\min_{a\in\mathbb R_{\ge0}^{D}\setminus\{0\}}\frac{a^{\mathsf T}K_\theta a}{a^{\mathsf T}a}.$$
  - *Budget*: with $\Delta E_{\mathrm{tot}}=E_\theta-E_0$,
$$\Delta E_{\mathrm{sign}}=E_{\mathrm{sign}}(s_\theta)-E_0\ \ge 0,\qquad \Delta E_{\mathrm{amp}}=E_\theta-E_{\mathrm{sign}}(s_\theta)\ \ge 0,\qquad \Delta E_{\mathrm{sign}}+\Delta E_{\mathrm{amp}}=\Delta E_{\mathrm{tot}},$$
  and the sign fraction $f_E=\Delta E_{\mathrm{sign}}/\Delta E_{\mathrm{tot}}\in[0,1]$. Both pieces are non-negative by construction (the minimum over $a$ includes $a=\lvert\psi_\theta\rvert$ and is bounded below by $E_0$), so the split is additive and order-independent. It is invariant under any diagonal gauge $\mathbf n\mapsto\pm$ applied to both $\psi_0$ and $\psi_\theta$, so it does not depend on the Jordan–Wigner ordering.
  - *Pairing budget*: with $a^\star$ the minimizer, $P_\phi(b)=\langle\phi\rvert\hat\Delta^\dagger_{b_0}\hat\Delta_b\lvert\phi\rangle/\langle\phi\vert\phi\rangle$ for the singlet bond-pair operator $\hat\Delta_b=(\hat c_{j\downarrow}\hat c_{i\uparrow}-\hat c_{j\uparrow}\hat c_{i\downarrow})/\sqrt2$ on nearest-neighbour bond $b=(i,j)$ and reference bond $b_0$, define $e_C^{\mathrm{tot}}=\max_{b:\,d(b,b_0)\ge r_{\min}}\lvert P_{\psi_\theta}(b)-P_{\psi_0}(b)\rvert$, $e_C^{\mathrm{sign}}$ the same with $\psi_\theta\to s_\theta a^\star$, and $f_C=e_C^{\mathrm{sign}}/e_C^{\mathrm{tot}}$.
  - *Rigorous lower bound*: deleting the positive off-diagonal entries of $K_\theta$ gives a stoquastic matrix $K_\theta^{-}$, and since $a^{\mathsf T}(K_\theta-K_\theta^{-})a\ge0$ for $a\ge0$, $E_{\mathrm{stoq}}(s_\theta)=\lambda_{\min}(K^-_\theta)\le E_{\mathrm{sign}}(s_\theta)$. Hence $f_E^{\mathrm{lo}}=\max(0,E_{\mathrm{stoq}}-E_0)/\Delta E_{\mathrm{tot}}\le f_E$.
  - *Solved means*: for a state-of-the-art fermionic NQS on a doped, $t'\neq0$, 2D cluster at $U/t\in\{4,8\}$, $f_E$ and $f_C$ are known with an optimizer-calibrated error, together with their trend in network width $H$, so that the question "is P1 binding?" has an instance-wise yes/no answer.
* **The Node Barrier**:
  - *Fundamental*: computing $E_{\mathrm{sign}}(s)$ is a minimization of a quadratic form over the non-negative orthant, i.e. a copositivity-type problem, which is co-NP-complete in general (Murty & Kabadi, Math. Program. 39, 117, 1987, DOI:10.1007/BF02592948). It is also only defined relative to the exact $\psi_0$, so the budget needs ED (or an equivalent reference) and cannot be measured at the sizes where P1 matters. At ED sizes the problem is therefore bracketed, not solved: every feasible point (the L-BFGS-B results from four starts, and the self-consistent ten Haaf fixed-node state with sign $s_\theta$) gives a rigorous upper bound, the stoquastic bound gives a rigorous lower bound, and an exact-sign calibration ($s_\theta\to s_0$, start from uniform amplitudes, must return $E_0$) checks for local-minimum trouble.
  - *Practical*: (i) published fermionic NQS benchmarks report energies and V-scores, not sign accuracy (searches below found no sign-resolved budget for HFDS or NNBF on Hubbard); (ii) the spin-model literature uses hybrid swaps $s_0\lvert\psi_\theta\rvert$ and sign-overlap scores, which are not additive and saturate with $V$; (iii) lattice fixed-node (ten Haaf et al., PRB 51, 13039, 1995) is not a sign-only quantity on a lattice, because the sign-flip term needs the guiding amplitudes, so $E_{\mathrm{VMC}}-E_{\mathrm{FN}}$ mixes the two error sources.
  - *Anti-Solution Space*: (a) declaring "signs are the problem" from a sign-overlap score $\sum_{\mathbf n}\lvert\psi_0\rvert^2[s_\theta=s_0]$ (it tends to $0$ for any fixed per-site accuracy as $V$ grows, and carries no energy weight); (b) budgets measured at half filling with $t'=0$, in 1D, or at $U=0$ (Hard Core); (c) budgets for a single, under-trained network (the answer then measures the optimizer, not the ansatz class), which is why the test sweeps width; (d) any decomposition whose two parts can be negative or depend on order.
### 2. Candidate Routes & Falsification Gates
* **Route 1**: *Sign-constrained optimal-amplitude budget with a copositive bracket.* Bound $E_{\mathrm{sign}}(s_\theta)$ from above by the lowest of bound-constrained L-BFGS-B over the full basis from four starts ($\lvert\psi_\theta\rvert$, $\lvert\psi_0\rvert$, uniform, the ten Haaf fixed-node state) and the fixed-node energy itself, bracket it from below by the stoquastic bound $E_{\mathrm{stoq}}(s_\theta)$, calibrate the optimizer on $s_0$, and evaluate $f_E$, $f_C$ for UHF, the Jastrow–Slater foil, and NNBF at widths $H\in\{8,32,128\}$ on 2D doped $t$–$t'$–$U$ clusters. Point of departure: the spin-model sign/amplitude studies only swap or fix signs; here the sign part is defined as a variational minimum, so the split is additive, non-negative and gauge-invariant.
  - Gate 0 (Novelty & Adoption): PASS. Related work: Westerhout et al. (Nat. Commun. 11, 1593, 2020; arXiv:1907.08186) showed that sign generalization is harder than amplitude generalization for frustrated spins; Szabó & Castelnovo (PRR 2, 033075, 2020; arXiv:2002.04613) separated phase and modulus networks; Bukov, Schmitt & Dupont (SciPost Phys. 10, 147, 2021; arXiv:2011.11214) attributed failure to a rugged landscape over the sign; arXiv:2308.09664 optimized amplitudes at fixed signs for $J_1$–$J_2$. None of these is fermionic, and none gives an additive energy budget or a pairing-correlation budget. Targeted searches for HFDS/NNBF/transformer-backflow Hubbard papers (Luo & Clark PRL 122, 226401; Robledo Moreno et al. PNAS 119, e2122059119; Lange et al. arXiv:2411.10430; Loehr & Clark arXiv:2510.26906; Viteritti et al. arXiv:2604.21978) found energies only, with no sign-resolved error. Adoption barrier of the existing tools: swap metrics are non-additive and order-dependent, and lattice FN is not sign-only. The concrete fix is the variational definition of $E_{\mathrm{sign}}$ together with the stoquastic lower bound and the exact-sign calibration.
  - Gate 1 (Conservation & Asymptotics): PASS. Particle number and $S^z$ are exact (fixed-sector basis); the budget is invariant under diagonal gauge changes; no polynomial-time claim is made: the method is explicitly exponential (ED-sized), and it is a diagnostic that answers a routing question, not a solver. The co-NP-hardness of the inner problem is handled by bracketing ($E_{\mathrm{stoq}}\le E_{\mathrm{sign}}\le E_{\mathrm{sign}}^{\mathrm{up}}\le E_{\mathrm{FN}}$), so $f_E^{\mathrm{up}}$ is a rigorous upper bound and $f_E^{\mathrm{lo}}$ a rigorous lower bound on the sign fraction.
  - Gate 2 (Baseline Gameability): PASS. The metric is state-specific, and the foil and UHF are measured in the same run; the diagnostic is informative only if it separates them from NNBF, which the smoke test already shows (UHF $f_E=0.033$, foil $0.231$, NNBF $0.141$ to $0.205$ on a $3\times2$ cluster). A foil cannot "hit" a routing answer: if the foil and NNBF had identical budgets, that itself would be a result (the backflow adds nothing to signs). The pre-committed $M$ uses only the widest NNBF.
  - Gate 3 (Hard-Core Shield): PASS. Instances are 2D clusters with $t'=-0.25t$, hole doping $1/6$ and $1/3$, and $U/t\in\{4,8\}$; the doped 2D $t$–$t'$ Hubbard model has no known stoquastic diagonal gauge, so each $K_\theta$ is expected to keep positive off-diagonals (sign-violating bonds) and the inner problem is a genuine sign-constrained one. In the smoke run this shows up as $E_{\mathrm{stoq}}<E_{\mathrm{sign}}$ for every state. Nothing relies on half filling, 1D or $U=0$; the $U=0$ and two-site cases appear only as correctness checks.
  - *Verdict*: SURVIVED
* **Route 2**: *Hybrid sign/amplitude swap (Shapley) budget.* Evaluate $E[s_\theta\lvert\psi_0\rvert]$ (variational signs, exact amplitudes) and $E[s_0\lvert\psi_\theta\rvert]$ (exact signs, variational amplitudes) and assign the energy error by the two-player Shapley average of the swaps.
  - Gate 0 (Novelty & Adoption): FAIL. Swapping or fixing signs is the existing spin-model practice (arXiv:1907.08186; arXiv:2308.09664). It is not standard for fermions because $\lvert\psi_\theta\rvert$ is co-adapted to the wrong signs: $E[s_0\lvert\psi_\theta\rvert]$ can be higher than $E_\theta$, so the "amplitude part" can be negative and the split depends on order. The route has no mechanism that fixes this. (Both swap energies are still printed by the test as a cross-check.)
  - Gate 1 (Conservation & Asymptotics): not evaluated (killed at Gate 0).
  - Gate 2 (Baseline Gameability): not evaluated.
  - Gate 3 (Hard-Core Shield): not evaluated.
  - *Verdict*: KILLED
* **Route 3**: *Sign-mismatch weight as the budget.* Use $w_{\mathrm{sign}}=\sum_{\mathbf n:\,s_\theta s_0=-1}\lvert\psi_0(\mathbf n)\rvert^2$ (or the sign overlap $\sum_{\mathbf n}\lvert\psi_0\rvert^2s_\theta s_0$) as the measure of how much error the signs carry.
  - Gate 0 (Novelty & Adoption): PASS. Sign-accuracy scores are used in spin NQS (arXiv:1907.08186) but not reported for fermionic NQS; an energy-weighted version would be new.
  - Gate 1 (Conservation & Asymptotics): FAIL. For any ansatz with a fixed per-site sign accuracy, $w_{\mathrm{sign}}$ grows to its saturation value exponentially in $V$ (orthogonality-catastrophe argument: the probability of a fully correct sign pattern over $V$ weakly correlated regions decays as $e^{-cV}$), so the metric stops discriminating states at moderate $V$. It is also not an energy quantity: a sign error on configurations connected only by small $\lvert H_{\mathbf n\mathbf n'}\rvert$ costs almost nothing, so $w_{\mathrm{sign}}$ cannot answer "what fraction of $\Delta E_{\mathrm{tot}}$".
  - Gate 2 (Baseline Gameability): not evaluated.
  - Gate 3 (Hard-Core Shield): not evaluated.
  - *Verdict*: KILLED
* **Route 4**: *Scalable budget by lattice fixed-node GFMC with the NQS as guiding function.* Run fixed-node projector QMC from $\psi_\theta$ and read $E_\theta-E_{\mathrm{FN}}$ as the amplitude part and $E_{\mathrm{FN}}-E_{\mathrm{ref}}$ as the sign part, with $E_{\mathrm{ref}}$ from DMRG/AFQMC, at sizes beyond ED.
  - Gate 0 (Novelty & Adoption): FAIL. DMC on top of NNBF is already done (Loehr & Clark arXiv:2510.26906), and the lattice FN energy is not a sign-only functional: the ten Haaf sign-flip potential $\sum_{\mathbf n'}H_{\mathbf n\mathbf n'}\psi_\theta(\mathbf n')/\psi_\theta(\mathbf n)$ over sign-violating pairs depends on the guiding amplitudes, so $E_{\mathrm{FN}}$ is not the best energy at fixed signs (ten Haaf et al., PRB 51, 13039, 1995). That is exactly why FN energies are not read as sign budgets, and this route has no mechanism that removes the amplitude dependence.
  - Gate 1 (Conservation & Asymptotics): not evaluated.
  - Gate 2 (Baseline Gameability): not evaluated.
  - Gate 3 (Hard-Core Shield): not evaluated.
  - *Verdict*: KILLED
### 3. Top Surviving Route & Sharpest Gap
* **Mechanism**: Route 1. On ED-sized doped 2D clusters, every state is evaluated exactly over the full basis, and the energy error is split into a sign-limited part $\Delta E_{\mathrm{sign}}=E_{\mathrm{sign}}(s_\theta)-E_0$ (best non-negative amplitudes under the NQS signs) and an amplitude part $\Delta E_{\mathrm{amp}}=E_\theta-E_{\mathrm{sign}}(s_\theta)$. The same optimal state $s_\theta a^\star$ gives the sign-limited pairing error. The split is bracketed from below by a stoquastic bound and calibrated on the exact signs. Sweeping NNBF width tells which part stops decreasing with capacity, which is the operational meaning of "binding constraint".
* **Sharpest Gap**: unverified assumption that the budget measured on $D\sim6\times10^5$ clusters transfers to the $4\times16$ to $8\times8$ sizes where P1 matters. Two specific risks: (i) at small $V$ a modest NNBF can represent nearly exact signs, so $f_E$ may be small here and grow with $V$ (the smoke run already shows $f_E$ falling with width on a 6-site cluster); (ii) $E_{\mathrm{stoq}}$ is loose (it gave $f_E^{\mathrm{lo}}=0$ in the smoke run), so there is no scalable, sign-only bound yet. A missing lemma that would close both: a bound on $E_{\mathrm{sign}}(s)-E_{\mathrm{stoq}}(s)$ in terms of the weight of sign-violating bonds, which would make $E_{\mathrm{stoq}}$ (computable by sign-free projector QMC at large $V$) a usable proxy.
### 4. 24-Hour Decisive Test & Compute Execution
* **Minimal Instance**: $4\times3$ square lattice, periodic in both directions ($V=12$, includes closed plaquette loops and diagonal $t'$ bonds, hence non-stoquastic and 2D), $t=1$, $t'=-0.25$. Three pre-committed instances: (0) $N_\uparrow=N_\downarrow=5$ (doping $\delta=1/6$), $U=8$, $D=627{,}264$; (1) $N_\uparrow=N_\downarrow=4$ ($\delta=1/3$), $U=8$, $D=245{,}025$; (2) $\delta=1/6$, $U=4$. States: UHF Slater; Jastrow–Slater foil (distance-resolved density Jastrow plus Gutzwiller factor, UHF-initialized orbitals trainable); NNBF (Luo & Clark form, one $\tanh$ hidden layer of width $H\in\{8,32,128\}$ producing configuration-dependent orbital shifts). All optimized by SR with the exact full-sum force and a 2000-sample metric (Woodbury solve), 1500 iterations.
* **Scalar Metric $M$**: everything decisive is evaluated on the NNBF with $H=128$. The sign-limited energy is bracketed by two rigorous bounds:
$$E_{\mathrm{stoq}}(s_\theta)\le E_{\mathrm{sign}}(s_\theta)\le E_{\mathrm{sign}}^{\mathrm{up}}(s_\theta)=\min\Big(\min_{k}E^{\mathrm{L\text{-}BFGS}}_{k},\ \langle H\rangle_{\mathrm{FN}},\ E_{\mathrm{FN}}\Big)\le E_{\mathrm{FN}}(s_\theta).$$
Here $E^{\mathrm{L\text{-}BFGS}}_{k}$ is the bound-constrained L-BFGS-B result from start $k\in\{\lvert\psi_\theta\rvert,\lvert\psi_0\rvert,\text{uniform},\lvert\phi_{\mathrm{FN}}\rvert\}$, and $E_{\mathrm{FN}}$ is the self-consistent ten Haaf lattice fixed-node energy for the sign $s_\theta$ (PRB 51, 13039, 1995): the sign-violating entries $(K_\theta)_{\mathbf n\mathbf n'}>0$ are deleted and moved to the diagonal as $(K_\theta)_{\mathbf n\mathbf n'}\,g_{\mathbf n'}/g_{\mathbf n}$, the Perron vector $\phi_{\mathrm{FN}}\ge0$ of the resulting stoquastic $H_{\mathrm{eff}}$ is fed back as the guide $g$ (start $g=1$, 10 iterations, floor $10^{-3}\max g$), and the lowest iterate is kept. Since $s_\theta\phi_{\mathrm{FN}}$ has sign $s_\theta$ and $\langle H_{\mathrm{eff}}-H\rangle\ge0$ on it (AM–GM on each deleted pair), $E_{\mathrm{FN}}\ge\langle H\rangle_{\mathrm{FN}}\ge E_{\mathrm{sign}}$. The reported fractions are
$$f_E^{\mathrm{up}}=\frac{E_{\mathrm{sign}}^{\mathrm{up}}-E_0}{E_\theta-E_0},\qquad f_E^{\mathrm{lo}}=\frac{\max(0,E_{\mathrm{stoq}}-E_0)}{E_\theta-E_0},\qquad f_E^{\mathrm{FN}}=\frac{E_{\mathrm{FN}}-E_0}{E_\theta-E_0},$$
together with the multi-start spread $\sigma=\big(\max_k E^{\mathrm{L\text{-}BFGS}}_{k}-\min_k E^{\mathrm{L\text{-}BFGS}}_{k}\big)/\Delta E_{\mathrm{tot}}$ over the starts that report convergence (all four if fewer than two converged). $M=f_E^{\mathrm{up}}$. The calibration error $\epsilon_{\mathrm{cal}}=E^{\mathrm{L\text{-}BFGS}}_{\text{uniform}}(s_0)-E_0$ is computed once per instance. Reported alongside (not decisive): $f_E^{\mathrm{lo}}$, $f_E^{\mathrm{FN}}$, $f_C$, $w_{\mathrm{sign}}$, swap energies, and $\Delta E_{\mathrm{sign}}$, $\Delta E_{\mathrm{amp}}$ for every width. Hard-Core check (reporting only, no effect on the verdict): the lowest three levels of the fixed-$(N_\uparrow,N_\downarrow)$ sector, $\Delta_1/V=(E_1-E_0)/V$ in units of $t$, and the long-range $d$-wave pair correlation $P_d=\operatorname{mean}_{b:\,d(b,b_0)\ge r_{\min}}\eta_bP(b_0,b)$ ($\eta_b=+1$ on $x$-bonds, $-1$ on $y$-bonds) of the $E_0$ and $E_1$ eigenvectors; the run prints "near-degenerate competitor present" if $\Delta_1/V<0.02\,t$.

### 5. Local Autopsy Log
* **Route 2 (hybrid swap / Shapley budget)**: killed at Gate 0. It is existing spin-model practice, and it is not adopted as a budget because $\lvert\psi_\theta\rvert$ is co-adapted to $s_\theta$, which makes the swap "amplitude part" possibly negative and the split order-dependent; the route had no fix for that. Its two swap energies are kept as cross-check outputs of the Route 1 test.
* **Route 3 (sign-mismatch weight)**: killed at Gate 1. $w_{\mathrm{sign}}$ saturates exponentially in $V$ for any fixed per-site sign accuracy and carries no energy weight, so it cannot give a fraction of $\Delta E_{\mathrm{tot}}$ and stops discriminating states at moderate size.
* **Route 4 (lattice FN-GFMC with NQS guide)**: killed at Gate 0. DMC on NNBF exists (arXiv:2510.26906), and the lattice fixed-node sign-flip potential depends on guiding amplitudes, so $E_{\mathrm{FN}}$ is not a sign-only energy (ten Haaf et al., PRB 51, 13039, 1995); the route did not remove that dependence.
* **Autopsy/Revision (hostile referee, Stage 4 of Route 1)**: four defects were fixed in `micro_test.py`, with no change to instances, states or training.
  1. *Optimizer bias made PASS anti-conservative.* $E_{\mathrm{sign}}$ came from L-BFGS-B alone, which gives only an upper bound, so a local minimum on the boundary of the non-negative orthant over-estimated $f_E$ and could produce a false PASS. Fix: a fourth start from the self-consistent ten Haaf fixed-node state for $s_\theta$; $E_{\mathrm{sign}}^{\mathrm{up}}$ is the minimum over all starts and the fixed-node energies, and $E_{\mathrm{FN}}$ is reported separately; PASS now also requires a multi-start spread $\sigma<0.05$, otherwise the instance is AMBIGUOUS; FAIL uses $f_E^{\mathrm{up}}$ and is therefore conservative.
  2. *Calibration rule promised but not coded.* $\epsilon_{\mathrm{cal}}>10^{-3}\,\Delta E_{\mathrm{tot}}$ now forces AMBIGUOUS, both in the per-instance verdict and when `aggregate` re-derives verdicts from the JSON.
  3. *Width-trend tie-break promised but not coded.* `aggregate` now computes $r_{\mathrm{sign}}$ and $r_{\mathrm{amp}}$ between the narrowest and widest NNBF for each AMBIGUOUS instance, declares the slower-decaying part binding, and prints "RERUN on 4x4 with 5 up / 5 down" when the rates are within a factor 1.5 or the instances disagree.
  4. *Hard-Core competing states.* The run now reports $E_0,E_1,E_2$, $\Delta_1/V$ and the $d$-wave pair correlation of the $E_0$ and $E_1$ eigenvectors, and flags a near-degenerate competitor if $\Delta_1/V<0.02\,t$ (reporting only).
