## Binding new rules for round 3
1. **Symmetry-projected control (mandatory).** Every test that compares variational states on a lattice cluster must include a control arm trained *with* projection onto $\psi_0$'s irrep: space group, plus total spin where your arms conserve $S^z$ only. Training with projection is standard practice (Romero, Nys & Carleo arXiv:2406.09077) and is stronger than post-hoc projection.
   * Reuse the verified projector and projected-SR code in `tests/P1.1P_projected/micro_test.py`. Copy it; do not import across folders. It handles the $4\times3$ torus group (translations × mirrors, $\lvert G\rvert=48$) and exact $\hat{S}^2$ projection.
   * Every PASS must beat the projected control, not only an unprojected one.
   * Also report every arm after post-hoc projection.
2. **No single-seed verdicts.** Use at least 3 seeds per arm, and decide on medians or paired comparisons. State the seed-to-seed spread.
3. **Hard Core coverage must be honest.**
   * The P1.1 instances have **no** same-sector competitor; their $E_1$ is a triplet at another momentum.
   * The $4\times3$, $4+4$, $U=8$ cluster has a same-sector avoided crossing near $t'\approx-0.32$, but its minimum in-sector gap is $0.273t$, about $0.023t$ per site. The critic noted that this is about $20\times$ the Hard-Core scale of $10^{-3}t$ per site.
   * If your leaf claims the competitor half, find and use a smaller in-sector gap. ED sweeps over $(t',U)$, filling, or another ED-feasible geometry are all acceptable, provided both competitors are confirmed in one irrep in code. Otherwise state explicitly "sign half only".
   * Remove every remaining citation of "$\Delta_1/V=0.007t$ same-sector competitor".
4. **Exact sums may not hide sampling pathologies.** If a route needs cross-overlaps, amplitude ratios, or other estimators that are heavy-tailed under sampling, the decisive test must include a sampled-estimator arm (genuine Monte Carlo from $\lvert\psi\rvert^2$ or the route's proposal) that measures that variance.
5. **Code must implement the claimed algorithm.** The cost and accuracy claimed in the markdown must be what the code does: no exact-vector shortcut standing in for a per-amplitude algorithm whose cost you claim. The PASS/FAIL logic stated in the markdown must be the logic in `--aggregate`, including ablations and premise checks that are supposed to gate the verdict.
6. **The Persistence Mandate still holds.**
   * Where the critic killed a route, apply Stage 3.3: Operation A on the exact failed component, or Operation B for a fresh orthogonal route.
   * Renaming a killed route, or reviving it without a new mechanism, is not persistence.
   * Target two distinct survivors.
   * Read the new global anti-patterns:
     * no NQS comparison on a symmetry-broken-start cluster without a projected control;
     * no decision or witness validated only by exact sums when its estimators are heavy-tailed under sampling.
7. **Pipelining.** The P1.1-P test (projected sign budget, Slurm job 407525) is running. Do not wait for it. If its verdict lands while you work, the orchestrator will message you.
